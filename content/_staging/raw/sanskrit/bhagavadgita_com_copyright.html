Understanding how to use and share the sacred teachings responsibly

Last updated: December 2, 2025

Non-Profit Mission

BhagavadGita.com is a non-profit initiative operated by the Ved Vyas Foundation. Our mission is to make the sacred wisdom of the Bhagavad Gita freely accessible to seekers worldwide, transcending barriers of language, geography, and economic circumstance.

We believe that spiritual knowledge should be available to all who seek it. All content on this platform is provided free of charge, and we do not seek to profit from the distribution of these sacred teachings.

Content Usage Guidelines

Public Domain Content

The original Sanskrit verses of the Bhagavad Gita are ancient sacred texts in the public domain. These verses may be freely used, reproduced, and distributed by anyone.

Translations and Commentaries

All translations and commentaries featured on BhagavadGita.com have been used with explicit permission from their respective authors, publishers, and organizations. Copyrights to these works remain with their original creators.

Personal Use

You are welcome to download, read, and use the content from this site for:

  • Personal study and spiritual practice
  • Educational purposes in classrooms and study groups
  • Non-commercial religious or spiritual teaching
  • Academic research and citation

Commercial Use Restrictions

The content may NOT be used for commercial purposes. This means you cannot:

  • Sell or bundle the content with products or services
  • Charge fees for access, including shipping or handling
  • Use the content in commercial applications or websites
  • Reproduce the content in commercial publications

Attribution Requirements

When using content from BhagavadGita.com, please provide appropriate attribution. A simple citation format is:

Source: BhagavadGita.com - The Bhagavad Gita Online
[Translation by Author Name, if applicable]
URL: https://bhagavadgita.com

Proper attribution helps others discover these teachings and supports our mission to spread this wisdom.

API and Data Access

We provide a free API for developers who wish to integrate Bhagavad Gita content into their non-commercial applications. When using our API:

  • Attribution must be clearly displayed
  • Usage must be non-commercial
  • Rate limits must be respected
  • Content integrity must be maintained

For API documentation and access, please visit our developer portal or contact us at [email protected]

Contributor Acknowledgements

We are deeply grateful to the organizations and individuals who have granted us permission to feature their translations and commentaries. For a complete list of contributors and their works, please visit our Acknowledgements page.

To acquire individual copies of any featured works, please contact the respective organizations directly. Contact information is available on our Acknowledgements page.

Supporting Our Mission

As a non-profit initiative, we rely on the generosity of supporters to maintain and improve this platform. If you find value in our work, please consider:

  • Making a donation to support our operations
  • Sharing our website with others
  • Contributing translations or improvements
  • Volunteering your time and expertise

Visit our Donate page to learn more about supporting our mission.

Copyright Infringement or Removal Requests

We respect the intellectual property rights of all content creators. If you believe that content on BhagavadGita.com infringes your copyright, or if you are a rights holder and would like your content removed, please contact us immediately.

Email: [email protected]

Please provide: (1) identification of the copyrighted work, (2) the location of the infringing material on our site, (3) your contact information, and (4) a statement of good faith belief that the use is not authorized.
