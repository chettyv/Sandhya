## Shloka

**Devanagari:** मेधासि देवि विदिताखिलशास्त्रसारा / दुर्गासि दुर्गभवसागरनौरसङ्गा । / श्रीः कैटभारिहृदयैककृताधिवासा / गौरी त्वमेव शशिमौलिकृतप्रतिष्ठा ॥4.11॥

**Meaning:** You are Medhā, O Devi — the intelligence that has grasped the essence of all the scriptures. You are Durgā — the boat, itself unattached, across the hard-to-cross ocean of worldly existence. You are Shri, who has made her one dwelling in the heart of Vishnu, the foe of Kaitabha. And you alone are Gauri, whose place is with Shiva, the moon-crested.

## Word by word

- **medhā asi devi** — you are Medhā, intelligence, O Devi
- **vidita-akhila-śāstra-sārā** — who has grasped the essence of all the scriptures
- **durgā asi** — you are Durgā
- **durga-bhava-sāgara-nauḥ** — the boat across the hard-to-cross ocean of worldly existence
- **asaṅgā** — unattached, untouched by what she crosses
- **śrīḥ** — Shri, Lakshmi
- **kaiṭabha-ari-hṛdaya-eka-kṛta-adhivāsā** — who has made her one dwelling in the heart of Kaitabha's foe (Vishnu)
- **gaurī tvam eva** — you alone are Gauri
- **śaśi-mauli-kṛta-pratiṣṭhā** — whose place is established with the moon-crested one (Shiva)

## Meaning

Four names, and a small theology in each. Medhā is not scripture but the intelligence that has digested it. Durgā is explained as it is spoken: she is the boat across an ocean that is durga — hard to cross — and she is asaṅgā, unattached; a boat does not sink into what it crosses. Then two homes. Shri lives in the heart of Vishnu, named here as Kaitabha's foe; Gauri has her place with Shiva. The hymn refuses to choose a house for her. Vaishnava readers hear Shri as the Lord's inseparable consort; Shaiva readers hear Gauri as Shiva's own śakti; Shakta tradition hears one Devi who is all four.

## Meaning (hi)

चार नाम, और हर एक में एक छोटा-सा दर्शन। मेधा शास्त्र नहीं, वह बुद्धि है जिसने शास्त्र को पचा लिया है। दुर्गा का अर्थ कहते-कहते खुल जाता है: वे उस सागर को पार कराने वाली नौका हैं जो दुर्ग है — दुस्तर — और वे असङ्गा हैं, अनासक्त। फिर दो घर। श्री विष्णु के हृदय में रहती हैं, जिन्हें यहाँ कैटभ का शत्रु कहा गया है; गौरी का स्थान शिव के साथ है। स्तोत्र उनके लिए एक घर चुनने से इनकार करता है। वैष्णव पाठ श्री में भगवान की अभिन्न संगिनी सुनता है; शैव पाठ गौरी में शिव की अपनी शक्ति; शाक्त परम्परा एक ही देवी सुनती है जो चारों हैं।

## Reflection

Which of these four — clear intelligence, a boat across trouble, abundance, steady companionship — do you most need to be carried by this week?
