## Shloka

**Devanagari:** तिलेषु तैलं दधिनीव सर्पिरापः स्रोतःस्वरणीषु चाग्निः । / एवमात्माऽत्मनि गृह्यतेऽसौ सत्येनैनं तपसा योऽनुपश्यति ॥1.15॥

**Meaning:** As oil in sesame seeds, as butter in curds, as water in stream-beds, as fire in the kindling sticks — so is that Self grasped within one's own self, by whoever seeks him with truth and with discipline.

## Word by word

- **tileṣu** — in sesame seeds
- **tailam** — oil
- **dadhini iva** — as in curds
- **sarpiḥ** — butter, ghee
- **āpaḥ** — water
- **srotaḥsu** — in stream-beds
- **araṇīṣu ca agniḥ** — and fire in the kindling sticks
- **evam** — so, in the same way
- **ātmā** — that Self
- **ātmani** — within one's own self
- **gṛhyate asau** — is grasped
- **satyena** — by truth
- **enam** — him
- **tapasā** — by discipline, by ardour
- **yaḥ anupaśyati** — whoever keeps looking for, seeks him

## Meaning

Four analogies from an ordinary kitchen and courtyard, all with the same shape: something completely present in its medium, yet visible nowhere in it until worked. Oil does not float on sesame seeds; it must be pressed out. Butter must be churned from curds, water dug for in the dry stream-bed, fire drilled from the sticks. So with the Self: it is not scarce, and it is not far — it is unextracted. The verse also names the only two tools it trusts, and neither is cleverness: satya, truth-telling down to the bone, and tapas, the slow heat of discipline. Nearly everything the traditions ask of a practitioner is one of these two in some costume. The divine is the oil; a practice is a press.

## Meaning (hi)

रसोई और आँगन से चार उपमाएँ, चारों की बनावट एक: कोई चीज़ अपने माध्यम में पूरी तरह मौजूद है, फिर भी बिना मेहनत कहीं दिखाई नहीं देती। तेल तिलों के ऊपर तैरता नहीं मिलता; उसे पेरना पड़ता है। मक्खन दही से मथना पड़ता है, सूखे स्रोत में पानी खोदना पड़ता है, आग अरणियों से मंथकर निकालनी पड़ती है। आत्मा के साथ भी ऐसा ही है: वह दुर्लभ नहीं, दूर भी नहीं — बस निकाला नहीं गया है। श्लोक अपने भरोसे के केवल दो औज़ार बताता है, और दोनों में से कोई चतुराई नहीं है: सत्य — हड्डी तक उतरती सच्चाई, और तप — अनुशासन की धीमी आँच। परंपराएँ साधक से जो कुछ माँगती हैं, वह प्रायः इन्हीं दोनों का कोई न कोई रूप है। दिव्यता तेल है; साधना कोल्हू।

## Reflection

The oil is already in the seed — what daily pressing are you willing to do?
