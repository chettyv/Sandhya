## Shloka

**Devanagari:** युजे वां ब्रह्म पूर्व्यं नमोभिर्विश्लोक एतु पथ्येव सूरेः । / शृण्वन्तु विश्वे अमृतस्य पुत्रा आ ये धामानि दिव्यानि तस्थुः ॥2.5॥

**IAST:** yuje vāṃ brahma pūrvyaṃ namobhirviśloka etu pathyeva sūreḥ । / śṛṇvantu viśve amṛtasya putrā ā ye dhāmāni divyāni tasthuḥ ॥2.5॥
**Say it:** yoo-JAY vaam BRAH-ma POOR-vyam na-MO-bhir vish-LO-ka AY-too path-YAY-va soo-RAYH / shrin-VAN-too VISH-vay a-mri-TAS-ya poot-RAA aa yay DHAA-maa-ni div-YAA-ni tas-THOOH
**Meaning:** For you I yoke the ancient prayer with reverence; let my verse go forth like the sun along its path. Hear it, all you children of immortality — all who have ascended to the heavenly dwellings.
**Meaning (hi):** तुम्हारे लिए मैं प्रणाम-सहित उस प्राचीन प्रार्थना को जोड़ता हूँ; मेरा श्लोक सूर्य के मार्ग की तरह दूर-दूर तक जाए। सुनो, अमृत के सभी पुत्रो — तुम भी, जो दिव्य धामों तक पहुँच चुके हो।

## Word by word

- **yuje** — I yoke
- **vām** — for you two (a Vedic dual; in the hymn's original setting a pair is addressed, and commentators here widen it to teacher and hearer)
- **brahma pūrvyam** — the ancient prayer, the primal sacred word
- **namobhiḥ** — with acts of homage
- **viślokaḥ** — the resounding verse
- **etu** — let it go forth
- **pathyā iva sūreḥ** — like the path of the sun
- **śṛṇvantu** — let them hear
- **viśve** — all
- **amṛtasya putrāḥ** — children of the immortal, heirs of immortality
- **ā ye tasthuḥ** — who have ascended, who abide
- **dhāmāni divyāni** — in the heavenly dwellings

## Meaning

One line of this verse traveled around the world. In September 1893, addressing the Parliament of Religions in Chicago, Swami Vivekananda quoted śṛṇvantu viśve amṛtasya putrāḥ — "hear, all you children of immortality" — and an old Rig Vedic call (the verse is Rig Veda 10.13.1) became a modern manifesto: human beings addressed not as born sinners but as heirs of the deathless. That is precisely the work the verse does inside the Upanishad too. After four verses of careful yoking, the poet stands up and proclaims — so sure of what is coming that heaven itself is summoned as audience. Whichever school a Hindu follows, the address is the same, and it is the boldest thing said about you all week: you are not small.

## Meaning (hi)

इस श्लोक की एक पंक्ति ने दुनिया का चक्कर लगाया। सितम्बर 1893 में शिकागो की विश्व धर्म संसद में स्वामी विवेकानन्द ने "शृण्वन्तु विश्वे अमृतस्य पुत्राः" — "सुनो, अमृत के सभी पुत्रो" — उद्धृत किया, और एक प्राचीन ऋग्वैदिक पुकार (यह श्लोक ऋग्वेद 10.13.1 है) आधुनिक घोषणापत्र बन गई: मनुष्य जन्मजात पापी नहीं, अमरत्व का उत्तराधिकारी है। उपनिषद के भीतर भी यह श्लोक ठीक यही काम करता है। चार श्लोकों की सधी हुई तैयारी के बाद कवि खड़ा होकर उद्घोष करता है — आने वाली बात पर इतना विश्वास कि श्रोता के रूप में स्वर्ग को ही बुला लिया। कोई भी परंपरा हो, संबोधन एक ही है, और यह आपके बारे में कही गई सबसे साहसी बात है: तुम छोटे नहीं हो।

## Reflection

How would you carry yourself through today if you began it as an heir of immortality rather than a bundle of faults?
