## Shloka

**Devanagari:** तस्य तैरभवद् युद्धमतिप्रबलदण्डिनः । / न्यूनैरपि स तैर्युद्धे कोलाविध्वंसिभिर्जितः ॥1.5॥

**IAST:** tasya tairabhavad yuddhamatiprabaladaṇḍinaḥ । / nyūnairapi sa tairyuddhe kolāvidhvaṃsibhirjitaḥ ॥1.5॥

**Meaning:** He, master of a very strong force, made war on them; yet though they were the lesser power, the Kola-destroyers defeated him in battle.

## Word by word

- **tasya** — his
- **taiḥ abhavat yuddham** — war arose with them
- **ati-prabala-daṇḍinaḥ** — of him who wielded a very powerful force
- **nyūnaiḥ api** — though fewer, though weaker
- **saḥ** — he
- **taiḥ yuddhe** — by them, in battle
- **kolāvidhvaṃsibhiḥ** — by the Kola-destroyers
- **jitaḥ** — was defeated

## Meaning

The stronger side lost. The verse states it as a plain military fact and refuses to explain it: Suratha had the greater army and was beaten by the smaller one. Later, the sage will name the hidden variable — Mahamaya, the power that decides outcomes no calculation can predict — but here the text simply lets the wrongness of the result stand. Anyone who has done everything with sufficient resources and still failed knows this verse from the inside. The defeat is the first crack through which the king will eventually fall out of his old life and toward the Goddess.

## Meaning (hi)

ताक़तवर पक्ष हार गया। श्लोक इसे एक सीधे सैनिक तथ्य की तरह कहता है और कोई सफ़ाई नहीं देता: सुरथ के पास बड़ी सेना थी, और छोटी सेना ने उन्हें हरा दिया। आगे ऋषि इस छिपे हुए कारक का नाम लेंगे — महामाया, वह शक्ति जो उन परिणामों का फ़ैसला करती है जिन्हें कोई गणित नहीं भाँप सकता — पर यहाँ पाठ परिणाम के इस उलटेपन को वैसे ही खड़ा रहने देता है। जिसने पर्याप्त साधनों के साथ सब कुछ किया हो और फिर भी असफल रहा हो, वह इस श्लोक को भीतर से पहचानता है। यही हार वह पहली दरार है जिससे राजा अपने पुराने जीवन से गिरकर देवी की ओर बढ़ेगा।

## Reflection

When did having every advantage still not be enough, and what did that defeat begin in you?
