## Shloka

**Devanagari:** अरा इव रथनाभौ संहता यत्र नाड्यः । / स एषोऽन्तश्चरते बहुधा जायमानः । / ओमित्येवं ध्यायथ आत्मानं स्वस्ति वः / पाराय तमसः परस्तात् ॥2.2.6॥

**Meaning:** Where the channels of the body come together like spokes at the hub of a chariot wheel, there he moves within, appearing in many ways. Meditate on the Self as Om. May it be well with you in crossing to the far shore beyond darkness.

## Word by word

- **arāḥ iva** — like spokes
- **ratha-nābhau** — at the hub of a chariot wheel
- **saṃhatāḥ** — gathered together
- **yatra nāḍyaḥ** — where the subtle channels meet
- **saḥ eṣaḥ** — this very one
- **antaḥ carate** — moves within
- **bahudhā jāyamānaḥ** — being born, appearing, in many ways
- **om iti** — as "Om"
- **evam dhyāyatha** — thus meditate on
- **ātmānam** — the Self
- **svasti vaḥ** — well-being to you
- **pārāya** — for the crossing
- **tamasaḥ parastāt** — to the far shore beyond darkness

## Meaning

The teaching turns anatomical and then tender. The place of meditation is located where the body's subtle channels gather like spokes at a hub — the heart, in the tradition's reading — and the one who moves there is the same one who appears as all your many states. The method is given in three words: om iti dhyayatha, meditate on the Self as Om, sound and self converging at the hub. Then the teacher briefly stops instructing in order to bless: svasti vah — safe passage to the shore beyond darkness. The blessing is part of the method. No one crosses on technique alone; in this tradition every crossing is accompanied.

## Meaning (hi)

उपदेश पहले शरीर-रचना बनता है, फिर कोमल हो जाता है। ध्यान का स्थान वहाँ बताया गया जहाँ सूक्ष्म नाड़ियाँ पहिये की नाभि में अरों की तरह जुटती हैं — परंपरा इसे हृदय पढ़ती है — और वहाँ विचरने वाला वही है जो तुम्हारी अनेक अवस्थाओं में प्रकट होता है। विधि तीन शब्दों में: "ओम् इति ध्यायथ" — आत्मा का ओम् रूप में ध्यान करो; नाद और आत्मा नाभि पर मिलें। फिर गुरु क्षण-भर उपदेश रोककर आशीर्वाद देते हैं: स्वस्ति वः — अंधकार से परे किनारे तक की यात्रा शुभ हो। आशीर्वाद भी विधि का अंग है। केवल तकनीक से कोई पार नहीं जाता; इस परंपरा में हर पार-यात्रा किसी की छाया में होती है।

## Reflection

Who once blessed your journey, and can you travel today as someone accompanied rather than alone?
