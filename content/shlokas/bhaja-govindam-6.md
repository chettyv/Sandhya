## Shloka

**Devanagari:** यावत्पवनो निवसति देहे / तावत्पृच्छति कुशलं गेहे । / गतवति वायौ देहापाये / भार्या बिभ्यति तस्मिन्काये ॥6॥

<!-- review note: No conversion artifacts spotted. भार्या (singular) with बिभ्यति (plural verb) is the received reading in printed editions too — a well-known crux, usually understood as "even the wife fears" — not a transcription slip. -->

**IAST:** yāvatpavano nivasati dehe / tāvatpṛcchati kuśalaṃ gehe । / gatavati vāyau dehāpāye / bhāryā bibhyati tasminkāye ॥6॥
**Say it:** YAA-vat PA-va-no ni-VA-sa-ti DAY-hay / TAA-vat PRICH-cha-ti ku-SHA-lum GAY-hay / GA-ta-va-ti VAA-yow day-HAA-paa-yay / BHAAR-yaa BIBH-ya-ti TAS-min KAA-yay
**Meaning:** As long as breath lives in the body, those at home ask after your welfare. When the breath departs and the body fails, even the wife dreads that same body.
**Meaning (hi):** जब तक देह में प्राण बसते हैं, तब तक घर में सब कुशल पूछते हैं। प्राण-वायु के निकलते ही, देह के ढह जाने पर, उसी काया से पत्नी तक डरती है।

## Word by word

- **yāvat** — as long as
- **pavanaḥ** — the breath, the vital air
- **nivasati** — dwells
- **dehe** — in the body
- **tāvat** — so long
- **pṛcchati** — (one) asks
- **kuśalam** — well-being
- **gehe** — in the house
- **gatavati vāyau** — when the breath has gone
- **deha-apāye** — at the body's failing
- **bhāryā** — the wife
- **bibhyati** — fears (the received text carries the plural form; understood as "even the wife fears")
- **tasmin kāye** — of that same body

## Meaning

The verse hangs everything on one observation about the moment of death: the body does not change, yet everything about how it is treated changes. While breath moves in it, it is "you", greeted and asked after; the instant breath leaves, the same body becomes something even the person who shared its bed cannot bear to be alone with. The point is not to shame the wife — her dread is human and universal. The point is what the fear reveals: what was loved was never merely the body, and what you are was never merely the body either. The verse asks you to locate yourself accordingly, before the demonstration.

## Meaning (hi)

श्लोक सब कुछ मृत्यु-क्षण के एक अवलोकन पर टिकाता है: देह वही की वही रहती है, पर उसके साथ बर्ताव सब बदल जाता है। जब तक उसमें साँस चलती है, वह "तुम" हो — अभिवादन और कुशल-क्षेम के पात्र; साँस निकलते ही वही काया ऐसी हो जाती है जिसके पास बिस्तर बाँटने वाली भी अकेले ठहरने से डरती है। मक़सद पत्नी को लज्जित करना नहीं है — उसका भय मानवीय और सार्वभौमिक है। मक़सद वह है जो यह भय उजागर करता है: जो प्रेम किया गया था वह कभी केवल देह नहीं थी, और तुम भी कभी केवल देह नहीं थे। श्लोक कहता है — यह प्रदर्शन होने से पहले ही स्वयं को सही जगह पहचान लो।

## Reflection

If what your loved ones cherish in you is not finally this body, what is it — and are you tending that?
