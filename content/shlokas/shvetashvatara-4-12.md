## Shloka

**Devanagari:** यो देवानां प्रभवश्चोद्भवश्च विश्वाधिपो रुद्रो महर्षिः । / हिरण्यगर्भं पश्यत जायमानं स नो बुद्ध्या शुभया संयुनक्तु ॥4.12॥

**IAST:** yo devānāṃ prabhavaścodbhavaśca viśvādhipo rudro maharṣiḥ । / hiraṇyagarbhaṃ paśyata jāyamānaṃ sa no buddhyā śubhayā saṃyunaktu ॥4.12॥
**Say it:** yo day-VAA-naam pra-bha-VUSH-chod-bha-VUSH-cha vish-VAA-dhi-po RUD-ro ma-har-SHIH / hi-run-ya-GAR-bhum PASH-ya-ta jaa-ya-MAA-num sa no BUDDH-yaa shu-bha-YAA sum-yu-NAK-tu
**Meaning:** He who is the source and the unfolding of the gods, the ruler of all, Rudra, the great seer, who beheld the golden germ as it was being born — may he join us with a clear understanding.

## Word by word

- **yaḥ** — he who
- **devānām** — of the gods
- **prabhavaḥ ca udbhavaḥ ca** — the source and the unfolding
- **viśva-adhipaḥ** — ruler of all
- **rudraḥ** — Rudra
- **maharṣiḥ** — the great seer
- **hiraṇyagarbham** — the golden germ, first-born of creation
- **paśyata** — beheld (Vedic form)
- **jāyamānam** — as it was being born
- **saḥ naḥ** — may he, us
- **buddhyā śubhayā** — with a clear, auspicious understanding
- **saṃyunaktu** — join, yoke

## Meaning

The prayer of 4.1 returns, now with a name in it. The refrain — may he yoke us to a clear buddhi — is identical, but the colorless One is now called Rudra: lord of all, the great seer, older than the gods he brings forth, present with open eyes when Hiranyagarbha, the golden seed of the universe, was born. For Shaiva traditions this verse, with its twin at 3.4, is foundational: the supreme bears the name Rudra-Shiva. Advaita reads Rudra here as the one Brahman wearing its ruling face; Vaishnava Vedanta reads the name as an office the supreme Lord fills. What no school disputes is the object of the prayer: not power — a good and shining mind.

## Meaning (hi)

4.1 की प्रार्थना लौटती है, अब एक नाम के साथ। टेक वही है — वह हमें शुभ बुद्धि से जोड़े — पर अवर्ण एक अब 'रुद्र' कहलाता है: सबका अधिपति, महर्षि, उन देवताओं से पुराना जिन्हें वह प्रकट करता है, और हिरण्यगर्भ — विश्व के सुनहरे बीज — के जन्म का खुली आँखों साक्षी। शैव परंपराओं के लिए यह श्लोक, और 3.4 में इसका जुड़वाँ, आधारभूत है: परम तत्व का नाम रुद्र-शिव है। अद्वैत यहाँ रुद्र को शासक-मुख ओढ़े एक ब्रह्म पढ़ता है; वैष्णव वेदांत इस नाम को परम प्रभु का एक पद। पर प्रार्थना की माँग पर कोई विवाद नहीं: शक्ति नहीं — एक अच्छा, उजला मन।
