## Shloka

**Devanagari:** ददाने दीनेभ्यः श्रियमनिशमाशानुसदृशी / ममन्दं सौन्दर्यप्रकरमकरन्दम् विकिरति । / तवास्मिन् मन्दारस्तबकसुभगे यातु चरणे / निमज्जन्मज्जीवः करणचरणः षट्चरणताम् ॥90॥

**IAST:** dadāne dīnebhyaḥ śriyamaniśamāśānusadṛśī / mamandaṃ saundaryaprakaramakarandam vikirati । / tavāsmin mandārastabakasubhage yātu caraṇe / nimajjanmajjīvaḥ karaṇacaraṇaḥ ṣaṭcaraṇatām ॥90॥
**Say it:** da-DAA-nay dee-NAYB-hyah shri-ya-ma-ni-sha-MAA-shaa-noo-sad-ri-sheem / a-MUN-dum saun-DUR-ya-pra-ka-ra-ma-ka-RUN-dum vi-ki-ra-ti / ta-VAAS-min mun-DAA-ra-sta-ba-ka-soo-bha-GAY YAA-too cha-ra-nay / ni-muj-jun-muj-JEE-vah ka-ra-na-cha-ra-nah shat-cha-ra-na-TAAM
**Meaning:** Into this foot of yours — lovely as a spray of mandara blossoms, endlessly granting the destitute wealth to match their hope, scattering unstintingly the honey of its massed beauty — may my life plunge, and, making the senses its feet, become the six-footed bee.
**Meaning (hi):** तुम्हारे इस चरण में — जो मन्दार के गुच्छे-सा सुहावना है, दीनों को उनकी आशा के अनुरूप सम्पदा अनवरत देता है, और अपने सौन्दर्य-राशि का मकरन्द जी खोलकर बिखेरता है — मेरा जीवन डूब जाए, और इन्द्रियों को अपने पाँव बनाकर छह पाँवों वाला भ्रमर बन जाए।
**Source:** Soundarya Lahari 90 (traditionally attributed to Adi Shankaracharya), sanskritdocuments.org ITRANS file, personal-study terms, https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_devii/soundaryalahari.itx

## Word by word

- **dadāne** — (into that foot) which gives
- **dīnebhyaḥ** — to the destitute
- **śriyam** — wealth
- **aniśam** — unceasingly
- **āśā-anusadṛśīm** — matching their hope
- **amandam** — abundantly, not sparingly
- **saundarya-prakara-makarandam** — the nectar of its massed beauty
- **vikirati** — (which) scatters
- **tava asmin caraṇe** — into this foot of yours
- **mandāra-stabaka-subhage** — lovely as a cluster of mandāra blossoms
- **yātu** — may it go, may it attain
- **nimajjan** — plunging in
- **maj-jīvaḥ** — my life
- **karaṇa-caraṇaḥ** — with the senses for feet
- **ṣaṭ-caraṇatām** — the state of the six-footed one, a bee

## Meaning

Shatcharana, "the six-footed one," is a classical name for the bee, and the pun carries the whole prayer. The Devi's foot is a flower-cluster dripping the nectar of beauty; the devotee asks that his very life dive into it and become the bee — with the five senses and the mind as its six feet. This is bhakti's answer to the scattered mind: not to amputate the senses but to give all of them one flower. Where the foot gives the poor "wealth to match their hope," the poet's own hope is stated by the metaphor — not wealth, but total absorption in the source of it.

## Meaning (hi)

'षट्चरण' — छह पाँवों वाला — भ्रमर का शास्त्रीय नाम है, और यही श्लेष पूरी प्रार्थना को उठाए है। देवी का चरण सौन्दर्य का मकरन्द टपकाता पुष्प-गुच्छ है; भक्त माँगता है कि उसका जीवन ही उसमें गोता लगाकर भ्रमर बन जाए — पाँच इन्द्रियाँ और मन जिसके छह पाँव हों। बिखरे मन के लिए भक्ति का यही उत्तर है: इन्द्रियों को काट देना नहीं, बल्कि उन सबको एक ही फूल सौंप देना। जो चरण दीनों को "आशा के अनुरूप" धन देता है, उसके आगे कवि की अपनी आशा रूपक से ही कह दी गई है — धन नहीं, धन के स्रोत में सम्पूर्ण तल्लीनता।

## Reflection

If all five of your senses and your mind had to settle on a single flower today, what would deserve them?
