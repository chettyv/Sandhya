## Shloka

**Devanagari:** समं देवि स्कन्दद्विपवदनपीतं स्तनयुगं / तवेदं नः खेदं हरतु सततं प्रस्नुतमुखम् । / यदालोक्याशङ्काकुलितहृदयो हासजनकः / स्वकुम्भौ हेरम्बः परिमृशति हस्तेन झडिति ॥72॥

<!-- review note: Suspected variant, not clearly an artifact: झडिति (jhaḍiti) is the source's spelling; many printed editions read झटिति (jhaṭiti), the commoner form of the adverb "at once". Left as the source has it. -->

**IAST:** samaṃ devi skandadvipavadanapītaṃ stanayugaṃ / tavedaṃ naḥ khedaṃ haratu satataṃ prasnutamukham । / yadālokyāśaṅkākulitahṛdayo hāsajanakaḥ / svakumbhau herambaḥ parimṛśati hastena jhaḍiti ॥72॥
**Say it:** SA-mum DAY-vi skan-da-dvi-pa-va-da-na-PEE-tum sta-na-yu-gum / ta-VAY-dum nah KHAY-dum HA-ra-tu sa-ta-tum pras-nu-ta-mu-kham / ya-daa-lok-yaa-shang-kaa-ku-li-ta-HRI-da-yo haa-sa-ja-na-kah / sva-KUM-bhow hay-RAM-bah pa-ri-mri-sha-ti HAS-tay-na JHA-di-ti
**Meaning:** O Devi, may this breast-pair of yours, from which Skanda and the elephant-faced one drank alike, its tips ever streaming with milk, take away our sorrow — beholding it, little Heramba, his heart flustered with doubt, pats his own frontal globes in haste, and laughter breaks out.
**Meaning (hi):** हे देवी, आपका यह स्तन-युग्म, जिससे स्कन्द और गजमुख (गणेश) ने एक समान दूध पिया और जिसके मुख से सदा दूध झरता है, हमारा दुःख हर ले — जिसे देखकर नन्हे हेरम्ब का हृदय सन्देह से घबरा उठता है और वे झट अपने ही मस्तक के दोनों कुम्भ टटोलने लगते हैं, और हँसी छूट जाती है।

## Word by word

- **samam** — alike, equally
- **devi** — O Devi
- **skanda-dvipa-vadana-pītam** — drunk from by Skanda and the elephant-faced (Ganesha)
- **stana-yugam tava idam** — this breast-pair of yours
- **naḥ khedam haratu** — may it carry away our sorrow
- **satatam prasnuta-mukham** — its tips ever streaming with milk
- **yad ālokya** — beholding which
- **āśaṅkā-ākulita-hṛdayaḥ** — his heart flustered with doubt
- **hāsa-janakaḥ** — provoking laughter
- **sva-kumbhau** — his own two frontal globes
- **herambaḥ** — Heramba (Ganesha)
- **parimṛśati** — feels all over, pats
- **hastena** — with his hand
- **jhaḍiti** — instantly

## Meaning

For one verse the cosmic poem becomes pure family comedy. The Devi's breasts are described only through what they gave: the same milk nursed Skanda the warrior and Ganesha the elephant-headed child — no favourites at this table. Then the joke: baby Ganesha glances at them, and his elephant logic panics — those round shapes! have my own frontal globes come off? — and he pats his forehead to check, while the household bursts out laughing. The verse's actual petition is tucked into one clause: may that nourishment carry away our sorrow. Grief is answered here not with doctrine but with the memory of being fed and the sound of a family laughing.

## Meaning (hi)

एक श्लोक के लिए यह ब्रह्माण्ड-काव्य विशुद्ध पारिवारिक विनोद बन जाता है। देवी के स्तनों का वर्णन बस उनके दिए से है: एक ही दूध ने योद्धा स्कन्द और गजमुख बालक गणेश को पाला — इस थाली में कोई भेद नहीं। फिर चुटकुला: नन्हे गणेश उन्हें देखते हैं और उनका गज-तर्क घबरा उठता है — ये गोल आकार! कहीं मेरे ही मस्तक के कुम्भ तो नहीं उतर गए? — और वे झट अपना माथा टटोलते हैं; पूरे परिवार की हँसी छूट जाती है। श्लोक की असली प्रार्थना एक उपवाक्य में दुबकी है: वही पोषण हमारा दुःख हर ले। यहाँ शोक का उत्तर सिद्धान्त नहीं — दूध की स्मृति और परिवार की हँसी है।

## Reflection

When sorrow visits you, which memory of being cared for still has the power to lighten it?
