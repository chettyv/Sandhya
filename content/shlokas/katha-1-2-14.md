## Shloka

**Devanagari:** अन्यत्र धर्मादन्यत्राधर्मा- / दन्यत्रास्मात्कृताकृतात् । / अन्यत्र भूताच्च भव्याच्च / यत्तत्पश्यसि तद्वद ॥1.2.14॥

**IAST:** anyatra dharmādanyatrādharmā- / danyatrāsmātkṛtākṛtāt । / anyatra bhūtācca bhavyācca / yattatpaśyasi tadvada ॥1.2.14॥
**Say it:** an-YUT-ra dhar-MAAD an-YUT-raa-dhar-MAAD / an-YUT-raas-MAAT kri-TAA-kri-TAAT / an-YUT-ra bhoo-TAACH-cha bhav-YAACH-cha / yat tat PASH-ya-si tad VA-da
**Meaning:** "Other than dharma, other than adharma, other than this whole realm of the done and the not-done, other than what has been and what is to come — what you see as That, tell me."

## Word by word

- **anyatra dharmāt** — other than dharma, the right
- **anyatra adharmāt** — other than its opposite
- **anyatra asmāt kṛta-akṛtāt** — other than this done-and-not-done (the whole realm of cause and effect)
- **anyatra bhūtāt ca** — other than what has been
- **bhavyāt ca** — and what is to come
- **yat tat paśyasi** — what you see as That
- **tat vada** — tell me that

## Meaning

The hinge question of the whole Upanishad, and it is the student who asks it. Nachiketa does not ask for morality, ritual technique, or a preview of the future; he brackets all of them — including religion's own proudest categories of right and wrong — and asks what stands outside every pair. This is not a rejection of dharma. It is a question about dharma's source: what is the ground on which right and wrong themselves are staged? The sharpest word is _paśyasi_ — "what you _see_." He asks for testimony, not theory; a report from someone looking, not a definition. Yama's answer begins in the next verse, and it is one syllable long.

## Meaning (hi)

पूरे उपनिषद का धुरी-प्रश्न, और उसे पूछता है विद्यार्थी। नचिकेता नैतिकता नहीं माँगता, कर्मकांड की विधि नहीं, भविष्य की झलक नहीं; वह इन सबको — धर्म-अधर्म जैसी धर्म की अपनी सबसे प्रतिष्ठित श्रेणियों समेत — एक ओर रखकर पूछता है कि हर जोड़े के बाहर क्या खड़ा है। यह धर्म का नकार नहीं है। यह धर्म के स्रोत का प्रश्न है: वह ज़मीन कौन-सी है जिस पर सही और ग़लत का मंच सजता है? सबसे पैना शब्द है 'पश्यसि' — "जो तुम्हें दिखता है।" वह सिद्धांत नहीं, गवाही माँगता है; परिभाषा नहीं, देखने वाले की रिपोर्ट। यम का उत्तर अगले श्लोक में शुरू होता है, और वह एक अक्षर का है।

## Reflection

If your deepest question tonight could not be about right and wrong, past or future, what would you ask?
