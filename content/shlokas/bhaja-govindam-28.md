## Shloka

**Devanagari:** सुखतः क्रियते रामाभोगः / पश्चाद्धन्त शरीरे रोगः । / यद्यपि लोके मरणं शरणं / तदपि न मुञ्चति पापाचरणम् ॥28॥

<!-- review note: No conversion artifacts spotted; the line reads cleanly against printed editions. रामा (rāmā, long final ā) is "woman", not the name राम (Rāma) — worth a reviewer glance since the two differ by one vowel. -->

**IAST:** sukhataḥ kriyate rāmābhogaḥ / paścāddhanta śarīre rogaḥ । / yadyapi loke maraṇaṃ śaraṇaṃ / tadapi na muñcati pāpācaraṇam ॥28॥
**Say it:** su-kha-TAH kri-YA-tay raa-MAA-bho-gah / pash-CHAAD HAN-ta sha-REE-ray RO-gah / YAD-ya-pi LO-kay MA-ra-num SHA-ra-num / TA-da-pi na MOON-cha-ti paa-PAA-cha-ra-num
**Meaning:** For pleasure one takes one's enjoyment of a woman; afterwards — alas — disease in the body. And though in this world death is the final refuge of all, still one does not give up the ways of sin.
**Meaning (hi):** सुख मानकर देह-भोग किया जाता है; पीछे — हाय — शरीर में रोग। यद्यपि इस लोक में अन्ततः मरण ही सबकी शरण है, फिर भी मनुष्य पाप का आचरण नहीं छोड़ता।
**Source:** Bhaja Govindam 28 (attributed to Adi Shankaracharya and his disciples), sanskritdocuments.org ITRANS file, personal-study terms, https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_vishhnu/bhajagovindam.itx

## Word by word

- **sukhataḥ** — for pleasure
- **kriyate** — is done, indulged in
- **rāmā-bhogaḥ** — the enjoyment of a woman
- **paścāt** — afterwards
- **hanta** — alas
- **śarīre** — in the body
- **rogaḥ** — disease
- **yadyapi** — although
- **loke** — in this world
- **maraṇam** — death
- **śaraṇam** — the (final) refuge
- **tadapi** — even so
- **na muñcati** — one does not abandon
- **pāpa-ācaraṇam** — sinful conduct

## Meaning

The stanza is an accounting of sequence: pleasure first, the bill afterwards — hanta, alas, sighs the verse at the predictability of it. Its real subject is not sensuality but the strange arithmetic of habit: a person can know the cost, watch the cost arrive, concede that death closes every account in the end, and still not stop. Knowledge of consequences, the verse observes, has never by itself been strong enough to change conduct — which is why the poem keeps prescribing stronger medicine than information: company, inquiry, remembrance. Read it without self-exemption; the target is whichever indulgence you continue while fully briefed on its price.

## Meaning (hi)

यह छन्द क्रम का हिसाब है: पहले सुख, पीछे बिल — "हन्त" कहकर श्लोक इस पूर्वानुमेयता पर आह भरता है। इसका असल विषय विषय-भोग नहीं, आदत का विचित्र गणित है: मनुष्य क़ीमत जानता है, क़ीमत को आते देखता है, मान भी लेता है कि अन्त में मृत्यु हर खाता बन्द कर देगी — और फिर भी रुकता नहीं। श्लोक का अवलोकन यह है कि परिणामों की जानकारी अपने बल पर आचरण कभी नहीं बदल पाई — इसीलिए कविता सूचना से कड़ी औषधियाँ लिखती रही है: संगति, जिज्ञासा, स्मरण। इसे स्वयं को छूट दिए बिना पढ़ो; निशाने पर वही लत है जिसे तुम उसकी पूरी क़ीमत जानते हुए जारी रखे हो।
