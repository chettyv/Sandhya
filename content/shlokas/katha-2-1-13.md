## Shloka

**Devanagari:** अङ्गुष्ठमात्रः पुरुषो ज्योतिरिवाधूमकः । / ईशानो भूतभव्यस्य स एवाद्य स उ श्वः । / एतद्वै तत् ॥2.1.13॥
**IAST:** aṅguṣṭhamātraḥ puruṣo jyotirivādhūmakaḥ । / īśāno bhūtabhavyasya sa evādya sa u śvaḥ । / etadvai tat ॥2.1.13॥
**Say it:** an-goosh-tha-MAA-trah poo-roo-SHO JYO-tir i-vaa-DHOO-ma-kah / ee-SHAA-no bhoo-ta-BHAV-yas-ya sa ay-VAAD-ya sa oo SHVAH / ay-TAD-vye tat
**Meaning:** The thumb-sized person is like a flame without smoke — lord of what was and what will be, he alone is today, and he tomorrow too. This, truly, is That.
**Meaning (hi):** अँगूठे भर का पुरुष धुएँ-रहित लौ जैसा है — भूत और भविष्य का स्वामी; वही आज है, और वही कल भी। यही तो वह है।

## Word by word

- **aṅguṣṭha-mātraḥ** — of the measure of a thumb
- **puruṣaḥ** — the person, the indwelling self
- **jyotiḥ iva** — like a light, a flame
- **adhūmakaḥ** — without smoke
- **īśānaḥ** — the lord
- **bhūta-bhavyasya** — of what has been and what will be
- **saḥ eva adya** — he alone today
- **saḥ u śvaḥ** — and he, too, tomorrow
- **etat vai tat** — this, truly, is That

## Meaning

The thumb-sized dweller again, now with two new touches. First, a flame without smoke: light that no longer flickers or smudges, burning clean and steady — the classical image of awareness unclouded. Second, an answer to time: he alone is today, and he tomorrow. Everything else in your life will be different tomorrow — the body a day older, the plans revised, the moods replaced — but the one who watches today and the one who will watch tomorrow are not two watchers. The lord of what was and what will be is not located somewhere in the past or the future; it is what stays present while past and future stream by. This, truly, is That.

## Meaning (hi)

फिर वही अँगूठे-भर का वासी, अब दो नए स्पर्शों के साथ। पहला: बिना धुएँ की लौ — ऐसा प्रकाश जो न काँपता है, न धुँधलाता है; साफ़ और स्थिर जलता है — निर्मल चेतना की शास्त्रीय उपमा। दूसरा: समय को उत्तर — वही आज है, और वही कल। आपके जीवन में बाकी सब कल बदल चुका होगा — देह एक दिन और पुरानी, योजनाएँ संशोधित, मनोदशाएँ बदली हुईं — पर जो आज देख रहा है और जो कल देखेगा, वे दो देखने वाले नहीं हैं। भूत और भविष्य का स्वामी कहीं भूत या भविष्य में नहीं बैठा; वह वही है जो वर्तमान बना रहता है, जब भूत और भविष्य बहते चले जाते हैं। यही तो वह है।

## Reflection

What in you will be exactly the same tomorrow as it is today, and can you rest there now?
