## Shloka

**Devanagari:** नैव स्त्री न पुमानेष न चैवायं नपुंसकः । / यद्यच्छरीरमादत्ते तेने तेने स युज्यते ॥5.10॥

<!-- review note: तेने तेने is carried by printed editions of this Upanishad; some editions regularize to तेन तेन. The sense either way is "with each body taken, with that it is joined". Not a scan artifact; left unaltered per policy. -->

**IAST:** naiva strī na pumāneṣa na caivāyaṃ napuṃsakaḥ । / yadyaccharīramādatte tene tene sa yujyate ॥5.10॥
**Say it:** NAI-va stree na poo-MAA-nay-sha na CHAI-vaa-yam na-POOM-sa-kah / yad-yach-cha-REE-ram aa-DAT-tay TAY-nay TAY-nay sa YOOJ-ya-tay
**Meaning:** This self is not female, not male, and not neuter either; whatever body it takes up, with that body it is joined.
**Meaning (hi):** यह आत्मा न स्त्री है, न पुरुष, न ही नपुंसक; जो-जो शरीर वह धारण करती है, उसी-उसी से जुड़ जाती है।

## Word by word

- **na eva strī** — not female
- **na pumān** — not male
- **eṣaḥ** — this one (the self)
- **na ca eva ayam** — nor is it
- **napuṃsakaḥ** — neuter
- **yat yat śarīram** — whatever body
- **ādatte** — it takes up
- **tene tene** — with each of those (received reading; = tena tena)
- **saḥ** — it
- **yujyate** — is joined

## Meaning

In the middle of the rebirth sequence the Upanishad pauses to strip a label off. The self that wanders from body to body is not female, not male, not neuter — gender belongs to the garment, not to the wearer. The teaching is stated flatly, as doctrine, and every major Vedantic school receives it the same way at this altitude: bodies are taken up and set down, and the taker is unmarked by their categories. The verse does not dissolve the body's dignity — it locates identity deeper than the body's forms. Whatever you have been told you are by the shape you wear, the text teaches that the one who wears it has always been something prior.

## Meaning (hi)

पुनर्जन्म के क्रम के बीच उपनिषद ठहरकर एक लेबल उतार देता है। जो आत्मा देह-देह भटकती है, वह न स्त्री है, न पुरुष, न नपुंसक — लिंग पोशाक का धर्म है, पहनने वाले का नहीं। शिक्षा सीधे सिद्धांत की तरह कही गई है, और इस ऊँचाई पर वेदांत की सभी प्रमुख परंपराएँ इसे एक ही तरह ग्रहण करती हैं: शरीर उठाए और उतारे जाते हैं, और उठाने वाला उनकी श्रेणियों से अछूता रहता है। श्लोक देह की गरिमा नहीं घटाता — वह पहचान को देह के रूपों से गहरा ठहराता है। आपके पहने हुए आकार ने आपको जो भी बताया हो, पाठ कहता है: पहनने वाला हमेशा उससे पहले का कुछ है।

## Reflection

Which labels attached to your body have you mistaken for the one who wears it?
