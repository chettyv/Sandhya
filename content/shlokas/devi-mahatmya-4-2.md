## Shloka

**Devanagari:** शक्रादयः सुरगणा निहतेऽतिवीर्ये / तस्मिन् दुरात्मनि सुरारिबले च देव्या । / तां तुष्टुवुः प्रणतिनम्रशिरोधरांसा / वाग्भिः प्रहर्षपुलकोद्गमचारुदेहाः ॥4.2॥

**Say it:** shak-RAA-da-yah soo-ra-ga-NAA NI-ha-tay a-ti-VEER-yay / TAS-min doo-RAAT-ma-ni soo-RAA-ri-ba-lay cha DAYV-yaa / taam toosh-too-VOOH pra-na-ti-NAM-ra-shi-RO-dha-RAAM-saa / VAAG-bhih pra-HAR-sha-poo-la-KOD-ga-ma-CHAA-roo-DAY-haah
**Meaning:** When that mighty, evil-hearted one and the army of the gods' enemies had been slain by the Devi, Indra and the hosts of gods praised her in words — their necks and shoulders bowed low in reverence, their bodies beautiful with the thrill of joy rising on their skin.

## Word by word

- **śakra-ādayaḥ sura-gaṇāḥ** — Indra and the other hosts of gods
- **nihate** — when (he) had been slain
- **ati-vīrye** — that exceedingly mighty one
- **tasmin durātmani** — that evil-hearted one
- **sura-ari-bale ca** — and the army of the gods' enemies
- **devyā** — by the Devi
- **tām tuṣṭuvuḥ** — praised her
- **praṇati-namra-śirodhara-aṃsāḥ** — their necks and shoulders bowed in reverence
- **vāgbhiḥ** — with words
- **praharṣa-pulaka-udgama-cāru-dehāḥ** — their bodies lovely with the thrill of joy rising on the skin

## Meaning

Before the gods say a word, the text shows their bodies: necks and shoulders bent, skin prickling with pulaka, the goosebumps Sanskrit poetry treats as the truest sign of strong feeling. Only then come the words. The order is deliberate: in Indian devotional writing, bhakti is first a bodily event and only afterwards a spoken one. The verse also settles the count of the dead without flourish — the mighty asura and his army "slain by the Devi" — in one clause before turning to praise. The metre has changed to the long, rolling vasantatilakā that will carry the hymn. A description of the gods, not their words, so it stays out of the daily pool.

## Meaning (hi)

देवता एक भी शब्द कहें, उससे पहले पाठ उनकी देह दिखाता है: गर्दन और कन्धे झुके हुए, त्वचा पर पुलक — वह रोमांच जिसे काव्य गहरे भाव का सच्चा चिह्न मानता है। शब्द उसके बाद आते हैं। यह क्रम जानबूझकर है — भारतीय भक्ति-साहित्य में भक्ति पहले देह की घटना है, बोली की बाद में। श्लोक मृतकों का हिसाब भी बिना अलंकार के एक ही उपवाक्य में निपटा देता है — महाबली असुर और उसकी सेना, "देवी के हाथों मारे गए" — और फिर स्तुति की ओर मुड़ता है। छन्द बदलकर लम्बा, लहराता वसन्ततिलका हो गया है जो पूरे स्तोत्र को वहन करेगा। यह देवताओं का वर्णन है, उनके शब्द नहीं, इसलिए दैनिक चयन से बाहर।

## Reflection

When was the last time gratitude showed up in your body before it reached your words?
