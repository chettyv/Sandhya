## Shloka

**Devanagari:** भ्रुवौ भुग्ने किंचिद्भुवनभयभङ्गव्यसनिनि / त्वदीये नेत्राभ्यां मधुकररुचिभ्यां धृतगुणम् । / धनुर्मन्ये सव्येतरकरगृहीतं रतिपतेः / प्रकोष्ठे मुष्टौ च स्थगयति निगूढान्तरमुमे ॥47॥

**Meaning:** O Uma, bent on breaking the fear of the worlds — your pair of brows, slightly arched, I take for the bow of the lord of Rati: its string your two eyes with their black-bee sheen, gripped in his right hand, its middle hidden where his fist and forearm cover it.

## Word by word

- **bhruvau bhugne kiṃcit** — your two brows, slightly bent
- **bhuvana-bhaya-bhaṅga-vyasanini** — O you who are devoted to breaking the fear of the worlds
- **tvadīye** — your
- **netrābhyām madhukara-rucibhyām** — with your two eyes lustrous as black bees
- **dhṛta-guṇam** — strung, fitted with its string
- **dhanuḥ manye** — I take (them) to be the bow
- **savya-itara-kara-gṛhītam** — held in his right hand ("the hand other than the left")
- **rati-pateḥ** — of the lord of Rati (Kama)
- **prakoṣṭhe muṣṭau ca** — with his forearm and fist
- **sthagayati nigūḍha-antaram** — he conceals it, its middle hidden
- **ume** — O Uma

## Meaning

A riddle of anatomy solved by a bow. The Devi's arched brows curve like a bow's two limbs; her long dark eyes beneath them run like the string. But between brow and eye there is a gap — the bridge of the nose — and a real bowstring is unbroken. The poet answers: Kama himself is holding the bow, and his fist and forearm hide the grip where string and stave meet. What lifts the verse beyond cleverness is the vocative it hangs on: bhuvana-bhaya-bhaṅga-vyasanini, "you whose settled habit is breaking the world's fear." The bow of love is strung on a face whose standing occupation is dispelling fear — beauty and refuge in a single glance.

## Meaning (hi)

शरीर-रचना की एक पहेली, जिसका उत्तर धनुष है। देवी की धनुषाकार भौंहें धनुष की दो भुजाओं-सी झुकी हैं; उनके नीचे लम्बे साँवले नेत्र प्रत्यञ्चा-से खिंचे हैं। पर भौंह और आँख के बीच नाक की चढ़ान का अन्तर है — जबकि असली डोरी अखण्ड होती है। कवि उत्तर देता है: धनुष स्वयं कामदेव के हाथ में है, और उसकी मुट्ठी और कलाई ने वही मध्य भाग ढक रखा है जहाँ डोरी और दण्ड मिलते हैं। श्लोक को चतुराई से ऊपर उठाता है उसका सम्बोधन: "भुवनभयभङ्गव्यसनिनि" — जिसे संसार का भय तोड़ने का व्यसन है। प्रेम का धनुष उस मुख पर चढ़ा है जिसका स्थायी काम भय हरना है — एक ही दृष्टि में सौन्दर्य भी, शरण भी।

## Reflection

Whose fear could you make it your settled habit to break?
