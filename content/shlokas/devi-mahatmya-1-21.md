## Shloka

**Devanagari:** किं नु तेषां गृहे क्षेममक्षेमं किं नु साम्प्रतम् । / कथं ते किं नु सद्वृत्ताः दुर्वृत्ताः किं नु मे सुताः ॥1.21॥

**IAST:** kiṃ nu teṣāṃ gṛhe kṣemamakṣemaṃ kiṃ nu sāmpratam । / kathaṃ te kiṃ nu sadvṛttāḥ durvṛttāḥ kiṃ nu me sutāḥ ॥1.21॥
**Say it:** kim noo TAY-shaam GRI-hay KSHAY-mum a-KSHAY-mum kim noo SAAM-pra-tum / KA-thum tay kim noo sad-VRIT-taah dur-VRIT-taah kim noo may soo-TAAH
**Meaning:** "Is all well in their house now, or is there trouble? How are they? Are my sons behaving well — or badly?"
**Meaning (hi):** "क्या अभी उनके घर में कुशल है, या कोई संकट? वे कैसे हैं? मेरे पुत्र सदाचारी हैं या दुराचारी?"

## Word by word

- **kim nu** — is it, I wonder?
- **teṣām gṛhe** — in their house
- **kṣemam** — wellbeing, safety
- **akṣemam** — trouble, lack of safety
- **sāmpratam** — at present
- **katham te** — how are they?
- **sad-vṛttāḥ** — well-conducted
- **dur-vṛttāḥ** — ill-conducted
- **me sutāḥ** — my sons

## Meaning

Five questions in two lines, and every one of them is about the people who threw him out. The verse reproduces the texture of anxious love: it does not reason, it circles. Are they safe? Are they well? Are the boys turning out all right? The last question is the most revealing — a father who was robbed by his sons is still worrying about their character, as if their conduct were still his responsibility to shape. The Devi Mahatmya refuses to call this foolish. It will shortly call it Mahamaya, the force that keeps every creature bound to its own, and it will say that even the wise are not exempt.

## Meaning (hi)

दो पंक्तियों में पाँच प्रश्न, और हर एक उन्हीं लोगों के बारे में जिन्होंने उसे निकाला। श्लोक चिन्तित प्रेम की बुनावट को हू-ब-हू उतारता है: वह तर्क नहीं करता, चक्कर काटता है। क्या वे सुरक्षित हैं? ठीक तो हैं? लड़के ठीक राह पर हैं? आख़िरी प्रश्न सबसे ज़्यादा उजागर करता है — जिस पिता को उसके बेटों ने लूटा, वह अब भी उनके चरित्र की चिन्ता कर रहा है, मानो उनका आचरण गढ़ना अब भी उसकी ज़िम्मेदारी हो। देवी माहात्म्य इसे मूर्खता नहीं कहता। वह जल्द ही इसे महामाया कहेगा — वह शक्ति जो हर प्राणी को उसके अपनों से बाँधे रखती है — और कहेगा कि ज्ञानी भी इससे बरी नहीं।

## Reflection

Whose wellbeing do you still worry about even though they have hurt you, and what does that worry tell you about love?
