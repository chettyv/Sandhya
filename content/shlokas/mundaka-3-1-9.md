**Meaning:** This subtle Self is to be known by awareness — here, where the life-breath has entered fivefold. Every creature's mind is woven through with these breaths; when that mind is purified, this Self shines forth.

## Word by word

- **eṣaḥ** — this
- **aṇuḥ** — subtle, minute
- **ātmā** — the Self
- **cetasā** — by awareness, by the mind
- **veditavyaḥ** — is to be known
- **yasmin** — in which (in this body)
- **prāṇaḥ** — the life-breath
- **pañcadhā** — fivefold
- **saṃviveśa** — has entered, settled
- **prāṇaiḥ** — by the breaths
- **cittam sarvam** — the whole mind
- **otam** — woven through, threaded
- **prajānām** — of living beings
- **yasmin viśuddhe** — when that (mind) is purified
- **vibhavati** — shines forth, comes into its own power
- **eṣaḥ ātmā** — this Self

## Meaning

The Self is called subtle not because it is small but because it slips through every coarse instrument; only awareness itself can know it. And awareness is not free-floating: in every creature, the verse says, the mind is woven through with the five movements of breath — thread and cloth of one fabric. That is why so many Hindu disciplines pair breath practice with clarity of mind; steady one and the other steadies with it. Nothing new has to be added. When the weave of mind and breath settles clean, the Self that was always present shines out on its own. Purification here is not adding shine — it is removing static from a signal that never stopped broadcasting.

## Meaning (hi)

आत्मा को सूक्ष्म इसलिए नहीं कहा गया कि वह छोटा है, बल्कि इसलिए कि वह हर स्थूल औज़ार की पकड़ से छूट जाता है; उसे केवल चेतना ही जान सकती है। और चेतना अकेली नहीं तैरती: हर प्राणी का चित्त प्राण की पाँच गतियों से बुना हुआ है — ताना-बाना एक ही कपड़े का। इसीलिए इतनी सारी हिंदू साधना-पद्धतियाँ श्वास और मन की निर्मलता को साथ-साथ साधती हैं; एक को थिर करो, दूसरा अपने आप थिरता है। कुछ नया जोड़ना नहीं है। जब मन-प्राण का यह ताना-बाना साफ़ होकर ठहर जाता है, तो जो आत्मा सदा से मौजूद था, वह अपने आप चमक उठता है। शुद्धि यहाँ चमक चढ़ाना नहीं — उस सिग्नल से शोर हटाना है जो कभी बंद हुआ ही नहीं था।

## Reflection

If your mind is woven from your breath, what might ten slow breaths do to the weave today?
