## Shloka

**Devanagari:** पदन्यासक्रीडापरिचयमिवारब्धुमनसः / स्खलन्तस्ते खेलं भवनकलहंसा न जहति । / अतस्तेषां शिक्षां सुभगमणिमञ्जीररणित / च्छलादाचक्षाणं चरणकमलं चारुचरिते ॥91॥

**Meaning:** O lady of lovely ways, the royal swans of your household, as if set on learning the art of your playful gait, keep stumbling after it — and still will not give up the game; and so your lotus foot, under cover of the ringing of its lovely jewelled anklets, seems to be calling out their lesson.

## Word by word

- **pada-nyāsa-krīḍā-paricayam** — acquaintance with the play of your footfall
- **iva** — as if
- **ārabdhu-manasaḥ** — minded to take (it) up
- **skhalantaḥ** — stumbling
- **te khelam** — your playful gait
- **bhavana-kalahaṃsāḥ** — the royal swans of the household
- **na jahati** — do not give up
- **ataḥ** — therefore
- **teṣām śikṣām** — their lesson
- **subhaga-maṇi-mañjīra-raṇita-cchalāt** — under the pretext of the ringing of the lovely jewelled anklets
- **ācakṣāṇam** — declaring, calling out
- **caraṇa-kamalam** — the lotus foot
- **cāru-carite** — O lady of lovely conduct

## Meaning

Sanskrit convention praises a graceful woman by saying she walks like a swan; this verse turns the compliment inside out. Here it is the swans who study her — the pet birds of the palace waddle after the Devi, stumbling, refusing to quit, like earnest students who cannot quite get the step. And her foot responds the way a good dance teacher does: the chime of its jewelled anklets becomes the spoken syllables of the lesson, keeping time for the strugglers. Grace, the verse suggests, does not humiliate its imitators; it teaches them — almost without their noticing — through the sheer music of its ordinary movements.

## Meaning (hi)

संस्कृत की रीति सुन्दरी की प्रशंसा में कहती है कि वह हंस-सी चलती है; यह श्लोक उस प्रशंसा को उलट देता है। यहाँ हंस ही देवी से सीख रहे हैं — महल के पालतू राजहंस उनके पीछे डगमगाते चलते हैं, गिरते-सम्भलते हैं, पर हार नहीं मानते — उन उत्साही विद्यार्थियों की तरह जिनसे ताल ठीक नहीं बैठती। और देवी का चरण वैसा ही करता है जैसा कोई अच्छा नृत्य-गुरु: मणि-नूपुरों की झनकार पाठ के बोल बन जाती है, लड़खड़ाते शिष्यों के लिए ताल देती हुई। श्लोक का संकेत है — सच्ची शालीनता अपनी नकल करने वालों को लज्जित नहीं करती; वह अपनी साधारण चाल के संगीत से ही, लगभग अनजाने, उन्हें सिखा देती है।

## Reflection

Who is quietly learning from the way you move through an ordinary day, whether or not you mean to be teaching?
