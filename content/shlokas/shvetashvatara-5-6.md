## Shloka

**Devanagari:** तद्वेदगुह्योपनिषत्सु गूढं तद्ब्रह्मा वेदते ब्रह्मयोनिम् । / ये पूर्वं देवा ऋषयश्च तद्विदुस्ते तन्मया अमृता वै बभूवुः ॥5.6॥

<!-- review note: No transcription artifacts spotted. तद्ब्रह्मा (brahmā, the creator, masculine) is the received reading; commentators gloss ब्रह्मयोनिम् both as "the womb that is Brahman" and as "the source of the Veda". -->

**IAST:** tadvedaguhyopaniṣatsu gūḍhaṃ tadbrahmā vedate brahmayonim । / ye pūrvaṃ devā ṛṣayaśca tadviduste tanmayā amṛtā vai babhūvuḥ ॥5.6॥
**Say it:** tad-VAY-da-goo-hyo-pa-ni-SHAT-soo GOO-dhum tad-BRAH-maa VAY-da-tay brah-ma-YO-nim / yay POOR-vum DAY-vaa RI-sha-yash-cha tad-vi-DOOS-tay tan-ma-YAA a-mri-TAA vai ba-BHOO-vooh
**Meaning:** That is what lies hidden in the Upanishads, the secret core of the Vedas; Brahmā knows it as the womb of Brahman. The gods and seers of old who knew it became of its very substance, and became immortal indeed.
**Meaning (hi):** वही है जो वेदों के गूढ़ मर्म — उपनिषदों — में छिपा है; ब्रह्मा उसे ब्रह्म की योनि के रूप में जानते हैं। पुराने देवता और ऋषि जो उसे जान गए, वे तन्मय हो गए — उसी के बने — और सचमुच अमर हुए।

## Word by word

- **tat** — that
- **veda-guhya-upaniṣatsu** — in the Upanishads, the hidden core of the Vedas
- **gūḍham** — concealed
- **tat brahmā vedate** — that Brahmā knows
- **brahma-yonim** — as the womb of Brahman (or: the source of the Veda)
- **ye pūrvam** — those who, of old
- **devāḥ ṛṣayaḥ ca** — gods and seers
- **tat viduḥ** — knew it
- **te tat-mayāḥ** — they, made of it
- **amṛtāḥ vai** — immortal indeed
- **babhūvuḥ** — became

## Meaning

Where is this God to be found? Not on the surface of the ritual texts but hidden in their secret — the Upanishads, the guhya, the confidential core of the Vedas. Even Brahmā, the creator, knows it not as his possession but as his womb, the source he himself came from. And the verse offers its credential: the gods and seers of old who knew it became tanmaya — made of it, absorbed into its substance — and thereby immortal. Knowing, here, is not information but transformation; you become what you truly know. The line quietly sets the standard for every study session, including this one: the goal of reading is not to hold the secret but to be held by it.

## Meaning (hi)

यह देव मिले कहाँ? कर्मकांड की सतह पर नहीं, बल्कि वेदों के गुह्य — गोपनीय मर्म — उपनिषदों में छिपा हुआ। स्वयं ब्रह्मा भी उसे अपनी संपत्ति की तरह नहीं, अपनी योनि — अपने उद्गम — की तरह जानते हैं। और श्लोक अपना प्रमाण भी देता है: पुराने देवता और ऋषि जो उसे जान गए, वे "तन्मय" हो गए — उसी के बने हुए — और इसीलिए अमर। यहाँ जानना सूचना नहीं, रूपांतरण है; जो सचमुच जाना, वही हो गए। यह पंक्ति हर स्वाध्याय का — आज के इस पाठ का भी — पैमाना चुपचाप तय कर देती है: पढ़ने का लक्ष्य रहस्य को मुट्ठी में रखना नहीं, रहस्य में समा जाना है।
