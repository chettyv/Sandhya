## Shloka

**Devanagari:** स त्वं प्रियान्प्रियरूपांश्च कामान् / अभिध्यायन्नचिकेतोऽत्यस्राक्षीः । / नैतां सृङ्कां वित्तमयीमवाप्तो / यस्यां मज्जन्ति बहवो मनुष्याः ॥1.2.3॥

**IAST:** sa tvaṃ priyānpriyarūpāṃśca kāmān / abhidhyāyannaciketo'tyasrākṣīḥ । / naitāṃ sṛṅkāṃ vittamayīmavāpto / yasyāṃ majjanti bahavo manuṣyāḥ ॥1.2.3॥
**Say it:** sa tvum pri-YAAN pri-ya-ROO-paansh-cha KAA-maan / a-bhi-DHYAA-yan na-chi-KAY-to at-yas-RAAK-sheeh / NAI-taam SRIN-kaam vit-ta-ma-YEE-ma-VAAP-to / YAS-yaam MUJ-jan-ti BA-ha-vo ma-noosh-YAAH
**Meaning:** You, Nachiketas, have looked desires full in the face — the dear things, the delightful shapes — and let them pass. You have not taken up that necklace of wealth in which so many people sink.

## Word by word

- **saḥ tvam** — you indeed
- **priyān** — dear things
- **priya-rūpān ca** — and delightful forms
- **kāmān** — desires
- **abhidhyāyan** — pondering, looking at steadily
- **naciketaḥ** — O Nachiketas
- **atyasrākṣīḥ** — you have let go, passed over
- **na etām** — not this
- **sṛṅkām** — necklace, chain (the word used for Yama's jewelled gift in 1.1.16)
- **vitta-mayīm** — made of wealth
- **avāptaḥ** — you have (not) taken up
- **yasyām** — in which
- **majjanti** — sink
- **bahavaḥ manuṣyāḥ** — many people

## Meaning

Yama is grading the exam he set in the first valli, where he offered Nachiketas sons, cattle, gold, long life, and pleasures no mortal is offered twice. The verb matters: abhidhyāyan — Nachiketas pondered the desires, looked at them steadily, and then let them go. This is not the renunciation of someone too timid to want; it is refusal with open eyes. The sṛṅkā, the necklace, echoes the jewelled chain Yama dangled in 1.1.16 — wealth as ornament, worn until it becomes the weight that pulls you under. The verse is Yama's welcome into the teaching: only someone who has examined the pleasant and set it down is ready to hear about the good.

## Meaning (hi)

यम उसी परीक्षा का नतीजा सुना रहे हैं जो पहली वल्ली में उन्होंने ली थी — नचिकेता के आगे पुत्र, पशु, सोना, लंबी उम्र और ऐसे भोग रखे गए जो किसी मनुष्य को दोबारा नहीं मिलते। यहाँ क्रिया पर ध्यान दीजिए: अभिध्यायन् — नचिकेता ने कामनाओं को ग़ौर से देखा, परखा, और तब छोड़ा। यह उस आदमी का त्याग नहीं जो चाहने से डरता है; यह खुली आँखों का इनकार है। सृङ्का यानी माला, 1.1.16 की उसी रत्नजड़ित माला की याद दिलाती है — धन पहले गहना, फिर वह बोझ जो डुबो देता है। यह श्लोक शिक्षा में यम का स्वागत-वचन है: श्रेय की बात सुनने का अधिकारी वही है जिसने प्रेय को परखकर नीचे रख दिया हो।

## Reflection

Which temptation have you examined closely enough to have earned the right to refuse it?
