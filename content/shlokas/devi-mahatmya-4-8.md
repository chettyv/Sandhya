## Shloka

**Devanagari:** यस्याः समस्तसुरता समुदीरणेन / तृप्तिं प्रयान्ति सकलेषु मखेषु देवि । / स्वाहासि वै पितृगणस्य च तृप्तिहेतुर् / उच्चार्यसे त्वमत एव जनैः स्वधा च ॥4.8॥

**Meaning:** O Devi, you are truly Svāhā — by whose utterance the whole company of gods finds satisfaction in every sacrifice — and you are the cause of the ancestors' contentment too; that is why people pronounce you as Svadhā as well.

## Word by word

- **yasyāḥ samudīraṇena** — by whose utterance
- **samasta-suratā** — the whole company of gods
- **tṛptim prayānti** — attain satisfaction
- **sakaleṣu makheṣu** — in all sacrifices
- **devi** — O Devi
- **svāhā asi vai** — you are indeed Svāhā, the offering-call to the gods
- **pitṛ-gaṇasya ca** — and of the host of ancestors
- **tṛpti-hetuḥ** — the cause of contentment
- **uccāryase** — you are pronounced, you are called
- **tvam ataḥ eva** — you, for this very reason
- **janaiḥ** — by people
- **svadhā ca** — as Svadhā too, the offering-call to the ancestors

## Meaning

Svāhā is the word spoken as an offering goes into the fire for the gods; svadhā is its counterpart when the offering is for the ancestors. Every rite runs on those two syllables. The verse says the Devi is not only the one who receives offerings but the word that carries them — the connection itself, not just its far end. That is why she is "pronounced" as Svāhā and Svadhā: the names are sounds people use at the fire. Obligation to the gods and obligation to the dead are the two lines a Hindu household holds together, and the hymn finds one power at the root of both.

## Meaning (hi)

स्वाहा वह शब्द है जो देवताओं के लिए आहुति अग्नि में डालते समय बोला जाता है; स्वधा उसका जोड़ा है, जब आहुति पितरों के लिए हो। हर अनुष्ठान इन्हीं दो अक्षरों पर चलता है। श्लोक कहता है कि देवी केवल आहुति पाने वाली नहीं, वह शब्द हैं जो आहुति को पहुँचाता है — सम्बन्ध स्वयं, न कि केवल उसका दूसरा सिरा। इसीलिए वे स्वाहा और स्वधा के रूप में "उच्चारित" होती हैं: ये नाम वे ध्वनियाँ हैं जिन्हें लोग अग्नि के सामने बोलते हैं। देवताओं के प्रति दायित्व और दिवंगतों के प्रति दायित्व — ये दो डोरियाँ एक हिन्दू घर साथ थामे रहता है, और स्तोत्र दोनों की जड़ में एक ही शक्ति पाता है।

## Reflection

Who are the ancestors whose contentment you still, in some form, feed?
