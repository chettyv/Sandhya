## Shloka

**Devanagari:** यथाऽऽदर्शे तथाऽऽत्मनि यथा स्वप्ने तथा पितृलोके । / यथाऽप्सु परीव ददृशे तथा गन्धर्वलोके / छायातपयोरिव ब्रह्मलोके ॥2.3.5॥

<!-- review note: the double avagraha in "यथाऽऽदर्शे" and "तथाऽऽत्मनि" marks the sandhi-elided long ā (yathā ādarśe, tathā ātmani) and is genuine, not an artifact. "परीव" is pari + iva ("as if all around", i.e. indistinctly), the standard reading in printed editions. Left unaltered per policy. -->

**IAST:** yathā''darśe tathā''tmani yathā svapne tathā pitṛloke । / yathā'psu parīva dadṛśe tathā gandharvaloke / chāyātapayoriva brahmaloke ॥2.3.5॥
**Say it:** ya-thaa-DAR-shay ta-THAAT-ma-ni ya-thaa SVAP-nay ta-thaa pi-tri-LO-kay / ya-THAAP-su pa-REE-va da-DRI-shay ta-thaa gan-dhar-va-LO-kay / chhaa-yaa-TA-pa-yor-i-va brah-ma-LO-kay
**Meaning:** As in a mirror, so is the Self seen in one's own being; as in a dream, so in the world of the ancestors; as a thing glimpsed wavering in water, so in the world of the gandharvas; as sharply as shadow and sunlight, so in the world of Brahma.
**Meaning (hi):** जैसे दर्पण में, वैसे अपने भीतर आत्मा दिखती है; जैसे स्वप्न में, वैसे पितृलोक में; जैसे पानी में झिलमिलाती छवि, वैसे गन्धर्वलोक में; और जैसे छाया और धूप — उतनी स्पष्ट — वैसे ब्रह्मलोक में।

## Word by word

- **yathā** — as
- **ādarśe** — in a mirror
- **tathā** — so
- **ātmani** — in oneself, in one's own being (read by the commentators as: in a purified intellect)
- **yathā svapne** — as in a dream
- **tathā pitṛloke** — so in the world of the ancestors
- **yathā apsu** — as in water
- **parī iva dadṛśe** — is seen as if blurred, wavering
- **tathā gandharvaloke** — so in the world of the gandharvas (celestial musicians)
- **chāyā-ātapayoḥ iva** — as shadow and sunlight — that distinctly
- **brahmaloke** — in the world of Brahma

## Meaning

The verse grades how clearly the Self can be seen, world by world. In a dream the image is murky; in water it trembles; in a mirror it is sharp. The commentarial tradition reads this as a quiet compliment to human life: here, in a mind made clean, the Self can appear mirror-clear — a clarity the ancestor-world and the gandharva-world do not offer, and which is otherwise matched only in Brahmaloka, where seer and seen stand apart like shadow and sunlight. The point is practical rather than cosmological: do not postpone. The sharpest seeing on offer is available now, in this body, to a mind made still.

## Meaning (hi)

यह श्लोक बताता है कि आत्मा का दर्शन हर लोक में एक-सा साफ़ नहीं होता। स्वप्न में छवि धुँधली है; पानी में काँपती है; दर्पण में एकदम स्पष्ट। भाष्य-परंपरा इसे मनुष्य-जन्म की मौन प्रशंसा मानती है: यहीं, निर्मल हुई बुद्धि में, आत्मा दर्पण जैसी साफ़ दिख सकती है — ऐसी स्पष्टता जो पितृलोक और गन्धर्वलोक में नहीं मिलती, और जो आगे केवल ब्रह्मलोक में है, जहाँ द्रष्टा और दृश्य छाया-धूप की तरह अलग-अलग दिखते हैं। संदेश ब्रह्मांड-विद्या नहीं, व्यवहार है: टालो मत। जो सबसे साफ़ दर्शन संभव है, वह यहीं उपलब्ध है — इसी शरीर में, ठहरे हुए मन को।
