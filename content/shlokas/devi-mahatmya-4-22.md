## Shloka

**Devanagari:** केनोपमा भवतु तेऽस्य पराक्रमस्य / रूपं च शत्रुभयकार्यतिहारि कुत्र । / चित्ते कृपा समरनिष्ठुरता च दृष्टा / त्वय्येव देवि वरदे भुवनत्रयेऽपि ॥4.22॥

**Meaning:** With what could this valour of yours be compared? And where is there a form like this — so captivating, and yet striking terror into enemies? Compassion in the heart and harshness in battle: in all three worlds, O Devi, giver of boons, these have been seen together only in you.

## Word by word

- **kena upamā bhavatu** — with what could there be a comparison
- **te asya parākramasya** — of this valour of yours
- **rūpam ca** — and (such a) form
- **śatru-bhaya-kāri** — causing fear to enemies
- **ati-hāri** — exceedingly captivating
- **kutra** — where (else)
- **citte kṛpā** — compassion in the heart
- **samara-niṣṭhuratā ca** — and harshness in battle
- **dṛṣṭā** — have been seen
- **tvayi eva** — in you alone
- **devi varade** — O Devi, giver of boons
- **bhuvana-traye api** — even in all three worlds

## Meaning

The paradox verse. The gods stop trying to describe and simply name the contradiction: a form captivating and terrifying at once; a heart full of compassion and a hand merciless in battle — both together, in one person, nowhere else in the three worlds. How do the two fit? The hymn's answer, across 4.18, 4.19 and 4.21, is that the harshness _is_ the compassion seen from the other side. Every tradition that recites the text — Shakta, Smarta, Vaishnava — reads this line the same way; they diverge only on whom they finally identify the Devi with. A rich verse for anyone who must be gentle and firm in the same week.

## Meaning (hi)

विरोधाभास का श्लोक। देवता वर्णन की कोशिश छोड़कर बस विरोधाभास का नाम ले लेते हैं: ऐसा रूप जो एक साथ मनोहर और भयावह है; करुणा से भरा हृदय और युद्ध में निर्मम हाथ — दोनों साथ, एक ही व्यक्ति में, तीनों लोकों में कहीं और नहीं। ये दोनों कैसे बैठते हैं? स्तोत्र का उत्तर — 4.18, 4.19 और 4.21 में — यह है कि कठोरता _ही_ करुणा है, दूसरी ओर से देखी गई। पाठ करने वाली हर परम्परा — शाक्त, स्मार्त, वैष्णव — इस पंक्ति को एक ही तरह पढ़ती है; अन्तर बस इसमें है कि अन्ततः वे देवी को किसके साथ एक मानती हैं।

## Reflection

Where in your life does the soft heart require the hard hand — and have you been withholding one to keep the other?
