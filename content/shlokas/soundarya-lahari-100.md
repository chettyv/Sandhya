## Shloka

**Devanagari:** प्रदीपज्वालाभिर्दिवसकरनीराजनविधिः / सुधासूतेश्चन्द्रोपलजललवैरर्घ्यरचना । / स्वकीयैरम्भोभिः सलिलनिधिसौहित्यकरणं / त्वदीयाभिर्वाग्भिस्तव जननि वाचां स्तुतिरियम् ॥100॥

**Meaning:** As the rite of arati offered to the sun with the flames of little lamps; as arghya made to the moon, nectar's own source, with drops of water sweated by moonstones; as the quenching of the ocean with its own waters — just so, Mother of all speech, is this hymn of praise to you, fashioned out of words that are already yours.

## Word by word

- **pradīpa-jvālābhiḥ** — with the flames of lamps
- **divasa-kara-nīrājana-vidhiḥ** — the rite of ārati to the day-maker, the sun
- **sudhā-sūteḥ** — to the begetter of nectar, the moon
- **candra-upala-jala-lavaiḥ** — with drops of water from moonstones
- **arghya-racanā** — the making of the arghya offering
- **svakīyaiḥ ambhobhiḥ** — with its own waters
- **salila-nidhi-sauhitya-karaṇam** — the satisfying of the ocean
- **tvadīyābhiḥ vāgbhiḥ** — with words that are your own
- **tava** — to you
- **janani** — O Mother
- **vācām** — of (all) speech
- **stutiḥ iyam** — is this hymn of praise

## Meaning

The hymn's famous closing verse, and its whole theology of gratitude in three images. A lamp waved before the sun offers the sun its own light; moonstones, said in poetry to weep under moonlight, offer the moon its own dew; every river poured into the ocean is the ocean's water coming home. So with praise: she is the mother of speech itself, and a hundred verses addressed to her were composed out of her own gift. The poem — traditionally attributed to Adi Shankaracharya, though scholars debate the attribution — signs off not with an author's name but with this confession: the words were never the author's to give.

## Meaning (hi)

स्तोत्र का प्रसिद्ध समापन-श्लोक, और तीन चित्रों में कृतज्ञता का उसका पूरा तत्त्वदर्शन। सूर्य के आगे घुमाया दीपक सूर्य को उसका अपना प्रकाश अर्पित करता है; चन्द्रकान्त मणियाँ — जो काव्य-रीति में चाँदनी छूते ही रिसने लगती हैं — चन्द्रमा को उसकी अपनी ओस चढ़ाती हैं; समुद्र में उँडेली हर नदी समुद्र का ही जल घर लौटाती है। स्तुति का भी यही हाल: देवी स्वयं वाणी की जननी हैं, और उन्हें अर्पित सौ श्लोक उन्हीं की देन से रचे गए। यह काव्य — परम्परा से आदि शंकराचार्य का माना जाता है, यद्यपि विद्वानों में इस पर मतभेद है — किसी रचनाकार के नाम से नहीं, इसी स्वीकारोक्ति से विदा लेता है: ये शब्द देने के लिए कभी रचनाकार के थे ही नहीं।

## Reflection

What have you been proud of giving that was, all along, first given to you?
