## Shloka

**Devanagari:** तावेव पवनर्धि च चक्रतुर्वह्निकर्म च । / अन्येषाञ्चाधिकारान् स स्वयमेवाधितिष्ठति । / ततो देवा विनिर्धूता भ्रष्टराज्याः पराजिताः ॥5.3॥

**IAST:** tāveva pavanardhi ca cakraturvahnikarma ca । / anyeṣāñcādhikārān sa svayamevādhitiṣṭhati । / tato devā vinirdhūtā bhraṣṭarājyāḥ parājitāḥ ॥5.3॥
**Say it:** taa-VAY-va pa-va-NAR-dhi cha chak-ra-TOOR VAH-ni-KAR-ma cha / an-yay-SHAAN chaa-dhi-KAA-raan sa sva-yam ay-VAA-dhi-TISH-tha-ti / ta-TOH day-VAA vi-nir-DHOO-taa BHRASH-ta-RAAJ-yaah pa-RAA-ji-taah
**Meaning:** Those two likewise seized the wind's power and the fire's function; and the offices of the others Shumbha himself took over. Then the gods, cast out, fallen from kingdom, stood defeated.

## Word by word

- **tau eva** — those two themselves
- **pavana-ṛdhim** — the wind's power, its flourishing
- **ca** — and
- **cakratuḥ** — the two took over
- **vahni-karma** — the fire's function
- **ca** — and
- **anyeṣām ca** — and of the others
- **adhikārān** — the offices
- **saḥ** — he (Shumbha)
- **svayam eva** — himself
- **adhitiṣṭhati** — presides over
- **tataḥ** — then
- **devāḥ** — the gods
- **vinirdhūtāḥ** — shaken out, cast out
- **bhraṣṭa-rājyāḥ** — fallen from kingdom
- **parājitāḥ** — defeated

## Meaning

The takeover completes itself: even the wind's motion and fire's burning — the most elemental of functions — pass into asura hands, and whatever offices remain, Shumbha simply sits on himself. The verse ends with three heavy past participles: shaken out, kingdom-less, defeated. The narrative logic of all three deeds of the Devi Mahatmya is compressed here — the gods' own power must be entirely exhausted before the Devi acts, because what she gives is not reinforcement but rescue from a different order altogether. The text lets the defeat be total, without softening it, so that what comes next cannot be mistaken for the gods' own comeback.

## Meaning (hi)

कब्ज़ा अपनी हद तक पहुँचता है: वायु की गति और अग्नि का जलना — सबसे बुनियादी कार्य — भी असुरों के हाथ चले जाते हैं, और जो पद बचते हैं उन पर शुम्भ खुद बैठ जाता है। श्लोक तीन भारी शब्दों पर खत्म होता है: निकाले हुए, राज्यच्युत, पराजित। देवी माहात्म्य के तीनों चरित्रों का तर्क यहाँ सिमटा है — देवताओं का अपना बल पूरी तरह चुक जाना चाहिए, तभी देवी उतरती हैं, क्योंकि वे जो देती हैं वह कुमक नहीं, किसी और ही कोटि का उद्धार है। पाठ हार को पूरा होने देता है, बिना नरम किए — ताकि आगे जो हो, उसे देवताओं की अपनी वापसी न समझ लिया जाए।
