## Shloka

**Devanagari:** आरभ्य कर्माणि गुणान्वितानि भावांश्च सर्वान् विनियोजयेद्यः । / तेषामभावे कृतकर्मनाशः कर्मक्षये याति स तत्त्वतोऽन्यः ॥6.4॥

## Word by word

- **ārabhya** — having set in motion
- **karmāṇi** — works
- **guṇa-anvitāni** — joined with the gunas
- **bhāvān ca sarvān** — and all states of being
- **viniyojayet yaḥ** — who allots, assigns
- **teṣām abhāve** — when these are no more
- **kṛta-karma-nāśaḥ** — the work done dissolves
- **karma-kṣaye** — on the wearing out of karma
- **yāti** — he goes on
- **saḥ** — he
- **tattvataḥ anyaḥ** — in essence other

## Meaning

A famously difficult verse, and the difficulty is part of its record — translators genuinely disagree about who "he" is. Read of the Lord: he begins the guna-woven works and assigns every being its condition, yet when the works dissolve he remains, other in essence than everything he made. Read of the seeker, in the line Shankara's school prefers: dedicate every act and every disposition to God, and when karma wears out, you go free — because you were never, in essence, the works. Both readings share one spine: neither God nor the self is finally identical with its output. The doer outlasts the deed.

## Meaning (hi)

यह श्लोक कठिन माना जाता है, और कठिनाई ईमानदारी से दर्ज है — अनुवादक इस पर सहमत नहीं कि 'वह' कौन है। ईश्वर-परक पाठ: वह गुणों से बुने कर्मों को आरम्भ करता है, हर प्राणी को उसकी दशा सौंपता है, पर कर्मों के विलीन होने पर भी बचा रहता है — अपनी रचना से तत्त्वतः अन्य। साधक-परक पाठ, जिसे शंकर-परंपरा पसंद करती है: हर कर्म, हर भाव ईश्वर को अर्पित करो; कर्म चुक जाए तो तुम मुक्त — क्योंकि तत्त्वतः तुम कभी कर्म थे ही नहीं। दोनों पाठों की रीढ़ एक है: न ईश्वर अपनी रचना में चुकता है, न आत्मा अपने कामों में। करने वाला किए हुए से बड़ा है।

## Reflection

If you are, in essence, other than your work, what are you afraid would be left of you without it?
