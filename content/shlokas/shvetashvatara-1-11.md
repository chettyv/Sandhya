## Shloka

**Devanagari:** ज्ञात्वा देवं सर्वपाशापहानिः क्षीणैः क्लेशेर्जन्ममृत्युप्रहाणिः । / तस्याभिध्यानात्तृतीयं देहभेदे विश्वैश्वर्यं केवल आप्तकामः ॥1.11॥

**IAST:** jñātvā devaṃ sarvapāśāpahāniḥ kṣīṇaiḥ kleśerjanmamṛtyuprahāṇiḥ । / tasyābhidhyānāttṛtīyaṃ dehabhede viśvaiśvaryaṃ kevala āptakāmaḥ ॥1.11॥
**Say it:** gyaat-VAA DAY-vum sar-va-paa-SHAA-pa-HAA-nih KSHEE-naih KLAY-shair jun-ma-MRIT-yoo-pra-HAA-nih / tus-YAA-bhi-DHYAA-naat tri-TEE-yum day-ha-BHAY-day vish-VAISH-var-yum KAY-va-la aap-ta-KAA-mah
**Meaning:** When the God is known, all fetters fall away; with the afflictions worn thin, birth and death come to an end. By meditating on him there is a third state — at the body's parting, lordship over all; whole in oneself, every longing fulfilled.

## Word by word

- **jñātvā devam** — having known the God
- **sarva-pāśa-apahāniḥ** — the falling away of all fetters
- **kṣīṇaiḥ kleśaiḥ** — with the afflictions worn thin
- **janma-mṛtyu-prahāṇiḥ** — the ceasing of birth and death
- **tasya abhidhyānāt** — by meditating on him
- **tṛtīyam** — a third (state)
- **deha-bhede** — at the parting of the body
- **viśva-aiśvaryam** — lordship over all
- **kevalaḥ** — alone, whole in oneself
- **āpta-kāmaḥ** — one whose longings are fulfilled

## Meaning

The refrain of 1.8 is unfolded into a sequence of releases: fetters fall, then the kleśas — the deep afflictions that keep the wheel of 1.6 spinning — wear thin, and the cycle itself winds down. The engine of all of it is one word this Upanishad loves: abhidhyāna, sustained loving attention to the God, not analysis of him. Commentators differ over what the "third" state is — a third condition beyond bondage and ordinary release, or the fruit that comes third in the list — and the theistic schools read the closing lordship as shared from the Lord by grace, while Advaita reads it as the natural sovereignty of the one Self. The last word may be the best translation of mokṣa on offer: āptakāma — not a person with no desires, but a person whose desire has finally arrived.

## Meaning (hi)

1.8 की टेक यहाँ मुक्तियों की एक शृंखला में खुलती है: पहले पाश गिरते हैं, फिर क्लेश — वे गहरी गाँठें जो 1.6 के चक्र को घुमाए रखती हैं — क्षीण होते हैं, और फिर चक्र ही थम जाता है। इस सबका इंजन एक शब्द है जो इस उपनिषद को प्रिय है: अभिध्यान — देव का विश्लेषण नहीं, उस पर टिका हुआ प्रेमपूर्ण ध्यान। "तृतीय" अवस्था क्या है, इस पर भाष्यकार अलग-अलग हैं — बंधन और साधारण मुक्ति से परे कोई तीसरी दशा, या सूची में तीसरा फल — और भेदवादी परंपराएँ अंतिम ऐश्वर्य को प्रभु की कृपा से मिला हुआ पढ़ती हैं, जबकि अद्वैत उसे एक आत्मा की सहज प्रभुता। अंतिम शब्द शायद मोक्ष का सबसे अच्छा अनुवाद है: आप्तकाम — वह नहीं जिसकी कोई चाह नहीं, बल्कि वह जिसकी चाह आख़िरकार पूरी हो गई।

## Reflection

If steady attention really is the key that loosens your tightest knots, what will you give it to first each day?
