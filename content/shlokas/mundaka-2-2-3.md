## Shloka

**Devanagari:** धनुर्गृहीत्वौपनिषदं महास्त्रं / शरं ह्युपासा निशितं सन्धयीत । / आयम्य तद्भावगतेन चेतसा / लक्ष्यं तदेवाक्षरं सोम्य विद्धि ॥2.2.3॥

**IAST:** dhanurgṛhītvaupaniṣadaṃ mahāstraṃ / śaraṃ hyupāsā niśitaṃ sandhayīta । / āyamya tadbhāvagatena cetasā / lakṣyaṃ tadevākṣaraṃ somya viddhi ॥2.2.3॥
**Say it:** dha-nur-gri-hee-TVOW-pa-ni-sha-dum ma-HAAST-rum / SHA-rum hyu-PAA-saa NI-shi-tum san-dha-YEE-ta / AA-yam-ya tad-bhaa-va-ga-TAY-na CHAY-ta-saa / LUCK-shyum ta-day-VAAK-sha-rum SOM-ya VID-dhi
**Meaning:** Take up the bow of the Upanishads, that great weapon; fit to it an arrow sharpened by devoted practice. Draw it with a mind absorbed into the being of That, and know, dear one: the target is that very Imperishable — pierce it.

## Word by word

- **dhanuḥ gṛhītvā** — taking up the bow
- **aupaniṣadam** — the one belonging to the Upanishads
- **mahā-astram** — the great weapon
- **śaram** — the arrow
- **hi upāsā-niśitam** — sharpened by devoted practice
- **sandhayīta** — let one fit it to the string
- **āyamya** — drawing it back
- **tat-bhāva-gatena cetasā** — with a mind entered into the being of That
- **lakṣyam** — the target
- **tat eva akṣaram** — that very Imperishable
- **somya** — dear one
- **viddhi** — pierce it, know it

## Meaning

The Upanishad now gives its method as an archer's checklist. The bow is "of the Upanishads" — the teaching itself supplies the frame and the tension. The arrow is you, and it does not come sharp: it is whetted by upasana, steady devoted practice, the daily honing that no flash of insight replaces. The draw is attention — a mind "gone into the being of That", pulled back from scatter into one line. Only then is the release worth anything. Notice what the verse quietly claims: truth is not sighted by drifting toward it but by tension rightly held — study, sharpening, and a gathered mind, all pointed at a single mark.

## Meaning (hi)

उपनिषद अब अपनी विधि एक धनुर्धर की सूची की तरह देता है। धनुष "औपनिषद" है — ढाँचा और तनाव शिक्षा ही देती है। बाण तुम स्वयं हो, और वह धारदार पैदा नहीं होता: उसे उपासना — रोज़ का सधा हुआ अभ्यास — तेज़ करती है, जिसकी जगह कोई अचानक मिली झलक नहीं ले सकती। डोरी का खिंचाव ध्यान है — "उस-मय" हुआ चित्त, बिखराव से खिंचकर एक सीध में आया हुआ। तभी छोड़ना सार्थक है। ग़ौर करो कि श्लोक चुपचाप क्या कह रहा है: सत्य तक बहते-बहते नहीं पहुँचा जाता; ठीक से साधा हुआ तनाव पहुँचाता है — स्वाध्याय, धार, और सिमटा हुआ मन, तीनों एक ही निशाने पर।
