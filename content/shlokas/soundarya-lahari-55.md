## Shloka

**Devanagari:** निमेषोन्मेषाभ्यां प्रलयमुदयं याति जगती / तवेत्याहुः सन्तो धरणिधरराजन्यतनये । / त्वदुन्मेषाज्जातं जगदिदमशेषं प्रलयतः / परित्रातुं शङ्के परिहृतनिमेषास्तव दृशः ॥55॥

**Say it:** ni-may-shon-MAY-shaabh-yaam PRA-la-yam-OO-da-yum YAA-ti JA-ga-tee / ta-VAYT-yaa-hooh SAN-to DHA-ra-ni-DHA-ra-RAA-jan-ya-TA-na-yay / tva-doon-may-SHAAJ JAA-tum JA-ga-di-dam-a-SHAY-shum PRA-la-ya-tah / pa-ri-TRAA-toom SHUN-kay PA-ri-HRI-ta-ni-MAY-shaas TA-va DRI-shah
**Meaning:** The wise say, O daughter of the king of mountains, that with the closing and the opening of your eyes the world goes to dissolution and rises again — and so I suspect it is to save this whole world, born of one opening of your eyes, from ever dissolving, that your eyes have given up blinking altogether.

## Word by word

- **nimeṣa-unmeṣābhyām** — by the closing and opening (of your eyes)
- **pralayam udayam** — dissolution and arising
- **yāti jagatī** — the world goes to
- **tava iti āhuḥ santaḥ** — so say the wise, of you
- **dharaṇi-dhara-rājanya-tanaye** — O daughter of the king of the earth-bearing mountains
- **tvat-unmeṣāt jātam** — born from the opening of your eyes
- **jagat idam aśeṣam** — this whole world
- **pralayataḥ paritrātum** — to save from dissolution
- **śaṅke** — I suspect
- **parihṛta-nimeṣāḥ** — having renounced blinking
- **tava dṛśaḥ** — your eyes

## Meaning

Hindu iconography knows the gods by an odd detail: their eyes never blink. This verse asks why the Devi's do not, and answers with a tenderness that turns cosmology into mothering. The wise say worlds rise when her eyes open and dissolve when they close. Taken strictly, that makes every blink an apocalypse. So, the poet reasons, she has simply stopped blinking — holding her eyes open without rest so that this world, born of one glance, is never even momentarily let go. What looks like an emblem of divinity becomes an act of ongoing care: existence continues because the Mother will not look away. The universe is kept alive the way a mother watches a sleeping child.

## Meaning (hi)

हिन्दू प्रतिमा-शास्त्र देवताओं को एक विचित्र लक्षण से पहचानता है: उनकी पलकें कभी नहीं झपकतीं। यह श्लोक पूछता है कि देवी की क्यों नहीं झपकतीं, और उत्तर में ब्रह्माण्ड-विद्या ममता बन जाती है। सन्त कहते हैं कि उनके नेत्र खुलते हैं तो जगत उदय होता है, मुँदते हैं तो प्रलय। कठोरता से लें तो हर पलक-झपक एक प्रलय है। तो कवि का तर्क है — उन्होंने पलक झपकना छोड़ ही दिया है; बिना विश्राम आँखें खोले रखती हैं, ताकि एक चितवन से जन्मा यह संसार क्षण-भर को भी छूट न जाए। जो दिव्यता का चिह्न लगता था, वह अनवरत देखभाल निकलती है: सृष्टि इसलिए चल रही है कि माँ नज़र नहीं हटातीं — जैसे कोई माँ सोते शिशु को एकटक देखती रहे।

## Reflection

Who in your life is held steady simply because you refuse to look away?
