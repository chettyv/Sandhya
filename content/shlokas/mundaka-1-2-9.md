## Shloka

**Devanagari:** अविद्यायां बहुधा वर्तमाना वयं / कृतार्था इत्यभिमन्यन्ति बालाः । / यत् कर्मिणो न प्रवेदयन्ति रागात् / तेनातुराः क्षीणलोकाश्च्यवन्ते ॥1.2.9॥

**Say it:** a-vid-YAA-yaam ba-hu-DHAA var-ta-MAA-naa va-YUM / kri-TAAR-thaa it-ya-bhi-MAN-yan-ti BAA-laah / yat kar-mi-NO na pra-VAY-da-yan-ti raa-GAAT / tay-NAA-tu-raah kshee-na-lo-KAASH chya-VAN-tay
**Meaning:** Dwelling in ignorance in many ways, the childish tell themselves, "We have reached the goal." Because those bound to works do not come to know — out of attachment — they sink down, anguished, when their worlds are spent.

## Word by word

- **avidyāyām** — in ignorance
- **bahudhā** — in many ways
- **vartamānāḥ** — dwelling
- **vayam kṛtārthāḥ** — "we have reached the goal"
- **iti abhimanyanti** — so they imagine
- **bālāḥ** — the childish, the immature
- **yat** — since
- **karmiṇaḥ** — those devoted to works
- **na pravedayanti** — do not come to know
- **rāgāt** — out of attachment
- **tena** — therefore
- **āturāḥ** — anguished, afflicted
- **kṣīṇalokāḥ** — their worlds exhausted
- **cyavante** — they fall

## Meaning

"We have arrived" — the verse treats this as the most dangerous sentence in the inner life. Its diagnosis is precise and generous at once: the karmis fail to know not because they are stupid but because of raga, attachment — the current arrangement pays them, so they have a stake in not looking further. The economics of the fall are equally exact: merit behaves like savings, and kshina-lokah means the account runs dry. What follows is not punishment imposed from above; it is arithmetic — a finite deposit funding a stay that was always temporary. The anguish comes from the surprise. The verse's mercy is in the word balah, childish: not wicked, just unfinished — still able to grow.

## Meaning (hi)

"हम तो पहुँच गए" — यह श्लोक इस वाक्य को भीतरी जीवन का सबसे ख़तरनाक वाक्य मानता है। इसका निदान सटीक भी है और उदार भी: कर्मी जान नहीं पाते, इसलिए नहीं कि वे मूर्ख हैं, बल्कि 'रागात्' — आसक्ति से; मौजूदा बंदोबस्त उन्हें फल देता है, इसलिए आगे न देखने में उनका हित बँधा है। गिरने का गणित भी उतना ही साफ़ है: पुण्य जमा-पूँजी की तरह चलता है, और 'क्षीणलोकाः' का मतलब है — खाता ख़ाली हो गया। जो होता है वह ऊपर से दिया दंड नहीं है; हिसाब है — सीमित जमा से चल रहा प्रवास, जो हमेशा से अस्थायी था। व्याकुलता उस अचरज से आती है। श्लोक की करुणा 'बालाः' शब्द में है: दुष्ट नहीं, बस कच्चे — जो अब भी बढ़ सकते हैं।

## Reflection

Where might comfort, rather than evidence, be what convinces you that you are already done growing?
