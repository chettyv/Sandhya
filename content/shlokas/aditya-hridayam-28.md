## Shloka

**Devanagari:** एतच्छ्रुत्वा महातेजा नष्टशोकोऽभवत्तदा । / धारयामास सुप्रीतो राघवः प्रयतात्मवान् ॥28॥

**Say it:** ay-tach-CHRUT-vaa ma-haa-TAY-jaa nash-ta-SHO-ko-bha-vat ta-DAA / dhaa-ra-yaa-MAA-sa su-PREE-to RAA-gha-vah pra-ya-TAAT-ma-vaan
**Meaning:** When he had heard this, the mighty Raghava's grief fell away; deeply gladdened and with a collected mind, he took the hymn to heart.

## Word by word

- **etat śrutvā** — having heard this
- **mahā-tejāḥ** — the one of great splendour
- **naṣṭa-śokaḥ** — his grief destroyed
- **abhavat** — became
- **tadā** — then
- **dhārayāmāsa** — he held it, took it to heart
- **su-prītaḥ** — deeply gladdened
- **rāghavaḥ** — Raghava (Rama)
- **prayata-ātmavān** — with a collected, disciplined self

## Meaning

The hinge of the whole text. Nothing outside has changed — Ravana still stands within bowshot — yet everything has: naṣṭa-śokaḥ, his grief destroyed, Rama is suprīta, deeply gladdened, and prayatātmavān, collected, self-possessed. The verb that carries it all is dhārayāmāsa: he held the hymn, took it into himself. Before reciting comes receiving; the teaching had to be gripped like a hand, not filed like information. The epic is precise about the order of rescue — state first, situation after. The battle's turn begins here, in an interior event no one on the field could see. Grief, the story insists, can end before circumstances do.

## Meaning (hi)

पूरे पाठ की धुरी। बाहर कुछ नहीं बदला — रावण अब भी बाण भर की दूरी पर खड़ा है — फिर भी सब बदल गया: नष्टशोकः — शोक नष्ट हुआ, राम सुप्रीत हैं — गहरे प्रसन्न — और प्रयतात्मवान् — संयत, अपने वश में। सारा भार एक क्रिया उठाती है: धारयामास — उन्होंने स्तोत्र को धारण किया, अपने भीतर उतार लिया। जप से पहले ग्रहण आता है; शिक्षा को सूचना की तरह दर्ज नहीं, हाथ की तरह थामना था। महाकाव्य उद्धार के क्रम को लेकर सटीक है — पहले दशा, फिर परिस्थिति। युद्ध का पासा यहीं पलटना शुरू होता है, एक ऐसी भीतरी घटना में जो मैदान पर किसी को दिखी नहीं। कथा की ज़िद है: शोक हालात से पहले भी समाप्त हो सकता है।
