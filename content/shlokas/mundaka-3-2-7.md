## Shloka

**Devanagari:** गताः कलाः पञ्चदश प्रतिष्ठा / देवाश्च सर्वे प्रतिदेवतासु । / कर्माणि विज्ञानमयश्च आत्मा / परेऽव्यये सर्वे एकीभवन्ति ॥3.2.7॥

**Meaning:** The fifteen parts go back to their foundations; all the deities of the body return to their presiding gods. Actions, and the self made of understanding — all become one in the supreme Imperishable.

## Word by word

- **gatāḥ** — gone, returned
- **kalāḥ** — the parts, constituents
- **pañcadaśa** — fifteen
- **pratiṣṭhāḥ** — to their foundations
- **devāḥ ca sarve** — and all the deities (the powers behind the senses)
- **prati-devatāsu** — into their corresponding presiding gods
- **karmāṇi** — actions
- **vijñānamayaḥ ca ātmā** — and the self made of understanding
- **pare** — in the supreme
- **avyaye** — in the imperishable
- **sarve** — all
- **ekībhavanti** — become one

## Meaning

This is the Upanishad's account of what happens when a knower's life completes: the borrowed things go home. The fifteen parts that assembled a person return to their foundations — water to water, breath to air; each faculty rejoins the cosmic power that stood behind it, eye to sun, speech to fire. Even actions, that long ledger, and the fine self made of understanding — all become one in the imperishable. Advaita reads ekībhavanti as full identity, difference ending like a wave's outline; Vishishtadvaita and Dvaita read an inseparable union in which the soul remains real within the Lord. What the verse itself refuses to do is leave anything at all outside the One.

## Meaning (hi)

यह उस घड़ी का उपनिषद का ब्योरा है जब ज्ञानी का जीवन पूरा होता है: उधार ली हुई चीज़ें अपने घर लौट जाती हैं। जिन पंद्रह कलाओं ने व्यक्ति को जोड़ा था, वे अपनी नींवों में लौटती हैं — पानी पानी में, प्राण वायु में; हर इंद्रिय अपने पीछे खड़ी ब्रह्मांडीय शक्ति से जा मिलती है — आँख सूर्य से, वाणी अग्नि से। कर्म भी — वह लंबा बहीखाता — और विज्ञानमय आत्मा भी: सब परम अव्यय में एक हो जाते हैं। अद्वैत 'एकीभवन्ति' को पूर्ण एकत्व पढ़ता है — भेद का मिट जाना, जैसे लहर की रेखा; विशिष्टाद्वैत और द्वैत उसे अभिन्न मिलन पढ़ते हैं जिसमें आत्मा प्रभु के भीतर वास्तविक बनी रहती है। श्लोक ख़ुद बस इतना करता है: किसी को भी उस एक से बाहर नहीं छोड़ता।

## Reflection

Which of the things you call "mine" — breath, body, cleverness — is really on loan, and from where?
