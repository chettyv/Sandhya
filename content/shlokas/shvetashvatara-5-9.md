## Shloka

**Devanagari:** बालाग्रशतभागस्य शतधा कल्पितस्य च । / भागो जीवः स विज्ञेयः स चानन्त्याय कल्पते ॥5.9॥

**IAST:** bālāgraśatabhāgasya śatadhā kalpitasya ca । / bhāgo jīvaḥ sa vijñeyaḥ sa cānantyāya kalpate ॥5.9॥
**Say it:** baa-LAAG-ra-sha-ta-BHAA-gas-ya sha-ta-DHAA kal-pi-TAS-ya cha / BHAA-go JEE-vah sa vig-YAY-yah sa chaa-nan-TYAA-ya KAL-pa-tay
**Meaning:** Take the tip of a hair, divide it a hundred ways, then divide one part a hundred ways again: know the individual soul to be like a part of that — and yet it is capable of infinity.

## Word by word

- **bāla-agra** — the tip of a hair
- **śata-bhāgasya** — of a hundredth part
- **śatadhā** — a hundredfold
- **kalpitasya ca** — further divided
- **bhāgaḥ** — a part
- **jīvaḥ** — the individual soul
- **saḥ vijñeyaḥ** — is to be known as
- **saḥ ca** — and yet it
- **ānantyāya** — for infinity
- **kalpate** — is fit, reaches

## Meaning

The arithmetic is deliberate: take the tip of a hair, divide it a hundred times, then divide one part a hundred times again — a ten-thousandth of almost nothing. That, says the verse, is the scale of the individual soul. Then it turns on a single small word: ca, "and yet" — this near-nothing is fit for ānantya, infinity. The traditions weigh the two halves differently in good faith: Vishishtadvaita and Dvaita take the soul as truly atomic, its greatness lying in capacity rather than size, while Advaita reads the minuteness as apparent only, the self being in truth unlimited. Either way the grammar itself is the teaching — a near-nothing, and yet made for the infinite.

## Meaning (hi)

गणित जानबूझकर है: बाल की नोक के सौ भाग कीजिए, फिर उनमें से एक के फिर सौ भाग — लगभग शून्य का दस-हज़ारवाँ हिस्सा। श्लोक कहता है, जीव का पैमाना यही है। और फिर वह एक छोटे-से शब्द पर मुड़ जाता है: "च" — और फिर भी — यह लगभग-शून्य आनन्त्य के योग्य है। परंपराएँ इन दोनों पलड़ों को ईमानदारी से अलग-अलग तौलती हैं: विशिष्टाद्वैत और द्वैत जीव को सचमुच अणु मानते हैं, जिसकी महिमा उसके आकार में नहीं, उसकी क्षमता में है; जबकि अद्वैत इस सूक्ष्मता को आभासी पढ़ता है — आत्मा वस्तुतः असीम है। दोनों पाठों में शिक्षा वाक्य की बनावट में ही है: जो लगते हो और जिसके योग्य हो, उनके बीच का वह "फिर भी"।

## Reflection

Where in your life are you letting your apparent size decide what you are capable of?
