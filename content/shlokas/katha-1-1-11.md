## Shloka

**Devanagari:** यथा पुरस्ताद् भविता प्रतीत / औद्दालकिरारुणिर्मत्प्रसृष्टः । / सुखँ रात्रीः शयिता वीतमन्युः / त्वां ददृशिवान्मृत्युमुखात् प्रमुक्तम् ॥1.1.11॥

**Meaning:** Auddalaki Aruni will be just as he was before — he will recognize you, by my leave. He will sleep peacefully through his nights, his anger gone, having seen you freed from the mouth of death.

## Word by word

- **yathā purastāt** — just as before
- **bhavitā** — he will be
- **pratītaḥ** — recognizing (you), trusting
- **auddālakiḥ āruṇiḥ** — Auddalaki Aruni (Nachiketa's father)
- **mat-prasṛṣṭaḥ** — by my leave, released by me
- **sukham** — peacefully
- **rātrīḥ śayitā** — he will sleep through the nights
- **vīta-manyuḥ** — his anger gone
- **tvām dadṛśivān** — having seen you
- **mṛtyu-mukhāt** — from the mouth of death
- **pramuktam** — set free

## Meaning

Death grants the first boon in full, and adds a detail no one asked for: the father will sleep peacefully through his nights. The verse measures forgiveness not in words but in sleep — the anger that hurled a son toward Death is the same anger that keeps a man staring at the ceiling. The father is named formally here, Auddalaki Aruni, as if to restore his dignity along with his rest. And the reunion is pictured precisely: he will see his son standing free of the very mouth of death, and the seeing itself will complete the repair.

## Meaning (hi)

मृत्यु पहला वर पूरा-पूरा देते हैं, और एक ऐसा ब्योरा भी जोड़ते हैं जो किसी ने माँगा नहीं था: पिता रातों को चैन से सोएँगे। यह श्लोक क्षमा को शब्दों से नहीं, नींद से नापता है — जिस क्रोध ने बेटे को मृत्यु की ओर धकेला था, वही क्रोध आदमी को रात भर छत ताकने पर मजबूर रखता है। यहाँ पिता को औपचारिक नाम से पुकारा गया है — औद्दालकि आरुणि — मानो विश्राम के साथ उनकी गरिमा भी लौटाई जा रही हो। और मिलन की तस्वीर भी सटीक है: वे अपने बेटे को साक्षात मृत्यु के मुख से छूटा हुआ देखेंगे, और वह देखना ही मरम्मत पूरी कर देगा।

## Reflection

Whose sound sleep might depend on a word of forgiveness from you?
