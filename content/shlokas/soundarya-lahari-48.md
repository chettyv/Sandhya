## Shloka

**Devanagari:** अहः सूते सव्यं तव नयनमर्कात्मकतया / त्रियामां वामं ते सृजति रजनीनायकतया । / तृतीया ते दृष्टिर्दरदलितहेमाम्बुजरुचिः / समाधत्ते संध्यां दिवसनिशयोरन्तरचरीम् ॥48॥

**Meaning:** Your right eye, having the sun for its nature, gives birth to the day; your left, as the moon, sovereign of night, creates the night of three watches; and your third glance, glowing like a golden lotus just barely open, brings forth the twilight that moves between day and night.

## Word by word

- **ahaḥ sūte** — gives birth to the day
- **savyam tava nayanam** — your right eye
- **arka-ātmakatayā** — by having the nature of the sun
- **triyāmām** — the night ("of three watches")
- **vāmam te** — your left (eye)
- **sṛjati** — creates
- **rajanī-nāyakatayā** — as the lord of night, the moon
- **tṛtīyā te dṛṣṭiḥ** — your third glance
- **dara-dalita-hema-ambuja-ruciḥ** — with the glow of a golden lotus just barely open
- **samādhatte** — brings into being, holds in place
- **sandhyām** — the twilight
- **divasa-niśayoḥ antara-carīm** — moving between day and night

## Meaning

In many Hindu texts the sun and moon are the two eyes of the cosmic form; the Devi has a third, and the poet asks what it must be doing. His answer: the right eye opens and it is day; the left opens and it is night; and the third, glowing gold like a lotus barely open, makes sandhya — dawn and dusk, the twilight that walks between the two. All time, this verse says, is her looking. The twilights are the traditional hours of prayer precisely because they belong to neither day nor night; here they are her gentlest glance, neither blazing nor dark. Whoever prays at dawn stands inside the light of her third eye.

## Meaning (hi)

कई ग्रन्थों में सूर्य और चन्द्रमा विराट रूप के दो नेत्र कहे गए हैं; देवी के पास तीसरा भी है, और कवि पूछता है कि वह क्या करता होगा। उत्तर: दाहिना नेत्र खुलता है तो दिन होता है; बायाँ खुलता है तो रात; और तीसरा — अधखिले सुनहरे कमल-सा दमकता — सन्ध्या रचता है, वह झुटपुटा जो दिन और रात के बीच चलता है। समय मात्र, यह श्लोक कहता है, देवी का देखना है। सन्ध्या-वेला प्रार्थना की परम्परागत घड़ी इसीलिए है कि वह न दिन की है न रात की; यहाँ वही देवी की सबसे कोमल दृष्टि है — न प्रचण्ड, न अन्धकारमय। जो भोर में प्रार्थना करता है, वह उनके तीसरे नेत्र के प्रकाश में खड़ा है।
