## Shloka

**Devanagari:** यश्च मर्त्यः स्तवैरेभिस्त्वां स्तोष्यत्यमलानने । / तस्य वित्तर्धिविभवैर्धनदारादिसम्पदाम् । / वृद्धयेऽस्मात्प्रसन्ना त्वं भवेथाः सर्वदाम्बिके ॥4.34॥

**Meaning:** And whatever mortal praises you with these hymns, O Devi of spotless face — be always gracious to him, O Ambika, so that through this his wealth, prosperity and power may grow, together with his riches, his family and every other good fortune.

## Word by word

- **yaḥ ca martyaḥ** — and whatever mortal
- **stavaiḥ ebhiḥ** — with these hymns
- **tvām stoṣyati** — will praise you
- **amala-ānane** — O Devi of spotless face
- **tasya** — his, for him
- **vitta-ṛddhi-vibhavaiḥ** — with wealth, prosperity and power
- **dhana-dāra-ādi-sampadām** — of fortunes such as riches, spouse and the rest
- **vṛddhaye** — for the increase
- **asmāt** — through this, by this (praise)
- **prasannā tvam bhavethāḥ** — may you be gracious
- **sarvadā** — always
- **ambike** — O Ambika

## Meaning

The gods' second request is not for themselves. It is for anyone who, later, says these same verses — a mortal, explicitly, martyaḥ — and this is the moment the text turns to face its own future readers. It is a phala-śruti, a statement of the fruit of recitation, and what it asks on the reader's behalf is ordinary: wealth, household, prosperity, a spouse. The Devi Mahatmya is at ease with worldly blessing as grace; the same list appeared in 4.15 and is not apologised for here. Out of the daily pool as dialogue, but anyone reciting the chapter should know that here the gods are, in a sense, praying for them.

## Meaning (hi)

देवताओं का दूसरा निवेदन अपने लिए नहीं है। यह उसके लिए है जो बाद में यही श्लोक कहे — एक मनुष्य, मर्त्यः — और यही वह क्षण है जब ग्रन्थ मुड़कर अपने भावी पाठकों की ओर देखता है। यह फल-श्रुति है, पाठ के फल का कथन, और पाठक की ओर से यह जो माँगता है वह साधारण है: धन, घर-गृहस्थी, समृद्धि, जीवनसाथी। देवी माहात्म्य सांसारिक आशीर्वाद को कृपा मानने में सहज है; यही सूची 4.15 में आई थी और यहाँ उसके लिए कोई सफ़ाई नहीं। संवाद होने से दैनिक चयन से बाहर, पर अध्याय पढ़ने वाले हर पाठक को पता होना चाहिए कि यहाँ देवता, एक अर्थ में, उसके लिए प्रार्थना कर रहे हैं।

## Reflection
