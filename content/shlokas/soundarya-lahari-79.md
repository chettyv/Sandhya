## Shloka

**Devanagari:** निसर्गक्षीणस्य स्तनतटभरेण क्लमजुषो / नमन्मूर्तेर्नारीतिलक शनकैस्त्रुट्यत इव । / चिरं ते मध्यस्य त्रुटिततटिनीतीरतरुणा / समावस्थास्थेम्नो भवतु कुशलं शैलतनये ॥79॥

<!-- review note: No conversion artifacts spotted. त्रुट्यत इव with pausa visarga dropped before इव is correct sandhi (truṭyataḥ + iva); the doubled truṭ- forms in pādas 2 and 3 are both genuine. -->

**IAST:** nisargakṣīṇasya stanataṭabhareṇa klamajuṣo / namanmūrternārītilaka śanakaistruṭyata iva । / ciraṃ te madhyasya truṭitataṭinītīrataruṇā / samāvasthāsthemno bhavatu kuśalaṃ śailatanaye ॥79॥
**Say it:** ni-sar-ga-KSHEE-nas-ya sta-na-ta-ta-bha-RAY-na kla-ma-ju-sho / na-man-MOOR-tayr NAA-ree-ti-la-ka sha-na-kais-TRUT-ya-ta i-va / chi-RUM tay MADH-yas-ya tru-ti-ta-ta-ti-nee-TEE-ra-ta-ru-naa / sa-MAA-vas-thaa-STHAYM-no BHA-va-tu ku-sha-lum shai-la-ta-na-yay
**Meaning:** Slender by nature, wearied by the weight it bears above, its form bowing until it seems slowly on the point of giving way — O crest-jewel among women, daughter of the mountain, may your waist, poised like a tree on a river-bank the current has torn, be safe and well for ever.
**Meaning (hi):** स्वभाव से क्षीण, ऊपर के भार से थकी, झुकती हुई ऐसी कि लगे धीरे-धीरे अब टूटी — हे नारी-तिलक, हे शैल-तनये! नदी की धार से कटे किनारे पर खड़े वृक्ष-सी अधर में थमी आपकी वह कटि सदा कुशल से रहे।

## Word by word

- **nisarga-kṣīṇasya** — slender by nature
- **stana-taṭa-bhareṇa** — under the weight of the bosom's slopes
- **klama-juṣaḥ** — grown weary
- **namat-mūrteḥ** — its form bowing
- **nārī-tilaka** — O crest-mark among women
- **śanakaiḥ truṭyataḥ iva** — as if slowly about to give way
- **ciram** — long, for ever
- **te madhyasya** — of your waist
- **truṭita-taṭinī-tīra-taruṇā** — with a tree on a river-bank torn by the stream
- **sama-avasthā-sthemnaḥ** — holding the same precarious poise
- **bhavatu kuśalam** — may there be well-being
- **śaila-tanaye** — O daughter of the mountain

## Meaning

Alone in this stretch of the poem, the poet stops describing and starts blessing. The Devi's waist is so slender, and what it carries so ample, that he imagines it like a tree on a river-bank the current has undercut — leaning, holding, surely about to go. And so the verse becomes a little āśīrvāda: kuśalaṃ bhavatu — may it be well, may it hold for ever. There is real tenderness in praying for the wellbeing of the all-powerful; the devotee's love outruns logic, the way one worries over a mother who needs no protection. Worship, this verse suggests, includes the absurd, affectionate impulse to bless the one who blesses us.

## Meaning (hi)

काव्य के इस पूरे खण्ड में केवल यहीं कवि वर्णन रोककर आशीर्वाद देने लगता है। देवी की कटि इतनी क्षीण है, और जो वह धारण करती है वह इतना भरा-पूरा, कि कवि को वह धार से कटे नदी-किनारे का वृक्ष लगती है — झुका हुआ, थमा हुआ, मानो अब गिरा। और श्लोक एक छोटा-सा आशीर्वचन बन जाता है: कुशलं भवतु — यह सदा सुरक्षित रहे। सर्वशक्तिमती के कुशल-क्षेम की प्रार्थना में एक सच्ची कोमलता है — भक्त का प्रेम तर्क से आगे निकल जाता है, जैसे हम उस माँ की चिन्ता करते हैं जिसे किसी रक्षा की आवश्यकता नहीं। पूजा में यह विलक्षण, स्नेह-भरा आवेग भी शामिल है: जो हमें आशीष देती है, उसे आशीष देना।

## Reflection

Who in your life is strong enough not to need your worry, and might still be touched to receive your blessing?
