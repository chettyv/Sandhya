## Shloka

**Devanagari:** देवैरत्रापि विचिकित्सितं किल / त्वं च मृत्यो यन्न सुज्ञेयमात्थ । / वक्ता चास्य त्वादृगन्यो न लभ्यो / नान्यो वरस्तुल्य एतस्य कश्चित् ॥1.1.22॥

**Meaning:** Even the gods, you say, had doubts here — and you, O Death, say it is not easily known. But no other teacher of this can be found who is your equal, and no other boon is equal to this one at all.

## Word by word

- **devaiḥ atra api** — even by the gods, on this
- **vicikitsitam kila** — doubt was had, you yourself say
- **tvam ca mṛtyo** — and you, O Death
- **yat na sujñeyam āttha** — since you say it is not easily known
- **vaktā ca asya** — and a teacher of this
- **tvādṛk anyaḥ** — another such as you
- **na labhyaḥ** — cannot be found
- **na anyaḥ varaḥ** — no other boon
- **tulyaḥ etasya** — equal to this
- **kaścit** — any at all

## Meaning

Nachiketa turns Death's refusal inside out and hands it back as an argument. If even the gods doubted, the question is not idle — it is the deepest one there is. If it is not easily known, then a qualified teacher is rarer still, and here one stands: who could teach the secret of death better than Death? And if no boon is equal to this one, accepting a lesser boon would be a bad trade by Death's own arithmetic. Each discouragement has been converted into a reason. This is what the tradition means by a fit student: not stubbornness, but reasoning that cannot be bought off.

## Meaning (hi)

नचिकेता मृत्यु के इनकार को उलटकर उन्हीं को तर्क बनाकर लौटा देता है। अगर देवताओं तक को संदेह रहा, तो प्रश्न बेकार नहीं — वह सबसे गहरा प्रश्न है। अगर यह सहज में जाना नहीं जाता, तो योग्य शिक्षक और भी दुर्लभ है — और वह सामने खड़ा है: मृत्यु का रहस्य स्वयं मृत्यु से बेहतर कौन सिखा सकता है? और अगर इस वर के बराबर कोई वर नहीं, तो कोई छोटा वर स्वीकारना ख़ुद मृत्यु के गणित से घाटे का सौदा होगा। हर हतोत्साह एक कारण में बदल दिया गया है। योग्य शिष्य से परंपरा का यही आशय है: ज़िद नहीं, बल्कि ऐसा तर्क जो किसी क़ीमत पर ख़रीदा न जा सके।

## Reflection

When you finally stand before someone who truly knows, do you soften your question or sharpen it?
