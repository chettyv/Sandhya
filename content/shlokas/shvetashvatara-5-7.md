## Shloka

**Devanagari:** गुणान्वयो यः फलकर्मकर्ता कृतस्य तस्यैव स चोपभोक्ता । / स विश्वरूपस्त्रिगुणस्त्रिवर्त्मा प्राणाधिपः सञ्चरति स्वकर्मभिः ॥5.7॥

**IAST:** guṇānvayo yaḥ phalakarmakartā kṛtasya tasyaiva sa copabhoktā । / sa viśvarūpastriguṇastrivartmā prāṇādhipaḥ sañcarati svakarmabhiḥ ॥5.7॥
**Say it:** goo-NAAN-va-yo yah pha-la-KAR-ma-kar-taa kri-TAS-ya TAS-yai-va sa cho-pa-BHOK-taa / sa vish-va-ROO-pas tri-goo-NAS tri-VART-maa praa-NAA-dhi-pah san-CHA-ra-ti sva-KAR-ma-bhih
**Meaning:** Joined to the qualities of nature, doing works for their fruits, the enjoyer of exactly what he has done — the embodied self, wearing every form, three-stranded, walking three roads, lord of the life-breaths, wanders on by its own actions.
**Meaning (hi):** गुणों से जुड़ा, फल के लिए कर्म करता, और जो किया है ठीक उसी का भोक्ता — यह देही, हर रूप धरता हुआ, तीन गुणों वाला, तीन राहों का यात्री, प्राणों का अधिपति, अपने ही कर्मों से चलता-फिरता है।

## Word by word

- **guṇa-anvayaḥ** — joined to the qualities (guṇas)
- **yaḥ** — who
- **phala-karma-kartā** — doer of acts for their fruits
- **kṛtasya tasya eva** — of just that which he has done
- **saḥ ca upabhoktā** — he is also the enjoyer
- **saḥ viśva-rūpaḥ** — he, taking every form
- **tri-guṇaḥ** — three-stranded, of the three guṇas
- **tri-vartmā** — going by three roads (commentators offer different threes)
- **prāṇa-adhipaḥ** — lord of the life-breaths
- **sañcarati** — wanders, moves on
- **sva-karmabhiḥ** — by his own actions

## Meaning

The camera turns from the Lord to us. This is the Upanishad's portrait of the jīva, the embodied self: entangled with the three guṇas, working for the fruits of its work, and — the text is precise — enjoying exactly what it has itself done. It wears form after form and travels by three roads; commentators offer different threes, dharma, adharma, and knowledge among them. This is the teaching of karma stated as doctrine, not as threat: the wandering is lawful, and its engine is our own action. The verses that follow (5.8–12) trace the mechanism. The steadying note is in the title the verse still grants: even wandering, the self remains lord of its own breaths.

## Meaning (hi)

कैमरा अब प्रभु से हटकर हम पर आता है। यह जीव का — देही आत्मा का — उपनिषद का चित्र है: तीन गुणों में उलझा, कर्मफल के लिए काम करता, और — पाठ यहाँ सटीक है — ठीक वही भोगता जो उसने स्वयं किया है। वह रूप पर रूप धरता है और तीन राहों से चलता है; व्याख्याकार अलग-अलग तीन गिनाते हैं — धर्म, अधर्म और ज्ञान उनमें हैं। यह कर्म की शिक्षा है — धमकी नहीं, सिद्धांत: भटकन नियमबद्ध है, और उसका इंजन हमारा अपना कर्म। आगे के श्लोक (5.8–12) इसी तंत्र को खोलते हैं। ढाढ़स उस उपाधि में है जो श्लोक अब भी हमें देता है: भटकता हुआ भी यह आत्मा अपने प्राणों का अधिपति है।

## Reflection

If you are the enjoyer of exactly what you do, what are you preparing to enjoy tomorrow?
