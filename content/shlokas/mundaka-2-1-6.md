## Shloka

**Devanagari:** तस्मादृचः साम यजूंषि दीक्षा / यज्ञाश्च सर्वे क्रतवो दक्षिणाश्च । / संवत्सरश्च यजमानश्च लोकाः / सोमो यत्र पवते यत्र सूर्यः ॥2.1.6॥

**Say it:** tas-maad-RI-chah SAA-ma ya-JOON-shi DEEK-shaa / yag-YAASH-cha SAR-vay KRA-ta-vo dak-shi-NAASH-cha / sam-vat-sa-RASH-cha ya-ja-MAA-nash-cha LO-kaah / SO-mo YAT-ra PA-va-tay YAT-ra SOOR-yah
**Meaning:** From him come the Rig verses, the Saman chants, the Yajus formulas, and initiation; all sacrifices, solemn rites, and the gifts to priests; the year too, and the sacrificer, and the worlds — where the moon shines purifying and where the sun shines.

## Word by word

- **tasmāt** — from him
- **ṛcaḥ** — the Rig verses
- **sāma** — the Sāman chants
- **yajūṃṣi** — the Yajus formulas
- **dīkṣā** — initiation
- **yajñāḥ ca sarve** — and all sacrifices
- **kratavaḥ** — solemn rites
- **dakṣiṇāḥ ca** — and the gifts to priests
- **saṃvatsaraḥ ca** — and the year, ritual time itself
- **yajamānaḥ ca** — and the sacrificer
- **lokāḥ** — the worlds
- **somaḥ yatra pavate** — where the moon flows bright, purifying
- **yatra sūryaḥ** — and where the sun shines

## Meaning

Now the whole apparatus of religion itself is folded into the emanation: the three Vedas, initiation, sacrifice, the priestly gift, even the calendar year and the person of the sacrificer, and the shining worlds those rites are said to win. Nothing about worship stands outside the source it worships — the ladder and the climber both come from the same ground. The Mundaka has already warned (in 1.2) that rites alone are leaky boats; this verse explains their true standing without scorn: ritual is real and derived, honourable and secondary. For practice today, let whatever form you use — prayer, chant, offering — be held as itself issued from the very one it addresses.

## Meaning (hi)

अब धर्म का पूरा तंत्र ही उस उद्गम में समेट लिया गया: तीनों वेद, दीक्षा, यज्ञ, दक्षिणा, यहाँ तक कि संवत्सर — काल की माप — और स्वयं यजमान, और वे उजले लोक भी जो अनुष्ठानों से मिलते कहे गए। उपासना का कुछ भी उस स्रोत से बाहर नहीं, जिसकी वह उपासना करती है — सीढ़ी और चढ़ने वाला, दोनों एक ही भूमि से उठे हैं। मुण्डक पहले ही (1.2 में) कह चुका है कि केवल कर्मकांड "टपकती नावें" हैं; यह श्लोक बिना उपहास के उनकी सही जगह बताता है: अनुष्ठान सच्चा भी है और व्युत्पन्न भी, आदरणीय भी और गौण भी। आज जो भी विधि अपनाओ — जप, पाठ, अर्पण — उसे उसी से निकला मानकर बरतो, जिसे वह संबोधित है।

## Reflection

Does your practice today point beyond itself, or has the ladder quietly become the destination?
