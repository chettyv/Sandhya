## Shloka

**Devanagari:** य एकोऽवर्णो बहुधा शक्तियोगाद्वर्णाननेकान्निहितार्थो दधाति । / विचैति चान्ते विश्वमादौ च देवः स नो बुद्ध्या शुभया संयुनक्तु ॥4.1॥

**Meaning:** The One, himself without color, who by the yoking of his own power deals out many colors with a purpose hidden in them; into whom the universe comes apart at the end, as it unfolded from him at the beginning — may that radiant God join us with a clear understanding.

## Word by word

- **yaḥ ekaḥ** — the One who
- **avarṇaḥ** — without color, without category
- **bahudhā** — in many ways
- **śakti-yogāt** — through the yoking of his power
- **varṇān anekān** — many colors
- **nihita-arthaḥ** — with a purpose hidden (within)
- **dadhāti** — bestows, deals out
- **vi ca eti** — and (into whom all) comes apart
- **ante** — at the end
- **viśvam** — the universe
- **ādau ca** — and at the beginning
- **devaḥ** — the radiant God
- **saḥ naḥ** — may he, us
- **buddhyā śubhayā** — with a clear, auspicious understanding
- **saṃyunaktu** — join, yoke

## Meaning

The fourth chapter opens with a prayer that carries its whole argument inside it. The supreme is avarṇa — without color, without category — yet through the yoking of his own power he pours out every color the world has, a hidden intent folded into each. The universe unfolds from him at the beginning and folds back into him at the end. Then comes the ask: may that God yoke us to a clear, auspicious buddhi. Advaita hears the colorless One as the attributeless Brahman appearing through its power; Shaiva and Vaishnava Vedanta hear a personal Lord wielding his real śakti. The prayer itself belongs to everyone: not wealth, not victory — a clean mind.

## Meaning (hi)

चौथा अध्याय एक ऐसी प्रार्थना से खुलता है जिसमें उसका पूरा तर्क समाया है। परम तत्व अवर्ण है — बिना रंग, बिना श्रेणी — फिर भी अपनी शक्ति के योग से वह संसार के सारे रंग उँडेलता है, और हर रंग में एक गूढ़ प्रयोजन रखता है। आदि में विश्व उसी से खुलता है, अंत में उसी में सिमट जाता है। और फिर माँग: वह देव हमें शुभ बुद्धि से जोड़ दे। अद्वैत इस अवर्ण एक को निर्गुण ब्रह्म सुनता है जो अपनी शक्ति से अनेक दिखता है; शैव और वैष्णव वेदांत उसे अपनी वास्तविक शक्ति धारण किए ईश्वर। पर प्रार्थना सबकी है: धन नहीं, विजय नहीं — बस एक साफ़ मन।

## Reflection

If you could ask for only one gift today — not success, but a clearer mind — what would that clarity change first?
