## Shloka

**Devanagari:** सङ्कल्पनस्पर्शनदृष्टिमोहैर्ग्रासांबुवृष्ट्यात्मविवृद्धिजन्म । / कर्मानुगान्यनुक्रमेण देही स्थानेषु रूपाण्यभिसम्प्रपद्यते ॥5.11॥

**Meaning:** Through delusions of intention, touch, and sight, and through the body's birth and growth fed by food and drink poured in like rain, the embodied self follows its own actions and takes on form after form, place after place, in due order.

## Word by word

- **saṅkalpana** — intention, imagining
- **sparśana** — touch
- **dṛṣṭi** — sight
- **mohaiḥ** — through the delusions (of these)
- **grāsa-ambu-vṛṣṭi** — the raining in of food and drink
- **ātma-vivṛddhi-janma** — the birth and growth of the embodied self
- **karma-anugāni** — following its own actions
- **anukrameṇa** — in due sequence
- **dehī** — the embodied one
- **sthāneṣu** — in place after place (body after body)
- **rūpāṇi** — forms
- **abhisamprapadyate** — takes on, arrives at

## Meaning

How does the wandering actually work? The verse gives the mechanism in one long compound: intention, touch, and sight — the inner reach, the contact, the gaze — each carrying its little delusion; then food and drink poured in like rain, growing a body around the self. Form follows action, anukrameṇa, in strict sequence; nothing in the order is arbitrary. The Upanishad presents this as sober doctrine — the physics of embodiment, not a threat. What is quietly practical is where the chain begins: not with the body but with saṅkalpana, the way intention leans. The traditions differ on much, but not on this: what you repeatedly reach for is already shaping what you will inhabit.

## Meaning (hi)

भटकन आख़िर चलती कैसे है? श्लोक पूरा तंत्र एक लंबे समास में दे देता है: संकल्प, स्पर्श और दृष्टि — भीतर की पहुँच, छूना, देखना — हरेक अपना छोटा-सा मोह लिए; फिर अन्न और जल, वर्षा की तरह भीतर उतरते हुए, आत्मा के चारों ओर देह उगाते हुए। रूप कर्म के पीछे चलता है — अनुक्रमेण, कड़े क्रम में; व्यवस्था में कुछ भी मनमाना नहीं। उपनिषद इसे संयत सिद्धांत की तरह रखता है — देह धारण का भौतिकशास्त्र, धमकी नहीं। चुपचाप काम की बात यह है कि श्रृंखला शुरू कहाँ से होती है: देह से नहीं, संकल्प से — इरादे के झुकाव से। परंपराएँ बहुत कुछ पर अलग हैं, पर इस पर नहीं: जिस ओर तुम बार-बार हाथ बढ़ाते हो, वही तय कर रहा है कि तुम कहाँ बसोगे।

## Reflection

What are you reaching for so habitually that it has begun to build where you will live?
