## Shloka

**Devanagari:** दुर्गे स्मृता हरसि भीतिमशेषजन्तोः / स्वस्थैः स्मृता मतिमतीव शुभां ददासि । / दारिद्र्यदुःखभयहारिणि का त्वदन्या / सर्वोपकारकरणाय सदाऽऽर्द्रचित्ता ॥4.17॥

**Meaning:** O Durga, remembered in distress, you take away the fear of every living being; remembered by those at ease, you grant an exceedingly clear and auspicious mind. O remover of poverty, suffering and fear — who but you has a heart always tender to do good to all?

## Word by word

- **durge** — O Durga
- **smṛtā** — when remembered
- **harasi** — you take away
- **bhītim** — fear
- **aśeṣa-jantoḥ** — of every living being, none excepted
- **svasthaiḥ smṛtā** — remembered by those who are well, at ease
- **matim atīva śubhām** — an exceedingly auspicious mind, clear judgment
- **dadāsi** — you give
- **dāridrya-duḥkha-bhaya-hāriṇi** — O remover of poverty, suffering and fear
- **kā tvat anyā** — who other than you
- **sarva-upakāra-karaṇāya** — for doing good to all
- **sadā ārdra-cittā** — (has) a heart that is always tender, always moved

## Meaning

The most-quoted verse of the chapter, and the reason many people know Durga as "she who takes away fear". Notice the two kinds of remembering. The frightened remember her and the fear goes; but the _well_ also remember her, and receive a clear and auspicious mind. The verse names the three plainest enemies — poverty, sorrow, fear — and gives her qualification for removing them: not strength but an ārdra-citta, a heart literally "wet", easily moved. Durga, the fortress-name, is explained by tenderness. Recited in Shakta, Smarta and Vaishnava households alike at Navratri, this is a verse about which the traditions have nothing to dispute.

## Meaning (hi)

अध्याय का सबसे अधिक उद्धृत श्लोक, और इसी वजह से इतने लोग दुर्गा को "भय हरने वाली" के रूप में जानते हैं। स्मरण के दो प्रकारों पर ध्यान दीजिए। भयभीत उन्हें याद करते हैं और भय चला जाता है; पर _स्वस्थ_ भी उन्हें याद करते हैं, और उन्हें मिलती है निर्मल, कल्याणकारी बुद्धि। श्लोक तीन सीधे शत्रुओं का नाम लेता है — दरिद्रता, दुःख, भय — और उन्हें हरने के लिए देवी की योग्यता बताता है: बल नहीं, आर्द्र-चित्त, शब्दशः "गीला", सहज द्रवित हृदय। दुर्गा की व्याख्या कोमलता से होती है। नवरात्रि में शाक्त, स्मार्त और वैष्णव घरों में पढ़ा जाने वाला यह ऐसा श्लोक है जिस पर परम्पराओं में कोई विवाद नहीं।

## Reflection

When you are not in trouble, do you still remember what steadies you — and what kind of mind does that give you?
