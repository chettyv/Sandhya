## Shloka

**Devanagari:** श्रवणायापि बहुभिर्यो न लभ्यः / शृण्वन्तोऽपि बहवो यं न विद्युः । / आश्चर्यो वक्ता कुशलोऽस्य लब्धा / आश्चर्यो ज्ञाता कुशलानुशिष्टः ॥1.2.7॥
**IAST:** śravaṇāyāpi bahubhiryo na labhyaḥ / śṛṇvanto'pi bahavo yaṃ na vidyuḥ । / āścaryo vaktā kuśalo'sya labdhā / āścaryo jñātā kuśalānuśiṣṭaḥ ॥1.2.7॥
**Say it:** shra-va-NAA-yaa-pi ba-hu-BHIR yo na LABH-yah / shrin-VUN-to-pi BA-ha-vo yum na vid-YOOH / aash-CHAR-yo vak-TAA ku-SHA-lo-sya lab-DHAA / aash-CHAR-yo GYAA-taa ku-sha-LAA-nu-SHISH-tah
**Meaning:** Many never even get the chance to hear of this Self; many, even hearing, do not know it. A wonder is the one who can teach it, skilled the one who attains it; a wonder the knower, taught by the skilled.
**Meaning (hi):** बहुतों को तो यह सुनने तक को नहीं मिलता; बहुत सुनकर भी इसे नहीं जान पाते। इसका कहने वाला अद्भुत है, पाने वाला कुशल; और कुशल गुरु से सीखा हुआ जानने वाला भी अद्भुत ही है।

## Word by word

- **śravaṇāya api** — even for hearing
- **bahubhiḥ** — by many
- **yaḥ na labhyaḥ** — who is not obtained (many never even hear of it)
- **śṛṇvantaḥ api** — even while hearing
- **bahavaḥ** — many
- **yam na vidyuḥ** — whom they do not come to know
- **āścaryaḥ** — a wonder, marvellous
- **vaktā** — the one who can speak of it, the teacher
- **kuśalaḥ** — skilled, fortunate
- **asya labdhā** — its attainer
- **jñātā** — the knower
- **kuśala-anuśiṣṭaḥ** — taught by one who is skilled

## Meaning

The verse is a funnel of rarity. Many never hear of the Self at all; of those who hear, many do not understand; rarer still is one who can genuinely teach it, and rare the one who, well taught, comes to know. Four roles, each a narrowing of the last: hearing, teaching, grasping, knowing. The Bhagavad Gita speaks in this same register of wonder at 2.29 — one sees it as a marvel, another speaks of it as a marvel. The point is not elitism but honesty about conditions: a speaker who knows, a listener who is ready, and the meeting of the two hardly ever line up — which is exactly why, when they do, the tradition calls it _āścarya_, a wonder.

## Meaning (hi)

यह श्लोक दुर्लभता की एक कीप है। बहुतों तक आत्मा की बात पहुँचती ही नहीं; जिन तक पहुँचती है, उनमें से बहुत समझ नहीं पाते; उससे भी दुर्लभ है वह जो इसे सचमुच सिखा सके, और वह जो ठीक से सिखाए जाने पर जान ले। चार भूमिकाएँ, हर अगली पिछली से संकरी: सुनना, कहना, पाना, जानना। भगवद्गीता 2.29 में इसी विस्मय की भाषा है — कोई इसे आश्चर्य की तरह देखता है, कोई आश्चर्य की तरह कहता है। बात विशिष्ट वर्ग की नहीं, परिस्थितियों की ईमानदारी की है: जानने वाला वक्ता, तैयार श्रोता, और दोनों की भेंट — यह संयोग विरला ही बनता है; इसीलिए परंपरा इसे 'आश्चर्य' कहती है।

## Reflection

Who in your life can actually speak of these things from experience — and when did you last truly listen to them?
