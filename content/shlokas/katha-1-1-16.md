## Shloka

**Devanagari:** तमब्रवीत् प्रीयमाणो महात्मा / वरं तवेहाद्य ददामि भूयः । / तवैव नाम्ना भविताऽयमग्निः / सृङ्कां चेमामनेकरूपां गृहाण ॥1.1.16॥

**Meaning:** Pleased with him, the great soul said: "I give you a further boon here today — this fire shall be known by your name alone. And take this many-formed garland as well."

## Word by word

- **tam** — to him
- **abravīt** — said
- **prīyamāṇaḥ** — being pleased
- **mahātmā** — the great-souled one (Yama)
- **varam** — a boon
- **tava** — to you
- **iha adya** — here, today
- **dadāmi** — I give
- **bhūyaḥ** — further, in addition
- **tava eva nāmnā** — by your name alone
- **bhavitā** — shall be (known)
- **ayam agniḥ** — this fire
- **sṛṅkām** — garland, glittering chain
- **ca imām** — and this
- **aneka-rūpām** — of many forms
- **gṛhāṇa** — accept

## Meaning

Nachiketa has just repeated the fire teaching back exactly as it was taught, and Yama is delighted. Beyond the promised boon he adds two gifts freely: the fire rite will now carry the boy's own name — the Nachiketa fire — and a many-formed garland goes with it. Teachers in every tradition have noticed what the extra gift marks: learning received attentively honours the giver, and thoroughness has a way of being answered with generosity. Shankara offers two readings of the sṛṅkā — a jewelled garland, or the many-formed path of ritual action itself — but the gesture of delight is the same in both.

## Meaning (hi)

नचिकेता ने अग्नि-विद्या ज्यों-की-त्यों दोहरा दी है, और यम प्रसन्न हैं। वचन दिए हुए वर के ऊपर वे अपनी ओर से दो उपहार जोड़ देते हैं: यह यज्ञ-अग्नि अब बालक के नाम से — नाचिकेत अग्नि — जानी जाएगी, और साथ में एक अनेक रूपों वाली माला। हर परंपरा के आचार्यों ने इस अतिरिक्त उपहार का संकेत पहचाना है: ध्यान से ग्रहण की गई विद्या देने वाले का सम्मान है, और गहराई से किया गया काम अक्सर उदारता से लौटता है। शंकराचार्य 'सृङ्का' के दो अर्थ देते हैं — रत्नों की माला, या कर्म का बहुरूपी मार्ग — पर प्रसन्नता का भाव दोनों में एक ही है।

## Reflection

What have you learned carefully enough that you could teach it back without losing anything?
