## Shloka

**Devanagari:** अग्नीर्मूर्धा चक्षुषी चन्द्रसूर्यौ / दिशः श्रोत्रे वाग् विवृताश्च वेदाः । / वायुः प्रणो हृदयं विश्वमस्य पद्भ्यां / पृथिवी ह्येष सर्वभूतान्तरात्मा ॥2.1.4॥

## Word by word

- **agnīḥ mūrdhā** — fire is his head (standard editions read agniḥ; see review note)
- **cakṣuṣī candra-sūryau** — his two eyes, the moon and sun
- **diśaḥ śrotre** — the directions, his two ears
- **vāk vivṛtāḥ ca vedāḥ** — his speech, the opened, revealed Vedas
- **vāyuḥ praṇaḥ** — air, his breath (standard editions read prāṇaḥ)
- **hṛdayaṃ viśvam asya** — the universe, his heart
- **padbhyāṃ pṛthivī** — from his two feet, the earth
- **hi eṣaḥ** — truly this one is
- **sarva-bhūta-antar-ātmā** — the inner self of all beings

## Meaning

This is the cosmic-person image: the universe described as one vast body, with fire for a head and the sun and moon for eyes. It echoes the Purusha hymn of the Rig Veda, and its work is devotional as much as philosophical — after 2.1.2 stripped away every form, this verse gives the mind a form made of everything, so that wherever you look you are looking at him. The closing phrase is the point: sarvabhūtāntarātmā, the inner self of all beings. Advaita takes "inner self" as literal identity — one Self in all — while Vishishtadvaita and Dvaita read the indwelling Lord within each distinct self; both agree that no being is empty of that presence.

## Meaning (hi)

यह विराट-पुरुष की छवि है: पूरा ब्रह्मांड एक विशाल शरीर की तरह — अग्नि सिर, सूर्य-चन्द्र आँखें। इसमें ऋग्वेद के पुरुष-सूक्त की गूँज है, और इसका काम दार्शनिक जितना ही भक्तिपूर्ण है — 2.1.2 में हर रूप हटा देने के बाद, यह श्लोक मन को सब-कुछ से बना एक रूप देता है, ताकि जहाँ भी देखो, उसी को देखो। अंतिम पद ही सार है: सर्वभूतान्तरात्मा — सब प्राणियों का भीतरी आत्मा। अद्वैत इसे शाब्दिक अभेद मानता है — सबमें एक ही आत्मा; विशिष्टाद्वैत और द्वैत हर अलग जीव के भीतर बसे अन्तर्यामी ईश्वर को पढ़ते हैं; पर दोनों मानते हैं कि कोई भी प्राणी उस उपस्थिति से ख़ाली नहीं।

## Reflection

Whose face today will you try to see as a face of the one you are looking for?
