## Shloka

**Devanagari:** य एष सुप्तेषु जागर्ति कामं कामं पुरुषो निर्मिमाणः । / तदेव शुक्रं तद्ब्रह्म तदेवामृतमुच्यते । / तस्मिँल्लोकाः श्रिताः सर्वे तदु नात्येति कश्चन । / एतद्वै तत् ॥2.2.8॥

**Say it:** ya AY-sha soop-TAY-shu JAA-gar-ti KAA-mum KAA-mum PU-ru-sho nir-mi-MAA-nah / ta-DAY-va SHOOK-rum tud-BRAH-ma ta-day-VAAM-ri-tum-OOCH-ya-tay / tus-MIL-lo-kaah shri-TAAH SAR-vay ta-du NAAT-yay-ti KASH-cha-na / ay-tud-VYE tut
**Meaning:** The person who stays awake in those who sleep, shaping desire after desire — that indeed is the Pure, that is Brahman, that alone is called the Immortal. On it all the worlds rest; none whatever goes beyond it. This indeed is That.

## Word by word

- **yaḥ eṣaḥ** — the one who
- **supteṣu** — in those who sleep
- **jāgarti** — stays awake
- **kāmam kāmam** — desire after desire
- **puruṣaḥ** — the person, the indwelling spirit
- **nirmimāṇaḥ** — fashioning, building
- **tat eva śukram** — that indeed is the Pure, the radiant
- **tat brahma** — that is Brahman
- **tat eva amṛtam ucyate** — that alone is called the Immortal
- **tasmin** — on that
- **lokāḥ śritāḥ sarve** — all the worlds rest
- **tat u na atyeti kaścana** — and none whatever passes beyond it
- **etat vai tat** — this indeed is That

## Meaning

The second half of Yama's answer: what does not travel at death. While the sleeper lies still, something in them stays awake, building dream after dream out of desire — and the Upanishad points at that night-watchman and says the unsayable: that is the Pure, that is Brahman, that is what "immortal" means. Not a distant principle, but the very awareness lighting your dreams tonight. Then the claim widens to full size: all worlds rest on it, and nothing steps beyond it. The refrain — etad vai tat — closes the loop: the answer to Nachiketa's question about death was awake inside him all along.

## Meaning (hi)

यम के उत्तर का दूसरा हिस्सा: वह, जो मृत्यु में कहीं नहीं जाता। सोने वाला निश्चल पड़ा है, पर उसमें कुछ जागता रहता है — कामना से स्वप्न-पर-स्वप्न गढ़ता हुआ — और उपनिषद उसी रात के पहरेदार की ओर इशारा करके अनकहा कह देता है: वही शुद्ध है, वही ब्रह्म है, "अमृत" का अर्थ ही वह है। कोई दूर का सिद्धान्त नहीं — वही चेतना जो आज रात तुम्हारे सपनों को रोशन करेगी। फिर दावा अपने पूरे क़द तक फैलता है: सारे लोक उसी पर टिके हैं, और उसके पार कोई नहीं जाता। टेक — एतद्वै तत् — घेरा पूरा कर देती है: नचिकेता के मृत्यु-प्रश्न का उत्तर उसके भीतर पहले से जाग रहा था।

## Reflection

Tonight, as you fall asleep, can you notice the one who will keep watch?
