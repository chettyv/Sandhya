## Shloka

**Devanagari:** दिव्यो ह्यमूर्तः पुरुषः स बाह्याभ्यन्तरो ह्यजः । / अप्राणो ह्यमनाः शुभ्रो ह्यक्षरात् परतः परः ॥2.1.2॥

<!-- review note: No transcription artifacts spotted; text matches the standard reading. This verse belongs to the sequential cosmology 2.1.2-10 (the puruṣa as source of gods, worlds, and rites) and is best read with its neighbours. -->

**IAST:** divyo hyamūrtaḥ puruṣaḥ sa bāhyābhyantaro hyajaḥ । / aprāṇo hyamanāḥ śubhro hyakṣarāt parataḥ paraḥ ॥2.1.2॥
**Say it:** div-YO hya-MOOR-tah PU-ru-shah sa baa-hyaa-BHYAN-ta-ro HYA-jah / a-PRAA-no hya-ma-NAAH SHUBH-ro hyak-sha-RAAT PA-ra-tah PA-rah
**Meaning:** Divine and formless is the Purusha; he is outside and within, unborn — without breath, without mind, radiant — higher even than the imperishable that lies beyond all things.
**Meaning (hi):** वह पुरुष दिव्य है, निराकार; बाहर भी है और भीतर भी, अजन्मा; न प्राण, न मन — शुभ्र, उज्ज्वल; और उस अक्षर से भी परे, जो स्वयं सबसे परे है।

## Word by word

- **divyaḥ** — divine, luminous
- **hi** — indeed
- **amūrtaḥ** — formless
- **puruṣaḥ** — the Purusha, the person-spirit
- **saḥ** — he
- **bāhya-abhyantaraḥ** — outside and inside at once
- **ajaḥ** — unborn
- **aprāṇaḥ** — without breath
- **amanāḥ** — without mind
- **śubhraḥ** — radiant, pure
- **akṣarāt parataḥ** — than the imperishable, which is itself beyond
- **paraḥ** — higher still

## Meaning

Having said that beings pour out of the Imperishable like sparks, the teacher now points at the source itself and removes every familiar handle. The Purusha is not a body, not a breath, not a mind — the very instruments through which we know anything are absent in it, because they arise from it. "Outside and inside" undoes location; "unborn" undoes time. Shankara's Advaita reads "beyond the imperishable" as beyond even the unmanifest seed-state of the world, while theistic Vedanta hears the supreme person who surpasses all that perishes and all that merely persists. Either way, the verse trains one reflex: whatever you can picture, the source is subtler than that.

## Meaning (hi)

चिंगारियों की उपमा देकर गुरु अब स्रोत की ओर संकेत करते हैं — और हमारी पकड़ के सारे हत्थे हटा देते हैं। वह पुरुष न शरीर है, न प्राण, न मन — जिन औज़ारों से हम कुछ भी जानते हैं, वे उसमें नहीं हैं, क्योंकि वे उसी से उपजे हैं। "बाहर भी, भीतर भी" कहकर स्थान की सीमा टूटती है; "अजन्मा" कहकर समय की। शांकर अद्वैत "अक्षर से परे" का अर्थ लेता है — जगत के अव्यक्त बीज से भी परे; भक्ति-परंपराएँ यहाँ उस परम पुरुष को सुनती हैं जो नश्वर और अविनाशी दोनों से ऊपर है। दोनों पाठ एक ही अभ्यास सिखाते हैं: मन में जो भी चित्र बने, स्रोत उससे सूक्ष्मतर है।

## Reflection

What happens to your idea of the divine if you gently set down every image you have of it?
