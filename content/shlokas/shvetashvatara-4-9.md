## Shloka

**Devanagari:** छन्दांसि यज्ञाः क्रतवो व्रतानि भूतं भव्यं यच्च वेदा वदन्ति । / अस्मान्मायी सृजते विश्वमेतत्तस्मिंश्चान्यो मायया सन्निरुद्धः ॥4.9॥

**Meaning:** The meters, the sacrifices, the rites, the vows, what has been and what shall be, and all that the Vedas declare — from this the maker of maya sends forth this universe; and within it the other one is held fast by maya.

## Word by word

- **chandāṃsi** — the meters, the hymns
- **yajñāḥ** — the sacrifices
- **kratavaḥ** — the rites
- **vratāni** — the vows
- **bhūtam bhavyam** — what has been and what shall be
- **yat ca** — and whatever
- **vedāḥ vadanti** — the Vedas declare
- **asmāt** — from this
- **māyī** — the wielder of maya
- **sṛjate** — sends forth
- **viśvam etat** — this universe
- **tasmin** — within it
- **ca anyaḥ** — and the other
- **māyayā** — by maya
- **sanniruddhaḥ** — held fast, confined

## Meaning

Everything solid gets listed — liturgy, ritual, vows, past and future, the Vedas themselves — and then the verse names their source: a māyī, a wielder of maya, streams all of it forth. Inside that outpouring sits "the other," the individual soul, held fast by the very power that made the show. Maya here need not mean illusion; in its older Vedic sense it is creative craft — a magician's real skill. Advaita reads the bound one as the Self under beginningless ignorance, freed by knowledge; the theistic schools read a real soul genuinely caught in the Lord's real power, freed by his grace. The next verse will name the magician openly — this one just makes sure you feel the net.

## Meaning (hi)

जो कुछ ठोस है, सब गिना जाता है — छंद, यज्ञ, व्रत, भूत और भव्य, स्वयं वेद — और फिर श्लोक उनके स्रोत का नाम लेता है: एक 'मायी', माया का धनी, यह सब बहा रहा है। उस प्रवाह के भीतर "दूसरा" बैठा है — जीव — उसी शक्ति में जकड़ा जिसने यह पूरा खेल रचा। यहाँ माया का अर्थ भ्रम होना ज़रूरी नहीं; पुराने वैदिक अर्थ में वह सृजन का हुनर है — जादूगर की सच्ची कला। अद्वैत बंधे हुए को अनादि अज्ञान में पड़ा आत्मा पढ़ता है, जो ज्ञान से छूटता है; भक्ति-परंपराएँ ईश्वर की सच्ची शक्ति में सचमुच उलझा जीव, जो कृपा से छूटता है। अगला श्लोक जादूगर का नाम खोलकर लेगा — यह श्लोक बस जाल का एहसास कराता है।

## Reflection

Which parts of your daily world feel so fixed, so given, that you have never asked who is streaming them forth?
