## Shloka

**Devanagari:** ॐ सह नाववतु । / सह नौ भुनक्तु । / सहवीर्यं करवावहै । / तेजस्वि नावधीतमस्तु । / मा विद्विषावहै ॥ / ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः ॥

**Say it:** om SA-ha NAA-va-va-too / SA-ha now bhoo-NUK-too / sa-ha-VEER-yum ka-ra-VAA-va-high / tay-JUS-vi naa-va-DHEE-tum-US-too / maa vid-vi-SHAA-va-high / om SHAAN-tih SHAAN-tih SHAAN-tih
**Meaning:** Om. May that One protect us both — teacher and student. May it nourish us both. May we work together with vigour; may what we study be radiant with light; may we never resent one another. Om — peace, peace, peace.

## Word by word

- **oṃ** — the sacred syllable
- **saha** — together
- **nau** — us both (teacher and student)
- **avatu** — may (that One) protect
- **bhunaktu** — may (that One) nourish, sustain
- **saha vīryam** — together, with vigour
- **karavāvahai** — may we two work, accomplish
- **tejasvi** — radiant, full of light
- **nau adhītam** — what we two have studied
- **astu** — may it be
- **mā vidviṣāvahai** — may we two never hate one another
- **śāntiḥ** — peace

## Meaning

This is a study prayer, still recited today before a lesson begins — it belongs to the Krishna Yajurveda and so opens not only the Katha Upanishad but also the Taittiriya and Shvetashvatara. Everything in it is dual: protect us _both_, nourish us _both_. Learning here is not a download but a relationship, and the prayer names its real danger — not failure to understand, but resentment between the two people trying. The threefold _śāntiḥ_ is traditionally explained as quieting three sources of disturbance: from the heavens, from other beings, and from within oneself. Said in the morning or evening, it turns any shared effort — study, work, family — into something undertaken together and protected.

## Meaning (hi)

यह अध्ययन से पहले बोली जाने वाली प्रार्थना है, जो आज भी पाठ शुरू होने से पहले पढ़ी जाती है। यह कृष्ण यजुर्वेद की है, इसलिए कठ के साथ-साथ तैत्तिरीय और श्वेताश्वतर उपनिषदों के आरंभ में भी मिलती है। इसका हर वाक्य दो लोगों के लिए है: हम _दोनों_ की रक्षा हो, हम _दोनों_ का पोषण हो। यहाँ सीखना अकेले की चीज़ नहीं, एक रिश्ता है — और प्रार्थना असली ख़तरे का नाम लेती है: समझ की कमी नहीं, बल्कि आपसी द्वेष। तीन बार 'शान्तिः' को परंपरा में तीन प्रकार की बाधाओं — दैविक, भौतिक और आंतरिक — के शमन के रूप में समझाया जाता है। सुबह या शाम कही जाए, तो यह हर साझा काम को सुरक्षित शुरुआत देती है।

## Reflection

Who is learning or working alongside you right now, and what would change if you began by praying for you both?
