## Shloka

**Devanagari:** त्रयाणां देवानां त्रिगुणजनितानां तव शिवे / भवेत् पूजा पूजा तव चरणयोर्या विरचिता । / तथा हि त्वत्पादोद्वहनमणिपीठस्य निकटे / स्थिता ह्येते शश्वन्मुकुलितकरोत्तंसमकुटाः ॥25॥

**Meaning:** O consort of Shiva, worship offered at your two feet becomes worship of all three gods born of your three qualities — and how could it not, when they stand forever beside the jewelled seat that bears your feet, their folded hands held like buds atop their crowns?

## Word by word

- **trayāṇām devānām** — of the three gods
- **tri-guṇa-janitānām** — born of the three guṇas
- **tava śive** — your, O Shivā (consort of Shiva)
- **bhavet pūjā** — becomes worship
- **pūjā tava caraṇayoḥ** — the worship of your two feet
- **yā viracitā** — which is performed
- **tathā hi** — for indeed
- **tvat-pāda-udvahana** — bearing your feet
- **maṇi-pīṭhasya nikaṭe** — near the jewelled seat
- **sthitāḥ hi ete** — these (three) stand
- **śaśvat** — forever
- **mukulita-kara** — hands folded like buds
- **uttaṃsa-makuṭāḥ** — worn as a crest upon their crowns

## Meaning

The logic is courtly and precise. Brahma, Vishnu and Rudra arise from her three guṇas — rajas, sattva, tamas — and hold the offices of creation, preservation and dissolution. But where are they? Standing beside the jewelled footstool that carries her feet, hands joined above their heads, never leaving. So anyone who bows to her feet has automatically bowed to everyone gathered around them: one act of worship at the root reaches every branch of divinity. The verse is often read as the charter of Shakta devotion — serve the source and the whole cosmos is served — a claim other Hindu traditions make for their own chosen deity in the same loving spirit.

## Meaning (hi)

तर्क दरबारी है, और सधा हुआ भी। ब्रह्मा, विष्णु और रुद्र देवी के तीन गुणों — रजस्, सत्त्व, तमस् — से जन्मे हैं और सृजन, पालन, संहार के पद सँभालते हैं। पर वे खड़े कहाँ हैं? देवी के चरण धारण करने वाले मणि-पीठ के पास, हाथ सिर के ऊपर जोड़े, कभी न हटते हुए। इसलिए जो उनके चरणों में झुका, वह अपने-आप उन सबके आगे भी झुक गया जो वहाँ जमा हैं: जड़ में की गई एक पूजा दिव्यता की हर शाखा तक पहुँच जाती है। शाक्त भक्ति इसे अपना घोषणापत्र मानती है — स्रोत की सेवा में सबकी सेवा है — और यही दावा अन्य हिन्दू परम्पराएँ अपने-अपने इष्ट के लिए उसी प्रेम-भाव से करती हैं।

## Reflection

Is your devotion scattered across many counters, or offered once at the root that feeds them all?
