## Shloka

**Devanagari:** ॐ ब्रह्मा देवानां प्रथमः संबभूव विश्वस्य कर्ता / भुवनस्य गोप्ता । / स ब्रह्मविद्यां सर्वविद्याप्रतिष्ठामथर्वाय / ज्येष्ठपुत्राय प्राह ॥1.1.1॥

**Meaning:** Om. Brahma arose first among the gods — maker of the universe, guardian of the world. He taught the knowledge of Brahman, the foundation of all knowledge, to his eldest son Atharvan.

## Word by word

- **oṃ** — the sacred syllable
- **brahmā** — Brahma, the creator god
- **devānām prathamaḥ** — first among the gods
- **saṃbabhūva** — arose, came into being
- **viśvasya kartā** — maker of the universe
- **bhuvanasya goptā** — guardian of the world
- **saḥ** — he
- **brahmavidyām** — the knowledge of Brahman
- **sarvavidyā-pratiṣṭhām** — the foundation of all knowledge
- **atharvāya** — to Atharvan
- **jyeṣṭhaputrāya** — his eldest son
- **prāha** — declared, taught

## Meaning

The Upanishad opens not with a doctrine but with a handover. Brahma — the creator god, first-born among the devas, and in the commentarial tradition carefully distinguished from brahman, the absolute this knowledge concerns — does the one thing worth doing with the highest knowledge: he gives it away, to his eldest son Atharvan. Calling brahmavidya "the foundation of all knowledge" announces the theme of the entire text — every other study rests on this one, the way all accounts rest on arithmetic. Nothing here is argued yet; what is modelled is the act itself. Wisdom in this tradition begins as inheritance, passed with the same care as a household's most fragile heirloom.

## Meaning (hi)

उपनिषद किसी सिद्धांत से नहीं, एक सौंपने की घटना से खुलता है। ब्रह्मा — सृष्टिकर्ता देव, देवताओं में प्रथम, जिन्हें भाष्य-परंपरा 'ब्रह्मन्' यानी परम तत्त्व से सावधानी से अलग रखती है — सबसे ऊँचे ज्ञान के साथ वही करते हैं जो करने योग्य है: उसे दे देते हैं, अपने ज्येष्ठ पुत्र अथर्वा को। ब्रह्मविद्या को "सर्वविद्या की प्रतिष्ठा" कहना पूरे ग्रंथ की घोषणा है — बाक़ी हर विद्या इसी पर टिकी है, जैसे सारे हिसाब गिनती पर। यहाँ अभी कोई तर्क नहीं है; दिखाया गया है सिर्फ़ वह कर्म — ज्ञान इस परंपरा में विरासत की तरह शुरू होता है, उतनी ही सँभाल के साथ आगे बढ़ाया हुआ।

## Reflection

Who handed you the most valuable thing you know, and what did it cost them to carry it to you?
