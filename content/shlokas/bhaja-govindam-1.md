## Shloka

**Devanagari:** भज गोविन्दं भज गोविन्दं / गोविन्दं भज मूढमते । / सम्प्राप्ते सन्निहिते काले / नहि नहि रक्षति डुकृङ्करणे ॥1॥

<!-- review note: No conversion artifacts spotted. डुकृङ्करणे prints here with ṅ (ङ्); the Pāṇinian dhātupāṭha entry it quotes is usually cited as डुकृञ् करणे (ḍukṛñ karaṇe, with ñ), and printed editions of this stanza split between the two spellings. Left as the source has it. -->

**IAST:** bhaja govindaṃ bhaja govindaṃ / govindaṃ bhaja mūḍhamate । / samprāpte sannihite kāle / nahi nahi rakṣati ḍukṛṅkaraṇe ॥1॥
**Say it:** BHA-ja go-VIN-dum BHA-ja go-VIN-dum / go-VIN-dum BHA-ja MOO-dha-ma-tay / sam-PRAAP-tay SAN-ni-hi-tay KAA-lay / na-hi na-hi RAK-sha-ti du-krin-KA-ra-nay
**Meaning:** Sing to Govinda, sing to Govinda, sing to Govinda, you deluded mind — when the appointed hour has drawn near, the grammar rule "ḍukṛñ karaṇe" will not save you, no, it will not.
**Meaning (hi):** गोविन्द को भजो, गोविन्द को भजो, हे मोहग्रस्त बुद्धि, गोविन्द को भजो — जब नियत घड़ी सिर पर आ पहुँचे, तब व्याकरण का सूत्र "डुकृञ् करणे" तुम्हें नहीं बचाएगा, नहीं बचाएगा।
**Source:** Bhaja Govindam 1 (attributed to Adi Shankaracharya and his disciples), sanskritdocuments.org ITRANS file, personal-study terms, https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_vishhnu/bhajagovindam.itx

## Word by word

- **bhaja** — worship, sing to, turn to
- **govindam** — Govinda, a name of Krishna/Vishnu
- **mūḍhamate** — O deluded mind
- **samprāpte** — when it has arrived
- **sannihite** — near at hand
- **kāle** — the (appointed) time
- **na hi na hi** — surely not, emphatically not
- **rakṣati** — saves, protects
- **ḍukṛñ karaṇe** — the dhātupāṭha rule for the root kṛ, "to do" — a real Pāṇinian grammar entry

## Meaning

This is the refrain of the whole poem: in traditional recitation it returns after every stanza that follows. The story holds that Shankara, walking through Kashi, heard an aged scholar drilling "ḍukṛñ karaṇe" — the genuine Pāṇinian dictionary entry for the root kṛ, "to do" — and spoke this verse on the spot. The charge is not against learning but against misplaced reliance: when death draws near, no rule of grammar will hold you up. Advaita reads Govinda as the saguṇa doorway to the one Self; devotional traditions address him as the personal Lord himself — the refrain carries both readings. Turn, it says, while there is still time.

## Meaning (hi)

यह पूरी रचना की टेक है: पारम्परिक पाठ में यह पंक्ति हर आगे आने वाले छन्द के बाद लौटती है। कथा है कि काशी की गली में शंकराचार्य ने एक वृद्ध पण्डित को "डुकृञ् करणे" रटते सुना — पाणिनीय धातुपाठ में "कृ" धातु की वास्तविक प्रविष्टि — और यह श्लोक तत्क्षण कह दिया। आक्षेप विद्या पर नहीं, ग़लत भरोसे पर है: मृत्यु निकट आए तो व्याकरण का कोई सूत्र सहारा नहीं देता। अद्वैत में गोविन्द उस एक आत्मतत्त्व का सगुण द्वार हैं; भक्ति-परम्पराएँ उन्हें साक्षात् अपना इष्ट मानकर पुकारती हैं — यह टेक दोनों पाठ साध लेती है। पुकार एक ही है: समय रहते मुड़ जाओ।
