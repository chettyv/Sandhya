## Word by word

- **yathā-vṛttam** — exactly as it had happened
- **tayoḥ** — to those two (Shiva and Vishnu)
- **tadvat** — just so, in that very way
- **mahiṣāsura-ceṣṭitam** — the doings of Mahishasura
- **tridaśāḥ** — the thirty (a collective name for the gods)
- **kathayām āsuḥ** — recounted, told
- **deva-abhibhava-vistaram** — the full extent of the gods' defeat

## Meaning

"Yathāvṛttam" — as it actually happened. The gods do not soften the report or save face; they lay out the whole extent of their own humiliation before Shiva and Vishnu. The text treats honest, unvarnished telling as the precondition for rescue: nothing in the next forty verses happens until the defeat has been fully spoken. It is a small verse with a hard discipline inside it — the strength that will soon blaze out of the assembled gods begins, unglamorously, with an accurate account of failure.

## Meaning (hi)

"यथावृत्तम्" — जैसा हुआ, वैसा ही। देवता अपनी रिपोर्ट को नरम नहीं करते, न अपनी लाज बचाते हैं; वे शिव और विष्णु के सामने अपने अपमान का पूरा विस्तार रख देते हैं। पाठ ईमानदार, बेलाग कथन को उद्धार की पूर्व-शर्त मानता है: अगले चालीस श्लोकों में कुछ भी तब तक नहीं होता जब तक हार पूरी तरह कह नहीं दी जाती। छोटे से श्लोक के भीतर एक कठोर अनुशासन है — जो तेज शीघ्र ही देवताओं में से प्रकट होने वाला है, उसकी शुरुआत असफलता के सटीक ब्यौरे से होती है।

## Reflection

Can you tell the story of your last defeat exactly as it happened, without trimming your own part in it?
