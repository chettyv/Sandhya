## Shloka

**Devanagari:** श्रीदेव्युवाच / अहं विभूत्या बहुभिरिह रूपैर्यदास्थिता । / सत्संहृतं मयैकैव तिष्ठाम्याजौ स्थिरो भव ॥10.5॥

**IAST:** śrīdevyuvāca / ahaṃ vibhūtyā bahubhiriha rūpairyadāsthitā । / satsaṃhṛtaṃ mayaikaiva tiṣṭhāmyājau sthiro bhava ॥10.5॥
**Say it:** shree-DAY-vyoo-VAA-cha / a-HUM vi-BHOOT-yaa ba-hoo-BHIR i-ha ROO-pair ya-DAAS-thi-taa / sat-sum-HRI-tum ma-YAI-kai-va tish-THAAM-yaa-jow sthi-RO BHA-va
**Meaning:** The blessed Devi said: When I stood here in many forms, that was by my own power. All that I have now withdrawn. I stand alone in battle. Be steady.

## Word by word

- **śrīdevī uvāca** — the blessed Devi said
- **aham** — I
- **vibhūtyā** — by my own power of manifestation
- **bahubhiḥ rūpaiḥ** — in many forms
- **iha** — here
- **yadā āsthitā** — when I stood
- **tat saṃhṛtam** — that has been withdrawn
- **mayā** — by me
- **ekā eva tiṣṭhāmi** — I stand alone
- **ājau** — in battle
- **sthiraḥ bhava** — be steady, stand firm

## Meaning

The pair of verses closes with a command that lands on Shumbha and on the listener alike: sthiro bhava — be steady. The Devi has answered the taunt of borrowed strength by revealing that all strength on the field was always hers; the many forms were her vibhuti, her power of self-display, and their withdrawal is as effortless as their sending forth. Shakta tradition reads this as the Devi's supreme sovereignty over manifestation; Advaita teachers hear the one reality drawing appearance back into itself; devotional readers across traditions hear something more intimate — that when everything scattered is gathered into one, what remains asks you to stop trembling and face it. The last two words are the whole teaching.

## Meaning (hi)

यह श्लोक-युग्म एक आदेश पर पूरा होता है, जो शुम्भ पर भी उतरता है और सुननेवाले पर भी: स्थिरो भव — स्थिर हो। "उधार की ताक़त" के ताने का उत्तर देवी ने यह दिखाकर दिया है कि मैदान की सारी शक्ति हमेशा उन्हीं की थी; अनेक रूप उनकी विभूति थे — आत्म-प्रकटन की सामर्थ्य — और उनका समेटना उतना ही सहज है जितना उनका भेजना। शाक्त परम्परा इसमें प्रकटन पर देवी की परम प्रभुता पढ़ती है; अद्वैती आचार्य एक सत्ता को आभास अपने में वापस खींचते सुनते हैं; और परम्पराओं भर के भक्त कुछ ज़्यादा आत्मीय सुनते हैं — कि जब बिखरा हुआ सब एक में सिमट जाए, तो जो शेष रहता है वह तुमसे काँपना छोड़कर सामना करने को कहता है। अन्तिम दो शब्द ही पूरी शिक्षा हैं।

## Reflection

What could you face steadily today if you trusted that the strength you have scattered across many supports is actually one?
