## Shloka

**Devanagari:** अरण्योर्निहितो जातवेदा गर्भ इव सुभृतो गर्भिणीभिः । / दिवे दिवे ईड्यो जागृवद्भिर्हविष्मद्भिर्मनुष्येभिरग्निः । / एतद्वै तत् ॥2.1.8॥

**Meaning:** Like a child carried with care by an expectant mother, the fire that knows all beings lies hidden in the two fire-sticks — to be honored day after day by wakeful people bearing offerings. This, truly, is That.

## Word by word

- **araṇyoḥ** — in the two fire-sticks (the kindling blocks)
- **nihitaḥ** — placed, hidden
- **jātavedāḥ** — Jātavedas, "knower of all that is born", a name of Agni
- **garbhaḥ iva** — like a child in the womb
- **subhṛtaḥ** — well borne, carried with care
- **garbhiṇībhiḥ** — by expectant mothers
- **dive dive** — day after day
- **īḍyaḥ** — to be praised, honored
- **jāgṛvadbhiḥ** — by the wakeful, the vigilant
- **haviṣmadbhiḥ** — by those bearing offerings
- **manuṣyebhiḥ** — by human beings
- **agniḥ** — Agni, the fire
- **etat vai tat** — this, truly, is That

## Meaning

The image turns homely and tender. Agni — Jatavedas, "knower of all that is born" — lies hidden in the two fire-sticks the way a child is carried, carefully and invisibly, by an expectant mother. The fire is truly there before anyone kindles it; friction only reveals what the wood was holding all along. So too the presence this whole sequence points to: hidden, whole, waiting. And the verse names the price of meeting it — jagrvadbhih, by the wakeful, day after day, bearing offerings. Not a heroic one-time search, but small, attentive, daily tending. This, truly, is That.

## Meaning (hi)

अब उपमा घरेलू और कोमल हो जाती है। अग्नि — जातवेदा, यानी 'हर जन्मे हुए को जानने वाला' — दो अरणियों (मंथन-काष्ठों) में वैसे छिपा है जैसे गर्भवती माँ अपने भीतर शिशु को सँभाले चलती है — सावधानी से, अनदेखा। आग जलाने से पहले भी लकड़ी में सचमुच मौजूद है; रगड़ केवल वही प्रकट करती है जो काठ पहले से थामे था। वैसी ही वह उपस्थिति है जिसकी ओर यह पूरा क्रम इशारा करता है: छिपी, पूर्ण, प्रतीक्षारत। और श्लोक मिलन की कीमत भी बता देता है — जागृवद्भिः: जागते हुए लोग, दिन-प्रतिदिन, हवि लेकर। कोई एक बार का वीर अभियान नहीं — छोटी, सजग, रोज़ की सेवा। यही तो वह है।

## Reflection

Which small fire of yours deserves to be tended today as carefully as an expectant mother carries her child?
