## Shloka

**Devanagari:** मन्मना भव मद्भक्तो मद्याजी मां नमस्कुरु । / मामेवैष्यसि सत्यं ते प्रतिजाने प्रियोऽसि मे ॥18.65॥
**IAST:** manmanā bhava madbhakto madyājī māṃ namaskuru । / māmevaiṣyasi satyaṃ te pratijāne priyo'si me ॥18.65॥
**Say it:** mun-ma-NAA BHA-va mud-BHUK-toh mud-YAA-jee maam na-mus-KOO-roo / maa-may-VIGH-shya-si SUT-yum tay pra-ti-JAA-nay pri-YO-si may
**Meaning:** Fix your mind on me, be devoted to me, offer worship to me, bow to me — and you will come to me. This I promise you truly, for you are dear to me.

## Word by word

- **mat-manāḥ** — with mind (fixed) on me
- **bhava** — be, become
- **mat-bhaktaḥ** — my devotee
- **mat-yājī** — one who offers worship to me
- **mām namaskuru** — bow to me
- **mām eva** — to me alone
- **eṣyasi** — you will come
- **satyam** — truly
- **te pratijāne** — I promise you
- **priyaḥ asi me** — you are dear to me

## Meaning

The first line repeats 9.34 word for word — think of me, love me, worship me, bow to me — but what is new here changes the temperature of the whole teaching. Krishna adds a personal oath: satyaṃ te pratijāne, "I promise you truly," and then a reason: "you are dear to me." The relationship the Gita has been describing is not one-directional; the devotion flows back. Devotional traditions read this verse, beside 18.66, as the heart of the Gita's final teaching. For daily practice, notice that the instruction is fourfold and small — a thought, a feeling, an act, a bow — and that it is spoken to someone already loved.

## Reflection

How would your practice feel today if you began from being already dear, rather than trying to become worthy?
