## Shloka

**Devanagari:** यो योनिं योनिमधितिष्ठत्येको विश्वानि रूपाणि योनीश्च सर्वाः । / ऋषिं प्रसूतं कपिलं यस्तमग्रे ज्ञानैर्बिभर्ति जायमानं च पश्येत् ॥5.2॥

**IAST:** yo yoniṃ yonimadhitiṣṭhatyeko viśvāni rūpāṇi yonīśca sarvāḥ । / ṛṣiṃ prasūtaṃ kapilaṃ yastamagre jñānairbibharti jāyamānaṃ ca paśyet ॥5.2॥

**Meaning:** He is the one who alone presides over every single womb, over all forms and all sources; who at the beginning bore the seer Kapila in knowledge, and watched him as he was being born.

## Word by word

- **yaḥ** — who
- **yonim yonim** — womb after womb, every single source
- **adhitiṣṭhati** — presides over
- **ekaḥ** — alone, as the one
- **viśvāni rūpāṇi** — all forms
- **yonīḥ ca sarvāḥ** — and all wombs
- **ṛṣim** — the seer
- **prasūtam** — brought forth, born
- **kapilam** — Kapila, "the tawny, golden one"
- **yaḥ tam agre** — who, him, at the beginning
- **jñānaiḥ** — with knowledges
- **bibharti** — bears, sustains, fills
- **jāyamānam** — as he was being born
- **ca paśyet** — and beheld

## Meaning

Every source of birth — every womb from which anything comes — has the same one presiding over it. Then the verse reaches back before all of them, to the very first birth: the ṛṣi Kapila, whom this God filled with knowledge and watched being born. Who is this Kapila? The readings honestly divide. Some hear the sage Kapila, founder of Sāṅkhya, honoured here as the first knower; the commentarial line through Shankara reads kapila — "the golden one" — as Hiraṇyagarbha, the first-born from whom creation unfolds. The Upanishad does not settle it, and neither should we. Either way the point stands: even the first and greatest knower was born, was watched over, and was given his knowing.

## Meaning (hi)

जन्म का हर स्रोत — हर योनि जिससे कुछ भी उपजता है — उसी एक के अधीन है। फिर श्लोक सब जन्मों से पीछे, पहले जन्म तक जाता है: ऋषि कपिल, जिन्हें इस देव ने ज्ञान से भरा और जन्म लेते हुए देखा। ये कपिल कौन हैं? पाठ ईमानदारी से बँटे हुए हैं। कुछ इन्हें सांख्य के प्रवर्तक कपिल मुनि सुनते हैं — पहले ज्ञानी के सम्मान में; शंकर की व्याख्या-परंपरा "कपिल" यानी "सुनहरे" को हिरण्यगर्भ पढ़ती है — सृष्टि का प्रथमज। उपनिषद इसे तय नहीं करता, तो हम भी नहीं करेंगे। दोनों पाठों में बात एक ही है: पहला और सबसे बड़ा ज्ञानी भी जन्मा था, देखा गया था, और उसका ज्ञान उसे दिया गया था।
