## Shloka

**Say it:** HUM-sah SHU-chi-shud va-su-RAAN-ta-rik-sha-sud / HO-taa VAY-di-shud A-ti-thir du-RO-na-sut / NRI-shud VA-ra-sud RI-ta-sud VYO-ma-sud / ub-JAA go-JAA ri-ta-JAA ad-ri-JAA RI-tum bri-HUT
**Meaning:** The swan seated in light, the Vasu in mid-air, the priest at the altar, the guest in the house; dwelling in man, in the best, in truth, in space — born of water, born of earth, born of the rite, born of the mountain — this is Rita, the vast Truth.

## Word by word

- **haṃsaḥ** — the swan (the sun; the one moving spirit)
- **śuci-ṣat** — seated in light, in the clear sky
- **vasuḥ** — the shining one, the Vasu
- **antarikṣa-sat** — seated in the mid-air
- **hotā** — the priest (the sacrificial fire)
- **vedi-ṣat** — seated on the altar
- **atithiḥ** — the guest
- **duroṇa-sat** — seated in the house
- **nṛ-ṣat** — dwelling in humans
- **vara-sat** — dwelling in the best (in the gods)
- **ṛta-sat** — dwelling in truth, in the sacred order
- **vyoma-sat** — dwelling in space
- **ab-jā** — born of water
- **go-jā** — born of earth
- **ṛta-jā** — born of the rite, of the sacred order
- **adri-jā** — born of the mountain
- **ṛtam bṛhat** — the vast Truth itself

## Meaning

The Katha borrows this riddle-hymn from the Rigveda (4.40.5) and lets one mantra do the work of a whole argument. One bright being — the swan — keeps changing its seat: the sky, the mid-air, the altar, the doorway where a guest sits; humans, gods, truth, space; born of water, earth, rite and mountain. Every station is different, the sitter is one, and its real name comes last: ṛta, the vast Truth. Advaita hears the swan as the one Self appearing in every seat, while Vishishtadvaita and Dvaita hear the Lord who indwells each dwelling while remaining himself. A beloved recitation verse — it turns the whole world into a list of places where the One is seated.

## Meaning (hi)

कठ यह पहेली-मंत्र ऋग्वेद (4.40.5) से लेता है, और यही एक मंत्र पूरे तर्क का काम कर देता है। एक ही उजला हंस अपना आसन बदलता रहता है: आकाश, अन्तरिक्ष, वेदी, घर की चौखट जहाँ अतिथि बैठता है; मनुष्य, देवता, ऋत, आकाश; जल, पृथ्वी, यज्ञ और पर्वत से जन्मा। आसन हर बार अलग है, बैठने वाला एक — और उसका असली नाम अन्त में आता है: ऋत, विराट सत्य। अद्वैत इस हंस को हर आसन पर प्रकट होती एक आत्मा के रूप में सुनता है, जबकि विशिष्टाद्वैत और द्वैत उसे हर घर में बसे ईश्वर के रूप में — जो बसकर भी स्वयं ही रहता है। पाठ का प्रिय मंत्र: सारा संसार उन जगहों की सूची बन जाता है जहाँ वह विराजमान है।

## Reflection

Which ordinary seat of your day — desk, kitchen, bus — could you treat today as one more place the swan is sitting?
