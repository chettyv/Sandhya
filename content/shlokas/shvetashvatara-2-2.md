## Shloka

**Devanagari:** युक्तेन मनसा वयं देवस्य सवितुः सवे । / सुवर्गेयाय शक्त्या ॥2.2॥

**IAST:** yuktena manasā vayaṃ devasya savituḥ save । / suvargeyāya śaktyā ॥2.2॥
**Say it:** yook-TAY-na MA-na-saa va-YAM day-VAS-ya sa-vi-TOOH sa-VAY / soo-var-gay-YAA-ya SHAK-tyaa
**Meaning:** With mind yoked, we stand in the impelling of the god Savitṛ, striving with all our strength for the passage to the heavenly light.

## Word by word

- **yuktena manasā** — with the mind yoked
- **vayam** — we
- **devasya savituḥ** — of the god Savitṛ
- **save** — in the impulse, the setting-in-motion
- **suvargeyāya** — for the way to the heavenly light (suvar, the older form of svar)
- **śaktyā** — with strength, to the limit of our power

## Meaning

Verse 2.1 showed Savitṛ yoking his own mind; this verse adds the "we." The community places itself inside the god's impulse — save, his setting-things-in-motion — and in the same breath pledges śaktyā, everything it has. Grace and effort arrive together, not as rivals but as the two hands of one act. The "heavenly light" (suvar) is old Vedic idiom for the luminous goal, which the Upanishad will go on to re-read as liberation; the Gāyatrī's Savitṛ, who "sets our thoughts in motion," is the same impeller invoked here. Theistic schools hear grace initiating and effort answering; Advaita finally sees one power moving in both — and the daily point is shared: give your whole strength, and know the momentum was never only yours.

## Meaning (hi)

श्लोक 2.1 में सविता ने अपना मन जोड़ा था; इस श्लोक में "हम" जुड़ते हैं। समुदाय स्वयं को देवता की प्रेरणा — "सव", उसकी गति देने वाली शक्ति — के भीतर रखता है, और उसी साँस में "शक्त्या" कहता है: अपना पूरा बल भी लगाएँगे। कृपा और पुरुषार्थ यहाँ प्रतिद्वंद्वी नहीं, एक ही कर्म के दो हाथ हैं। "सुवर्" — स्वर्गिक प्रकाश — उस उजले लक्ष्य का पुराना वैदिक नाम है, जिसे उपनिषद आगे मुक्ति के रूप में पढ़ेगा; और गायत्री के सविता, जो "हमारी बुद्धि को प्रेरित करें", यहाँ भी वही प्रेरक हैं। भक्ति-प्रधान परंपराओं में कृपा पहल करती है और पुरुषार्थ उत्तर देता है; अद्वैत में अंततः दोनों में एक ही शक्ति चलती है — दैनिक सीख साझा है: पूरा बल दो, और जानो कि गति अकेले तुम्हारी नहीं थी।

## Reflection

Where in today's effort could you give your full strength while trusting that the momentum is not yours alone?
