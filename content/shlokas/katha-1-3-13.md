## Shloka

**Devanagari:** यच्छेद्वाङ्मनसी प्राज्ञस्तद्यच्छेज्ज्ञान आत्मनि । / ज्ञानमात्मनि महति नियच्छेत्तद्यच्छेच्छान्त आत्मनि ॥1.3.13॥

<!-- review note: "वाङ्मनसी" (manasī) is the received reading in printed editions — a Vedic locative form, glossed by the commentators as "speech into the mind"; not an artifact. -->

**IAST:** yacchedvāṅmanasī prājñastadyacchejjñāna ātmani । / jñānamātmani mahati niyacchettadyacchecchānta ātmani ॥1.3.13॥
**Say it:** yach-CHAYD VAANG ma-na-SEE PRAAG-yas tad-yach-CHAYJ GYAA-na AAT-ma-ni / GYAA-nam AAT-ma-ni ma-HA-ti ni-yach-CHAYT tad-yach-CHAYCH CHAAN-ta AAT-ma-ni
**Meaning:** Let the wise one merge speech into mind, mind into the knowing self, that knowing into the great Self, and the great into the Self of peace.
**Meaning (hi):** बुद्धिमान वाणी को मन में समेटे, मन को ज्ञानरूप आत्मा में, उस ज्ञान को महान आत्मा में, और महान को शांत आत्मा में विलीन कर दे।

## Word by word

- **yacchet** — let him rein in, merge
- **vāk** — speech
- **manasī** — into the mind (Vedic locative)
- **prājñaḥ** — the discerning one, the wise
- **tat** — that (the mind)
- **yacchet** — let him merge
- **jñāne ātmani** — into the knowing self, the intellect
- **jñānam** — that knowing
- **ātmani mahati** — into the great self
- **niyacchet** — let him settle, restrain
- **tat** — that
- **yacchet** — let him merge
- **śānte ātmani** — into the peaceful Self

## Meaning

A complete meditation instruction in one verse — the practical answer to "how?". The discerning person merges speech, the outermost activity, into mind; mind into the "knowing self", the intellect; the intellect into the "great self"; and that into the "peaceful Self", the still centre this whole valli has been climbing toward. Each step is yaccha — a gentle reining-in, the chariot's own verb — not suppression. It is interiorization by stages: the coarser settles into the subtler, and the practitioner does at the level of attention exactly what the previous two verses mapped at the level of reality.

## Meaning (hi)

एक ही श्लोक में पूरी ध्यान-विधि — और "कैसे?" का व्यावहारिक उत्तर। विवेकी व्यक्ति वाणी को — सबसे बाहरी गतिविधि को — मन में समेटे; मन को "ज्ञान आत्मा" यानी बुद्धि में; बुद्धि को "महान आत्मा" में; और उसे "शांत आत्मा" में — उस स्थिर केंद्र में जिसकी ओर पूरी वल्ली चढ़ती आई है। हर चरण "यच्छ" है — रथ की अपनी क्रिया, लगाम की कोमल खिंचाई — दमन नहीं। यह चरणों में अंतर्मुख होना है: स्थूल सूक्ष्म में ठहरता जाता है, और साधक ध्यान के स्तर पर ठीक वही करता है जो पिछले दो श्लोकों ने सत्ता के स्तर पर नक्शे की तरह खींचा था।

## Reflection

Which outermost layer — speech, perhaps — could you quiet first tonight?
