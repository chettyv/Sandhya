## Shloka

**Devanagari:** न संदृशे तिष्ठति रूपमस्य न चक्षुषा पश्यति कश्चनैनम् । / हृदा हृदिस्थं मनसा य एनमेवं विदुरमृतास्ते भवन्ति ॥4.20॥

**IAST:** na saṃdṛśe tiṣṭhati rūpamasya na cakṣuṣā paśyati kaścanainam । / hṛdā hṛdisthaṃ manasā ya enamevaṃ viduramṛtāste bhavanti ॥4.20॥

**Meaning:** His form does not stand within sight; no one sees him with the eye. Those who know him with the heart and the mind, as dwelling in the heart — they become immortal.

## Word by word

- **na saṃdṛśe** — not within sight
- **tiṣṭhati** — stands
- **rūpam asya** — his form
- **na cakṣuṣā** — not with the eye
- **paśyati kaścana** — does anyone see
- **enam** — him
- **hṛdā** — with the heart
- **hṛdi-stham** — dwelling in the heart
- **manasā** — with the mind
- **ye enam evam viduḥ** — those who know him thus
- **amṛtāḥ te bhavanti** — they become immortal

## Meaning

The companion of 4.19 turns refusal into invitation. His form does not stand in the field of sight; no eye, however trained, will ever deliver him. But the verse does not leave you in the dark — it hands you a different organ. Hṛdā: with the heart. Hṛdistham: for that is where he dwells. What the eye cannot reach lives closer than the eye. Knowing him there, heart and mind together, makes one amṛta — deathless. Advaita reads this as the Self knowing itself, which can never become an object; the devotional schools read the indwelling Lord who chooses the heart over the retina. Either way the search turns inward and softens: you stop hunting an image and start keeping company.

## Meaning (hi)

4.19 का साथी श्लोक इनकार को निमंत्रण बना देता है। उसका रूप दृष्टि के दायरे में नहीं ठहरता; कोई आँख, कितनी भी सधी हो, उसे नहीं दिखा पाएगी। पर श्लोक अँधेरे में नहीं छोड़ता — वह दूसरा ही उपकरण थमा देता है। 'हृदा': हृदय से। 'हृदिस्थम्': क्योंकि वह वहीं रहता है। जो आँख की पहुँच से बाहर है, वह आँख से भी निकट बसा है। उसे वहाँ जानना — हृदय और मन साथ लेकर — मनुष्य को अमृत करता है। अद्वैत में यह आत्मा का स्वयं को जानना है, जो कभी विषय नहीं बनता; भक्ति-परंपराओं में वह अंतर्यामी जो दृष्टि की जगह हृदय चुनता है। दोनों में खोज भीतर मुड़कर कोमल हो जाती है: तुम छवि का पीछा छोड़कर संगति करने लगते हो।

## Reflection

What if, instead of trying to picture God today, you simply sat with the sense that something kind is already seated in your chest?
