## Shloka

**Devanagari:** सरस्वत्याः सूक्तीरमृतलहरीकौशलहरीः / पिबन्त्याः शर्वाणि श्रवणचुलुकाभ्यामविरलम् । / चमत्कारश्लाघाचलितशिरसः कुण्डलगणो / झणत्कारैस्तारैः प्रतिवचनमाचष्ट इव ते ॥60॥

**Meaning:** O Sharvani — as you drink in Sarasvati's eloquent sayings without pause, using your two ears for cupped palms — sayings that steal the charm of waves of nectar — your head sways in wondering praise, and the cluster of your earrings, with its clear high ringing, seems to be speaking your reply.

## Word by word

- **sarasvatyāḥ sūktīḥ** — the eloquent sayings of Sarasvati
- **amṛta-laharī-kauśala-harīḥ** — which steal the charm of waves of nectar
- **pibantyāḥ** — of (you) who drink
- **śarvāṇi** — O Sharvani (consort of Sharva, Shiva)
- **śravaṇa-culukābhyām** — with the two cupped palms of your ears
- **aviralam** — without pause
- **camatkāra-ślāghā-calita-śirasaḥ** — (of you) whose head sways in praise of their brilliance
- **kuṇḍala-gaṇaḥ** — the cluster of earrings
- **jhaṇatkāraiḥ tāraiḥ** — with clear, high-ringing sounds
- **prativacanam** — the reply
- **ācaṣṭe iva te** — seems to speak, on your behalf

## Meaning

A scene from the divine court: Sarasvati, goddess of speech, is reciting, and the Devi listens. Listening is drawn as drinking — ears cupped like palms at a stream, taking in words sweeter than nectar without pause. Then the detail that makes the verse: moved by a fine phrase, the Devi's head sways in appreciation, her earrings swing and chime — and their bright ringing sounds like her answer. The Mother of the universe is shown doing something rare among the powerful: giving complete, delighted attention while another speaks, and replying only with the music of her pleasure. Even Sarasvati's eloquence, the poem hints, finds its fulfilment in being heard by her.

## Meaning (hi)

देव-सभा का एक दृश्य: वाणी की देवी सरस्वती सुना रही हैं, और देवी सुन रही हैं। सुनने को पीने की तरह खींचा गया है — जैसे धारा पर अंजुलि, वैसे कान; अमृत से मीठे वचन बिना रुके भीतर उतरते जाते हैं। फिर वह ब्योरा जो श्लोक को बना देता है: किसी सुन्दर पंक्ति पर देवी का सिर सराहना में हिल उठता है, कुण्डल झूलकर खनक जाते हैं — और उनकी उजली झंकार उत्तर-सी सुनाई देती है। जगन्माता को वह करते दिखाया गया है जो समर्थों में दुर्लभ है: दूसरे के बोलते समय पूरा, प्रसन्न ध्यान देना, और उत्तर में केवल अपने आनन्द का संगीत। संकेत यह भी है — सरस्वती की वाणी भी अपनी सार्थकता देवी के सुनने में पाती है।

## Reflection

When you listen today, can you give the speaker the kind of attention that itself feels like a reply?
