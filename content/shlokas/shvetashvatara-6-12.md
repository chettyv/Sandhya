## Shloka

**Devanagari:** एको वशी निष्क्रियाणां बहूनामेकं बीजं बहुधा यः करोति । / तमात्मस्थं येऽनुपश्यन्ति धीरास्तेषां सुखं शाश्वतं नेतरेषाम् ॥6.12॥

**Meaning:** The one ruler of the many who do not act of themselves, who makes the one seed manifold — the wise who see him abiding in themselves, theirs is lasting happiness; it belongs to no one else.

## Word by word

- **ekaḥ vaśī** — the one ruler
- **niṣkriyāṇām bahūnām** — of the many who do not act of themselves
- **ekam bījam** — the one seed
- **bahudhā yaḥ karoti** — who makes manifold
- **tam ātma-stham** — him, abiding in oneself
- **ye anupaśyanti** — who see, who keep seeing
- **dhīrāḥ** — the wise, the steady
- **teṣām** — theirs
- **sukham śāśvatam** — lasting happiness
- **na itareṣām** — not others'

## Meaning

A near-twin of Katha 2.2.12, with its own first line. Where the Katha said "one form made many", this verse says "one seed" — bījam — and adds niṣkriyāṇām: the many do not act of themselves. Everything that moves borrows its motion; the one ruler lends it. The second half is identical to the Katha's, and its promise is the same: the dhīrās, the steadfast, who keep seeing him in themselves — anupaśyanti, continuously, not a single glimpse — hold happiness that lasts. How one seed becomes a forest is where the schools part: appearance through māyā for Advaita, real unfolding under a real Lord for Vishishtadvaita and Dvaita — but the last line's promise is common ground.

## Meaning (hi)

कठ 2.2.12 का लगभग जुड़वाँ, अपनी पहली पंक्ति के साथ। कठ ने कहा था 'एक रूप अनेक हुआ'; यहाँ 'एक बीज' है — बीजम् — और एक नया शब्द: निष्क्रियाणाम् — अनेक स्वयं क्रिया नहीं करते। जो कुछ हिलता है, उधार की गति से हिलता है; देने वाला वह एक वशी है। उत्तरार्ध कठ जैसा ही है, और वादा भी वही: जो धीर उसे अपने भीतर लगातार देखते हैं — अनुपश्यन्ति, एक झलक नहीं — टिकने वाला सुख उन्हीं का है। एक बीज वन कैसे बनता है, यहीं धाराएँ बँटती हैं — अद्वैत में माया से आभास, विशिष्टाद्वैत और द्वैत में सच्चे ईश्वर के अधीन सच्चा विस्तार — पर अंतिम पंक्ति का वादा साझा है।

## Reflection

What motion in you today — effort, love, attention — could you trace back to its lender?
