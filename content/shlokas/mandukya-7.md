## Shloka

**Devanagari:** नान्तःप्रज्ञं न बहिष्प्रज्ञं नोभयतःप्रज्ञं न प्रज्ञानघनं न प्रज्ञं नाप्रज्ञम् । / अदृष्टमव्यवहार्यमग्राह्यमलक्षणं अचिन्त्यमव्यपदेश्यमेकात्मप्रत्ययासारं प्रपञ्चोपशमं शान्तं शिवमद्वैतं चतुर्थं मन्यन्ते स आत्मा स विज्ञेयः ॥7॥

**IAST:** nāntaḥprajñaṃ na bahiṣprajñaṃ nobhayataḥprajñaṃ na prajñānaghanaṃ na prajñaṃ nāprajñam । / adṛṣṭamavyavahāryamagrāhyamalakṣaṇaṃ acintyamavyapadeśyamekātmapratyayāsāraṃ prapañcopaśamaṃ śāntaṃ śivamadvaitaṃ caturthaṃ manyante sa ātmā sa vijñeyaḥ ॥7॥
**Say it:** NAAN-tah-PRAG-yum na ba-hish-PRAG-yum NO-bha-ya-tah-PRAG-yum na prag-YAA-na-gha-num na PRAG-yum NAA-prag-yum / a-DRISH-tum av-ya-va-HAAR-yum a-GRAAH-yum a-LUK-sha-num a-CHIN-tyum av-ya-pa-DAYSH-yum ay-KAAT-ma-PRUT-ya-YAA-saa-rum pra-pun-CHO-pa-sha-mum SHAAN-tum shi-VUM ad-VY-tum cha-toor-THUM mun-YUN-tay sa AAT-maa sa vig-YAY-yah
**Meaning:** Not inwardly knowing, not outwardly knowing, not knowing both ways; not a mass of knowing, not knowing, not unknowing. Unseen, beyond all dealings, ungraspable, without mark, unthinkable, unnameable; its essence the certainty of the one Self; the stilling of all appearance — peaceful, auspicious, non-dual: that, the wise hold, is the fourth. That is the Self; that is to be known.
**Meaning (hi):** जो न भीतर जानता है, न बाहर, न दोनों ओर; जो न प्रज्ञान-पुंज है, न ज्ञाता, न अज्ञ। अदृष्ट, व्यवहार से परे, अग्राह्य, लक्षण-रहित, अचिन्त्य, अकथनीय; एक आत्मा की प्रतीति ही जिसका सार है; जहाँ सारा प्रपंच थम जाता है — शान्त, शिव, अद्वैत: उसे ही ज्ञानी चौथा मानते हैं। वही आत्मा है; वही जानने योग्य है।

## Word by word

- **na antaḥ-prajñam** — not inwardly knowing
- **na bahiḥ-prajñam** — not outwardly knowing
- **na ubhayataḥ-prajñam** — not knowing both ways
- **na prajñāna-ghanam** — not a mass of knowing
- **na prajñam** — not knowing
- **na aprajñam** — not unknowing
- **adṛṣṭam** — unseen
- **avyavahāryam** — beyond all dealings
- **agrāhyam** — ungraspable
- **alakṣaṇam** — without mark
- **acintyam** — unthinkable
- **avyapadeśyam** — unnameable
- **eka-ātma-pratyaya-sāram** — its essence the certainty of the one Self
- **prapañca-upaśamam** — the stilling of appearance
- **śāntam** — peaceful
- **śivam** — auspicious
- **advaitam** — non-dual
- **caturtham manyante** — they hold to be the fourth
- **saḥ ātmā** — that is the Self
- **saḥ vijñeyaḥ** — that is to be known

## Meaning

The landmark sentence of the Upanishad, and it works entirely by subtraction. Each state examined so far is denied in turn — not waking's outward knowing, not dream's inward knowing, not sleep's massed seed of knowing — until even "knowing" and "not-knowing" are set down. What remains cannot be seen, grasped, marked, thought, or named; it can only be, traced as the single thread of "I" running through every state. Over it the text breathes its three quietest words: peaceful, auspicious, non-dual. Advaita, through Gaudapada and Shankara, hears turiya as the sole reality in which appearance ceases; other Vedantic schools hear the self's highest condition, with the world's reality intact. Less a fourth state than the ground of the other three.

## Meaning (hi)

यह उपनिषद का शिखर-वाक्य है, और यह पूरी तरह घटाव से काम करता है। अब तक जाँची हर अवस्था बारी-बारी नकारी जाती है — न जागरण का बाहरी जानना, न स्वप्न का भीतरी, न नींद का बीज-सा घना जानना — यहाँ तक कि 'जानना' और 'न जानना' भी रख दिए जाते हैं। जो शेष रहता है, वह न देखा जा सकता है, न पकड़ा, न सोचा, न कहा; वह बस है — हर अवस्था में से गुज़रता 'मैं' का एक ही धागा। उस पर ग्रंथ अपने तीन सबसे शान्त शब्द छोड़ता है: शान्त, शिव, अद्वैत। अद्वैत परंपरा — गौड़पाद और शंकर से होकर — तुरीय को एकमात्र सत्य सुनती है जिसमें प्रपंच थम जाता है; अन्य वेदान्ती परंपराएँ इसे आत्मा की सर्वोच्च दशा मानती हैं, जगत की सत्ता सहित। यह चौथी अवस्था कम, बाकी तीनों की आधारभूमि अधिक है।

## Reflection

Can you rest for one breath today as the one who watches waking, dreaming, and sleeping come and go?
