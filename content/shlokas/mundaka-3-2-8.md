## Shloka

**Devanagari:** यथा नद्यः स्यन्दमानाः समुद्रेऽ / स्तं गच्छन्ति नामरूपे विहाय । / तथा विद्वान् नामरूपाद्विमुक्तः / परात्परं पुरुषमुपैति दिव्यम् ॥3.2.8॥

**IAST:** yathā nadyaḥ syandamānāḥ samudre' / staṃ gacchanti nāmarūpe vihāya । / tathā vidvān nāmarūpādvimuktaḥ / parātparaṃ puruṣamupaiti divyam ॥3.2.8॥
**Say it:** ya-THAA NAD-yah syan-da-MAA-naah sa-mud-RAY AS-tum / gach-CHAN-ti naa-ma-ROO-pay vi-HAA-ya / ta-THAA vid-VAAN naa-ma-roo-PAAD vi-MUK-tah / pa-RAAT-pa-rum PU-ru-shum u-PAI-ti DIV-yum
**Meaning:** As flowing rivers disappear into the sea, leaving name and form behind, so the knower, freed from name and form, reaches the divine Person beyond the beyond.

## Word by word

- **yathā** — as
- **nadyaḥ** — rivers
- **syandamānāḥ** — flowing on
- **samudre** — in the sea
- **astam gacchanti** — come to rest, disappear home (as the sun "sets")
- **nāma-rūpe** — name and form (the two of them)
- **vihāya** — leaving behind
- **tathā** — so
- **vidvān** — the knower
- **nāma-rūpāt** — from name and form
- **vimuktaḥ** — fully freed
- **parāt param** — beyond the beyond, higher than the highest
- **puruṣam** — the Person
- **upaiti** — reaches, approaches
- **divyam** — divine

## Meaning

Perhaps the most beloved image in the Upanishads. A river is mostly its banks: Ganga is Ganga because of where her edges run. At the sea the banks end, and what was never really separate stops carrying a separate name. Astam gacchanti — the rivers "set" the way the sun sets: not destroyed, gone home. What gets left behind is exactly two things, name and form — the label and the outline that made a someone. Advaita hears that the water was always ocean; the devotional schools hear the soul reaching the divine Person and resting in him, distinct as a river's water yet held entirely. Notice the direction of the loss: nothing real is lost — only the outline.

## Meaning (hi)

शायद उपनिषदों की सबसे प्रिय उपमा। नदी दरअसल अपने किनारों का नाम है: गंगा गंगा है क्योंकि उसके किनारे कहीं ख़ास बहते हैं। समुद्र पर किनारे ख़त्म हो जाते हैं, और जो कभी सचमुच अलग था ही नहीं, वह अलग नाम ढोना बंद कर देता है। 'अस्तं गच्छन्ति' — नदियाँ वैसे ही 'अस्त' होती हैं जैसे सूरज: नष्ट नहीं, घर पहुँची हुईं। पीछे छूटती हैं ठीक दो चीज़ें — नाम और रूप — वह लेबल और वह रेखा जिनसे 'कोई' बनता था। अद्वैत सुनता है कि पानी हमेशा से सागर ही था; भक्ति-परंपराएँ सुनती हैं कि आत्मा दिव्य पुरुष तक पहुँचकर उसमें विश्राम पाती है — नदी के जल-सी भिन्न, फिर भी पूरी तरह उसी में समाई। खोने की दिशा देखो: असली कुछ नहीं खोता — सिर्फ़ रेखा।

## Reflection

If your name and your outline were set down for one minute, what would still be flowing?
