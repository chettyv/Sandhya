## Shloka

**Devanagari:** संयुक्तमेतत्क्षरमक्षरं च व्यक्ताव्यक्तं भरते विश्वमीशः । / अनीशश्चात्मा बध्यते भोक्तृभावाज्ज्ञात्वा देवं मुच्यते सर्वपाशैः ॥1.8॥

**Meaning:** The Lord carries all this, joined together — the perishable and the imperishable, the manifest and the unmanifest. But the self, no lord, is bound, because it takes the enjoyer's stance; when it knows the God, it is released from every fetter.

## Word by word

- **saṃyuktam** — joined together
- **etat** — this
- **kṣaram** — the perishable
- **akṣaram ca** — and the imperishable
- **vyakta-avyaktam** — the manifest and the unmanifest
- **bharate** — carries, supports
- **viśvam** — the whole, the universe
- **īśaḥ** — the Lord
- **anīśaḥ ca ātmā** — but the self, which is not lord
- **badhyate** — is bound
- **bhoktṛ-bhāvāt** — because of its stance as enjoyer
- **jñātvā devam** — having known the God
- **mucyate** — is released
- **sarva-pāśaiḥ** — from all fetters

## Meaning

Here the Upanishad states its refrain for the first time: jñātvā devaṃ mucyate sarvapāśaiḥ — knowing God, one is released from every fetter. The diagnosis that precedes it is precise. The world does not bind you; your stance toward it does — bhoktṛbhāva, standing before everything as its consumer. And the word for fetter, pāśa, is no accident of vocabulary: Lord, soul, and bond (pati, paśu, pāśa) became the master-grammar of Shaiva theology, which reveres this text, while Advaita reads the fetters as ignorance to be dissolved and Vishishtadvaita as bonds the Lord himself unties. The cure is not effortful escape but acquaintance — the fetters loosen upon knowing him, the way fear of a rope loosens upon seeing it clearly.

## Meaning (hi)

यहाँ उपनिषद पहली बार अपनी टेक कहता है: ज्ञात्वा देवं मुच्यते सर्वपाशैः — देव को जानकर मनुष्य हर पाश से छूट जाता है। इससे पहले का निदान बहुत सटीक है। संसार आपको नहीं बाँधता; उसके सामने आपका रुख़ बाँधता है — भोक्तृभाव, हर चीज़ के आगे उपभोक्ता बनकर खड़े होना। और बंधन के लिए शब्द 'पाश' संयोग नहीं है: पति, पशु, पाश — यही त्रयी आगे चलकर शैव दर्शन की मूल व्याकरण बनी, जो इस ग्रंथ को अपना मानता है; जबकि अद्वैत पाशों को अज्ञान पढ़ता है जो ज्ञान से घुल जाता है, और विशिष्टाद्वैत उन्हें बंधन मानता है जिन्हें प्रभु स्वयं खोलते हैं। उपाय ज़ोर लगाकर भागना नहीं, पहचान है — उसे जान लेने पर पाश वैसे ही ढीले पड़ जाते हैं जैसे रस्सी को साफ़ देख लेने पर उसका डर।

## Reflection

Which knot in your life is really just the habit of standing before the world as its consumer?
