## Shloka

**Devanagari:** गतास्ते मञ्चत्वं द्रुहिणहरिरुद्रेश्वरभृतः / शिवः स्वच्छच्छायाघटितकपटप्रच्छदपटः । / त्वदीयानां भासां प्रतिफलनरागारुणतया / शरीरी शृङ्गारो रस इव दृशां दोग्धि कुतुकम् ॥92॥

**Meaning:** Druhina, Hari, Rudra and Ishvara, bearing you up, have become your couch; Shiva is its coverlet, feigned out of his own clear white radiance — and, flushed red by the reflection of your glow, he yields wonder to the eyes, as though love's own rasa had taken a body.

## Word by word

- **gatāḥ te mañcatvam** — have become your couch
- **druhiṇa-hari-rudra-īśvara-bhṛtaḥ** — Druhina (Brahma), Hari, Rudra and Ishvara as its bearers
- **śivaḥ** — Shiva (Sadashiva)
- **svaccha-cchāyā-ghaṭita-kapaṭa-pracchada-paṭaḥ** — a seeming coverlet fashioned of his own clear lustre
- **tvadīyānām bhāsām** — of your radiances
- **pratiphalana-rāga-aruṇatayā** — through the redness caught from their reflection
- **śarīrī** — embodied
- **śṛṅgāraḥ rasaḥ iva** — like the rasa of love itself
- **dṛśām** — for the eyes
- **dogdhi** — milks, yields
- **kutukam** — wonder, delight

## Meaning

In Shri Vidya contemplation the Devi is enthroned on a couch whose four legs are Brahma, Vishnu, Rudra and Ishvara, with Sadashiva as its board and cover — the five cosmic functions of creation, preservation, dissolution, concealment and grace pictured, audaciously, as her furniture. The powers that run the universe are her household objects. Then the colour turns: white Sadashiva blushes rose in the reflection of her radiance, love embodied. Shakta readers hear in this Shakti's primacy over even the highest Shiva, while Advaita-minded readers hear pure consciousness taking on the hue of its own power — the image genuinely carries both, and the poem does not choose.

## Meaning (hi)

श्रीविद्या की भावना में देवी उस पर्यंक पर विराजती हैं जिसके चार पाये ब्रह्मा, विष्णु, रुद्र और ईश्वर हैं, और सदाशिव उसका फलक और आच्छादन — सृष्टि, स्थिति, संहार, तिरोधान और अनुग्रह, ये पाँच जगत्-कार्य साहसपूर्वक उनके घर का सामान बनाकर चित्रित हैं। जो शक्तियाँ ब्रह्मांड चलाती हैं, वे देवी की गृह-सामग्री हैं। फिर रंग बदलता है: श्वेत सदाशिव देवी की कान्ति के प्रतिबिम्ब से गुलाबी हो उठते हैं — मानो मूर्तिमान प्रेम। शाक्त पाठक इसमें परम शिव पर भी शक्ति की प्रधानता सुनते हैं, जबकि अद्वैत-रुचि वाले पाठक शुद्ध चैतन्य का अपनी ही शक्ति के रंग में रँग जाना — छवि सचमुच दोनों को साधती है, और काव्य कोई निर्णय नहीं देता।

## Reflection

If the powers you most respect turned out to be furniture in the presence of something greater, how would your fears rearrange themselves?
