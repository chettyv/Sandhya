## Shloka

**Devanagari:** तस्माच्च देवा बहुधा संप्रसूताः / साध्या मनुष्याः पशवो वयांसि । / प्राणापानौ व्रीहियवौ तपश्च / श्रद्ध सत्यं ब्रह्मचर्यं विधिश्च ॥2.1.7॥

**IAST:** tasmācca devā bahudhā saṃprasūtāḥ / sādhyā manuṣyāḥ paśavo vayāṃsi । / prāṇāpānau vrīhiyavau tapaśca / śraddha satyaṃ brahmacaryaṃ vidhiśca ॥2.1.7॥
**Say it:** tas-MAACH-cha DAY-vaa ba-hu-DHAA sam-pra-SOO-taah / SAADH-yaa ma-NUSH-yaah PA-sha-vo va-YAAN-si / praa-naa-PAA-now vree-hi-ya-VOW TA-pash-cha / SHRAD-dha SAT-yum brah-ma-CHAR-yum vi-DHISH-cha
**Meaning:** And from him the gods are begotten, in their many orders; the celestial Sadhyas, humans, beasts, and birds; the in-breath and the out-breath, rice and barley, austerity, faith, truth, the disciplined life, and sacred rule.
**Meaning (hi):** और उसी से देवता अनेक रूपों में उत्पन्न हुए; साध्यगण, मनुष्य, पशु और पक्षी; प्राण और अपान, धान और जौ, तप, श्रद्धा, सत्य, ब्रह्मचर्य और विधि — सब उसी से।

## Word by word

- **tasmāt ca** — and from him
- **devāḥ** — the gods
- **bahudhā** — in many ways, many orders
- **saṃprasūtāḥ** — are begotten
- **sādhyāḥ** — the Sādhyas, an old class of celestial beings
- **manuṣyāḥ** — humans
- **paśavaḥ** — animals
- **vayāṃsi** — birds
- **prāṇa-apānau** — the in-breath and out-breath
- **vrīhi-yavau** — rice and barley, the offering grains
- **tapaḥ ca** — and austerity
- **śraddhā** — faith (printed here as śraddha; see review note)
- **satyam** — truth
- **brahmacaryam** — the disciplined, student's life
- **vidhiḥ ca** — and sacred rule, right procedure

## Meaning

The list keeps widening until it takes in things we usually file under "human effort": not just gods, people, animals and birds, not just breath and the grains offered in sacrifice, but tapas, faith, truth, brahmacharya, right procedure. The virtues themselves are begotten of the Purusha. That quietly corrects a common self-image — as if discipline were something we manufacture against the grain of the universe. In this verse, the capacity to be faithful, truthful and restrained is part of the original endowment, as natural as barley and birds. Practice, then, is less like building something from nothing and more like letting an inherited seed have its season.

## Meaning (hi)

सूची फैलती जाती है, यहाँ तक कि उसमें वे चीज़ें भी आ जाती हैं जिन्हें हम "मनुष्य का पुरुषार्थ" मान बैठते हैं: देवता, मनुष्य, पशु-पक्षी और यज्ञ के धान-जौ ही नहीं — तप, श्रद्धा, सत्य, ब्रह्मचर्य और विधि भी। गुण स्वयं पुरुष से जन्मे हैं। यह चुपचाप हमारी एक आम धारणा सुधारता है — मानो अनुशासन कोई चीज़ है जिसे हम सृष्टि की धारा के विरुद्ध गढ़ते हैं। इस श्लोक में श्रद्धावान, सत्यनिष्ठ और संयमी होने की क्षमता मूल विरासत का हिस्सा है — उतनी ही सहज जितने जौ और पक्षी। तब अभ्यास शून्य से कुछ खड़ा करना नहीं, बल्कि मिले हुए बीज को उसकी ऋतु देना है।

## Reflection

Which quality you strain after might grow easier if you treated it as already planted in you?
