## Shloka

**Devanagari:** यस्य देवे परा भक्तिः यथा देवे तथा गुरौ । / तस्यैते कथिता ह्यर्थाः प्रकाशन्ते महात्मनः ॥6.23॥

<!-- review note: The pausa visarga in भक्तिः यथा is as extracted; printed editions apply sandhi and read परा भक्तिर्यथा देवे. Left unaltered per policy. -->

**IAST:** yasya deve parā bhaktiḥ yathā deve tathā gurau । / tasyaite kathitā hyarthāḥ prakāśante mahātmanaḥ ॥6.23॥
**Say it:** YAS-ya DAY-vay pa-RAA BHAK-tih ya-THAA DAY-vay ta-THAA gu-ROW / tus-YAI-tay ka-thi-TAA hyar-THAAH pra-kaa-SHUN-tay ma-HAAT-ma-nah
**Meaning:** Whoever has supreme devotion to God, and to the teacher as to God — for that great soul these truths, exactly as they have been told, shine forth of themselves.
**Meaning (hi):** जिसकी देव में परा भक्ति है, और जैसी देव में वैसी ही गुरु में — उस महात्मा के लिए ये कहे हुए अर्थ स्वयं प्रकाशित हो उठते हैं।

## Word by word

- **yasya** — whoever has
- **deve** — toward God
- **parā bhaktiḥ** — supreme devotion
- **yathā deve tathā gurau** — as toward God, so toward the teacher
- **tasya** — for that one
- **ete kathitāḥ hi arthāḥ** — these truths that have been told
- **prakāśante** — shine forth
- **mahātmanaḥ** — for that great soul

## Meaning

The Upanishad's final verse, and a first in scripture: bhakti — devotion — appears here as a term for the first time in the principal Upanishads, arriving already fully formed and already parā, supreme. The closing claim is quietly radical: these truths do not merely need study; they prakāśante, shine forth — and they shine for the one who holds God, and the teacher who carries God's word, in reverence. Bhakti traditions receive the verse as a near-scriptural rule of guru-devotion; Advaita reads it as the qualification for the teaching to take root; all agree it ties understanding to reverence. Meaning, this last verse says, opens to love.

## Meaning (hi)

उपनिषद का अंतिम श्लोक, और शास्त्र में एक शुरुआत: प्रधान उपनिषदों में 'भक्ति' शब्द पहली बार यहीं आता है — और आते ही पूर्ण रूप में, 'परा' होकर। समापन का दावा चुपचाप क्रांतिकारी है: ये अर्थ केवल अध्ययन नहीं माँगते; ये 'प्रकाशन्ते' — स्वयं प्रकाशित होते हैं — और उसके लिए, जो देव को, और देव-वाणी को धारण करने वाले गुरु को, श्रद्धा से धारण करता है। भक्ति-परंपराएँ इस श्लोक को गुरु-भक्ति का लगभग शास्त्रीय विधान मानती हैं; अद्वैत इसे शिक्षा के फलने की योग्यता पढ़ता है; इस पर सब सहमत हैं कि यह समझ को श्रद्धा से बाँधता है। अर्थ, यह अंतिम श्लोक कहता है, प्रेम के आगे खुलता है।

## Reflection

Who taught you the truths you actually live by — and when did you last honor them the way this verse suggests?
