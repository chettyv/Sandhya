## Shloka

**Devanagari:** अमात्यैर्बलिभिर्दुष्टैर्दुर्बलस्य दुरात्मभिः । / कोषो बलञ्चापहृतं तत्रापि स्वपुरे ततः ॥1.7॥

**IAST:** amātyairbalibhirduṣṭairdurbalasya durātmabhiḥ । / koṣo balañcāpahṛtaṃ tatrāpi svapure tataḥ ॥1.7॥
**Say it:** a-MAAT-yair BA-li-bhir DOOSH-tair door-BA-las-ya doo-RAAT-ma-bhih / KO-sho BA-lun chaa-pa-hri-TUM ta-TRAA-pi sva-POO-ray TA-tah
**Meaning:** Then, there in his own city, his treasury and his army were seized by wicked, powerful, evil-minded ministers — now that he was weak.
**Meaning (hi):** फिर वहाँ, उन्हीं के अपने नगर में, दुष्ट, बलवान और दुरात्मा मंत्रियों ने — उन्हें दुर्बल पाकर — उनका कोष और सेना दोनों हथिया लिए।

## Word by word

- **amātyaiḥ** — by the ministers
- **balibhiḥ** — powerful
- **duṣṭaiḥ** — wicked
- **durbalasya** — of him who was now weak
- **durātmabhiḥ** — evil-minded
- **koṣaḥ** — treasury
- **balam ca** — and the army
- **apahṛtam** — was seized, carried off
- **tatra api sva-pure** — even there, in his own city
- **tataḥ** — then

## Meaning

The outer enemy took the empire; the inner circle takes the rest. Suratha's own ministers — men who served him while he was strong — strip him of treasury and army the moment he is weak. The verse's compressed music of du- sounds (duṣṭair durbalasya durātmabhiḥ) hammers the betrayal home. This is the storyteller's second lesson in loss: the structures we trust hold their shape only under pressure of our strength. When that fails, loyalty bought by power dissolves with it. The king now has nothing left that the world gave him — which is precisely where the story needs him.

## Meaning (hi)

बाहरी शत्रु ने साम्राज्य लिया; भीतरी घेरा बाक़ी सब ले लेता है। सुरथ के अपने मंत्री — जो उनके बलवान रहते सेवा करते थे — उनके दुर्बल पड़ते ही कोष और सेना हड़प लेते हैं। श्लोक में "दु" की ठोकरें (दुष्टै-दुर्बल-दुरात्म) इस विश्वासघात को ठोक-ठोककर बजाती हैं। हानि का यह दूसरा पाठ है: जिन ढाँचों पर हम भरोसा करते हैं, वे हमारी शक्ति के दबाव में ही अपना आकार रखते हैं। शक्ति चूकते ही, सत्ता से ख़रीदी गई निष्ठा उसी के साथ घुल जाती है। अब राजा के पास वह कुछ भी नहीं बचा जो संसार ने दिया था — और कथा को वे ठीक इसी जगह चाहिए।

## Reflection

How much of the loyalty around you depends on what you can currently provide?
