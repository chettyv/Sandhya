## Shloka

**Devanagari:** एतेषु यश्चरते भ्राजमानेषु यथाकालं / चाहुतयो ह्याददायन् । / तं नयन्त्येताः सूर्यस्य रश्मयो यत्र / देवानां पतिरेकोऽधिवासः ॥1.2.5॥

**Meaning:** Whoever performs his rites while these tongues are blazing, at the proper time — him these oblations, taking him up, lead along the rays of the sun to where the one lord of the gods has his dwelling.

## Word by word

- **eteṣu bhrājamāneṣu** — while these (tongues) are blazing
- **yaḥ carate** — whoever performs (his rites)
- **yathākālam** — at the proper time
- **ca āhutayaḥ** — and the oblations
- **hi ādadāyan** — indeed taking (him) up
- **tam nayanti** — they lead him
- **etāḥ sūryasya raśmayaḥ** — these rays of the sun
- **yatra** — to where
- **devānām patiḥ ekaḥ** — the one lord of the gods
- **adhivāsaḥ** — has his dwelling

## Meaning

The promise at ritual's height, and the text keeps it: offerings poured at the right moment into the living flame do not vanish — they become carriers, rays of the sun that bear the offerer up to the dwelling of "the one lord of the gods," which the commentators gloss as Indra's heaven, the bright world earned by works. Notice the physics of the image: what you give, transformed, is what transports you; generosity becomes the road. This is the ascending half of the chapter's argument, and it is told without irony — rites performed rightly reach exactly what they aim at. The verses that follow will ask the harder question: how long does an earned heaven last, and what happens when the earning runs out?

## Meaning (hi)

कर्मकांड के शिखर का वादा — और ग्रंथ उसे निभाता है: सही क्षण पर, जीती हुई लौ में डाली गई आहुतियाँ खोती नहीं — वे वाहक बन जाती हैं, सूर्य की किरणें, जो यजमान को "देवताओं के एक स्वामी" के धाम तक उठा ले जाती हैं; भाष्यकार इसे इन्द्र का स्वर्ग पढ़ते हैं — कर्मों से कमाया हुआ उजला लोक। छवि की भौतिकी देखिए: जो तुमने दिया, वही रूपांतरित होकर तुम्हें ले जाता है; उदारता ही रास्ता बन जाती है। यह अध्याय की चढ़ाई वाली आधी दलील है, और बिना व्यंग्य के कही गई है — ठीक से किए कर्म ठीक अपने लक्ष्य तक पहुँचते हैं। आगे के श्लोक कठिन प्रश्न उठाएँगे: कमाया हुआ स्वर्ग कितना टिकता है, और कमाई चुक जाए तब क्या?

## Reflection

If your efforts carried you exactly as far as they deserve and no further, where would you land?
