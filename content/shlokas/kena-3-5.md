## Shloka

**Devanagari:** तस्मिँस्त्वयि किं वीर्यमित्यपीदँ सर्वं / दहेयं यदिदं पृथिव्यामिति ॥3.5॥

<!-- review note: the candrabindu in तस्मिँस्त्वयि and अपीदँ is genuine Vedic orthography as transcribed on Wikisource, not an artifact. Devanagari left exactly as extracted. -->

**IAST:** tasmim̐stvayi kiṃ vīryamityapīdam̐ sarvaṃ / daheyaṃ yadidaṃ pṛthivyāmiti ॥3.5॥
**Say it:** tas-MIMS-tva-yi kim VEER-ya-mi-tya-PEE-dum SAR-vum / da-HAY-yum ya-di-DUM pri-thi-VYAA-mi-ti
**Meaning:** "What power is there in such a one as you?" "I could burn all this — whatever there is upon the earth."
**Meaning (hi):** "तुम-जैसे में क्या सामर्थ्य है?" "मैं यह सब जला सकता हूँ — पृथ्वी पर जो कुछ भी है।"

## Word by word

- **tasmin tvayi** — in you, such as you are
- **kim vīryam** — what power?
- **iti** — thus (it asked)
- **api** — even
- **idam sarvam** — all this
- **daheyam** — I could burn
- **yat idam pṛthivyām** — whatever is here on the earth
- **iti** — thus (he answered)

## Meaning

A story verse. The yakṣa's question carries a sting the translation should keep: "in such a one as you" — what power is in you? Agni does not hear the irony. He answers with his whole portfolio: I could burn everything on earth. It is true, and it is beside the point, because the power to burn was never his private possession — the first verse of the story has already told us whose victory this is. The nasal candrabindu vowels in the Devanagari are genuine Vedic spelling, not typographical errors. The boast only finds its meaning in the next verse, against a single blade of grass.

## Meaning (hi)

यह कथा-श्लोक है। यक्ष के प्रश्न में एक चुभन है जिसे अनुवाद में बचाए रखना चाहिए: "तुम-जैसे में" — तुममें क्या बल है? अग्नि व्यंग्य नहीं सुन पाते। वे अपनी पूरी पूँजी गिना देते हैं: पृथ्वी पर जो कुछ है, सब जला सकता हूँ। बात सच है — और फिर भी बेकार, क्योंकि जलाने की शक्ति कभी उनकी निजी संपत्ति थी ही नहीं; कथा का पहला श्लोक बता चुका है कि विजय किसकी है। देवनागरी में चंद्रबिंदु वैदिक शुद्ध पाठ है, छपाई की भूल नहीं। यह डींग अपना अर्थ अगले श्लोक में पाती है — एक अकेले तिनके के सामने।

## Reflection

Which of your powers do you describe in total terms — "I could handle anything" — and who lent it to you?
