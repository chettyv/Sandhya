## Shloka

**Devanagari:** भवानि त्वं दासे मयि वितर दृष्टिं सकरुणा / मिति स्तोतुं वाञ्छन् कथयति भवानि त्वमिति यः । / तदैव त्वं तस्मै दिशसि निजसायुज्यपदवीं / मुकुन्दब्रह्मेन्द्रस्फुटमकुटनीराजितपदाम् ॥22॥

<!-- review note: The pāda break "सकरुणा / मिति" splits sakaruṇām iti mid-word — source lineation, not a textual error; printed editions carry the join सकरुणामिति continuously. No other conversion artifacts spotted. -->

**IAST:** bhavāni tvaṃ dāse mayi vitara dṛṣṭiṃ sakaruṇā / miti stotuṃ vāñchan kathayati bhavāni tvamiti yaḥ । / tadaiva tvaṃ tasmai diśasi nijasāyujyapadavīṃ / mukundabrahmendrasphuṭamakuṭanīrājitapadām ॥22॥
**Say it:** bha-VAA-ni tvam DAA-say MA-yi VI-ta-ra DRISH-tim sa-ka-roo-NAAM / I-ti STO-tum VAAN-chhan ka-tha-YA-ti bha-VAA-ni tvam I-ti yah / ta-DAI-va tvam TAS-mai DI-sha-si ni-ja-saa-YOOJ-ya-pa-da-veem / moo-KOON-da-brah-MEN-dra-sphoo-ta-ma-koo-ta-nee-RAA-ji-ta-pa-daam
**Meaning:** A devotee means to pray, "Bhavani, turn your compassionate glance on me, your servant" — but the instant he has said "bhavāni tvam," which also means "may I become you," you grant him oneness with yourself: the state whose feet the bright crowns of Mukunda, Brahma and Indra light in worship.
**Meaning (hi):** भक्त कहना चाहता है — "भवानी, अपने इस दास पर करुणा-भरी दृष्टि डालो"; पर "भवानि त्वम्" कहते ही — जिसका एक अर्थ "मैं तुम हो जाऊँ" भी है — तुम उसे उसी क्षण अपना सायुज्य दे देती हो: वह पद, जिसके चरणों की आरती मुकुन्द, ब्रह्मा और इन्द्र के दमकते मुकुट करते हैं।
**Source:** Soundarya Lahari 22 (traditionally attributed to Adi Shankaracharya), sanskritdocuments.org ITRANS file, personal-study terms, https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_devii/soundaryalahari.itx

## Word by word

- **bhavāni** — O Bhavani; also, from the verb bhū, "may I become"
- **tvam** — you
- **dāse mayi** — on me, your servant
- **vitara** — bestow, cast
- **dṛṣṭim sakaruṇām** — a glance full of compassion
- **iti stotum vāñchan** — wishing to pray thus
- **kathayati** — says, utters
- **bhavāni tvam iti yaḥ** — whoever (has said) "bhavāni tvam"
- **tadā eva** — that very instant
- **tasmai diśasi** — you grant to him
- **nija-sāyujya-padavīm** — the state of union with your own self
- **mukunda-brahma-indra** — Mukunda (Vishnu), Brahma and Indra
- **sphuṭa-makuṭa** — (whose) shining crowns
- **nīrājita-padām** — (the state) whose feet they light in lamp-worship

## Meaning

The verse turns on one pun. A devotee opens a humble petition — "Bhavani, look kindly on your servant" — but in Sanskrit the first two words, bhavāni tvam, can also be a whole prayer in themselves: "may I become you." The Mother hears that larger meaning before the sentence is finished and answers it, granting sāyujya, union with herself — a state so high that Vishnu, Brahma and Indra wave the lamps of their own shining crowns before its feet. Her grace does not measure out exactly what was asked; it answers the deepest thing the words could mean. The devotee comes asking for a glance and is given her very being.

## Meaning (hi)

पूरा श्लोक एक श्लेष पर टिका है। भक्त विनती शुरू करता है — "भवानी, अपने दास पर कृपा-दृष्टि करो" — पर संस्कृत में पहले दो शब्द "भवानि त्वम्" अपने-आप में एक पूरी प्रार्थना भी हैं: "मैं तुम हो जाऊँ।" माँ वाक्य पूरा होने से पहले ही वह गहरा अर्थ सुन लेती हैं और उसी का उत्तर देती हैं — सायुज्य, अपने साथ एकता; वह पद जिसके चरणों के आगे विष्णु, ब्रह्मा और इन्द्र अपने चमकते मुकुटों के दीप घुमाते हैं। उनकी कृपा माँगे हुए को तौलती नहीं; शब्दों में छिपी सबसे गहरी पुकार का उत्तर देती है। भक्त दृष्टि माँगने आया था, और उसे उनका होना ही मिल गया।

## Reflection

When you ask the divine for something small, are you ready for the far larger answer hiding inside your own words?
