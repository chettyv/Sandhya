## Shloka

## Word by word

- **sūkṣma-atisūkṣmam** — subtler than the subtle
- **kalilasya madhye** — in the midst of the tangle, the dense chaos
- **viśvasya sraṣṭāram** — the creator of the universe
- **aneka-rūpam** — of many forms
- **viśvasya ekam** — the one, of all the universe
- **pariveṣṭitāram** — the encircler
- **jñātvā** — having known
- **śivam** — the kind one; Shiva
- **śāntim atyantam** — peace beyond end
- **eti** — one attains

## Meaning

Two impossible addresses in one verse: subtler than the subtlest thing, yet found in the middle of the kalila — the thick, chaotic tangle of the world; wearing countless forms, yet the single ring drawn around everything. The word that resolves the paradox is śiva, which in this Upanishad hovers deliberately between an adjective, "the kind one," and the name Shaiva tradition hears: Shiva himself. That hover is honest. Shaiva Vedanta reads the personal Lord; Advaita reads the benign face of the one Brahman; Vaishnava commentators read the adjective as describing Narayana. The promise does not hover at all: know him, and peace is not partial or periodic — atyantam, without end.

## Meaning (hi)

एक श्लोक में दो असंभव पते: सूक्ष्म से भी सूक्ष्म, फिर भी संसार की घनी उलझन — 'कलिल' — के ठीक बीच में; अनगिनत रूपों वाला, फिर भी सबके चारों ओर खिंचा एक ही घेरा। इस विरोधाभास को जो शब्द सुलझाता है वह है 'शिव' — जो इस उपनिषद में जान-बूझकर विशेषण ("कल्याणमय") और शैव परंपरा के सुने नाम — स्वयं शिव — के बीच झूलता है। यह झूलना ईमानदार है। शैव वेदांत यहाँ साक्षात् ईश्वर पढ़ता है; अद्वैत एक ब्रह्म का सौम्य मुख; वैष्णव भाष्यकार नारायण का विशेषण। पर वादा बिलकुल नहीं झूलता: उसे जानो, और शांति आंशिक या सामयिक नहीं — 'अत्यन्तम्', अनंत।

## Reflection

Can you look for the kind one today exactly where life is most tangled, instead of waiting for the tangle to clear?
