## Shloka

**Devanagari:** समाने वृक्षे पुरुषो निमग्नोऽ / नीशया शोचति मुह्यमानः । / जुष्टं यदा पश्यत्यन्यमीशमस्य / महिमानमिति वीतशोकः ॥3.1.2॥

**Meaning:** On that same tree a person sits sunk down, grieving, deluded by his own helplessness. But when he sees the other — the beloved Lord — and sees "this glory is his," he becomes free of sorrow.

## Word by word

- **samāne vṛkṣe** — on the same tree
- **puruṣaḥ** — the person, the embodied self
- **nimagnaḥ** — sunk, immersed, drowned
- **anīśayā** — by helplessness, by lack of mastery
- **śocati** — grieves
- **muhyamānaḥ** — being deluded, confused
- **juṣṭam** — the beloved, the one served and delighted in
- **yadā paśyati** — when he sees
- **anyam īśam** — the other, the Lord
- **asya mahimānam** — his glory
- **iti** — thus (seeing "it is his")
- **vītaśokaḥ** — freed from sorrow

## Meaning

This verse resolves the image of the two birds. The eating bird is now a person drowning — not in pleasure but in helplessness, grieving because everything he tastes runs out and nothing obeys him. The turn comes not from tasting better fruit but from looking up and seeing the other bird: the Lord, the one who was always beside him. Advaita hears "the other" as one's own true Self, whose glory the grieving self mistakenly took to be a stranger's; the theistic schools hear a real, distinct Lord whose glory the soul finally recognizes and shares. On either reading, sorrow ends the moment attention shifts from what I lack to what is already here.

## Meaning (hi)

यह श्लोक दो पक्षियों की उपमा को आगे ले जाता है। खाने वाला पक्षी अब एक डूबता हुआ व्यक्ति है — सुख में नहीं, बेबसी में डूबा, शोक करता हुआ, क्योंकि जो भी चखता है वह चुक जाता है और कुछ भी उसके वश में नहीं। मोड़ तब आता है जब वह ऊपर देखता है और दूसरे पक्षी को — प्रभु को — पहचानता है, जो हमेशा से पास ही था। अद्वैत में "दूसरा" अपना ही सच्चा आत्मा है, जिसकी महिमा को शोकग्रस्त जीव पराया समझ बैठा था; भक्ति-परंपराओं में वह वास्तविक, भिन्न ईश्वर है जिसकी महिमा को जीव अंततः पहचानता है। दोनों पाठों में शोक उसी क्षण मिटता है जब ध्यान "मेरे पास क्या नहीं है" से हटकर "जो पहले से यहाँ है" पर टिकता है।

## Reflection

When you feel stuck and helpless today, what happens if you pause the tasting and simply look for the companion who watches?
