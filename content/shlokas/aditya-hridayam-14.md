## Shloka

**Devanagari:** आतपी मण्डली मृत्युः पिङ्गलः सर्वतापनः । / कविर्विश्वो महातेजाः रक्तः सर्वभवोद्भवः ॥14॥

<!-- review note: "महातेजाः रक्तः" keeps the pausa visarga before r-; printed editions applying sandhi read महातेजा रक्तः (mahātejā raktaḥ). Left unaltered per policy. -->

**IAST:** ātapī maṇḍalī mṛtyuḥ piṅgalaḥ sarvatāpanaḥ । / kavirviśvo mahātejāḥ raktaḥ sarvabhavodbhavaḥ ॥14॥
**Say it:** aa-ta-PEE man-da-LEE MRIT-yuh PIN-ga-lah sar-va-TAA-pa-nah / ka-vir-VISH-vo ma-haa-TAY-jaah RAK-tah sar-va-bha-VOD-bha-vah
**Meaning:** He blazes with heat, bears the orb, and is Death itself; tawny-gold, the scorcher of all; the seer-poet, the all, mighty in splendour, the crimson one, the origin of all becoming.
**Meaning (hi):** वे तप्त तेज वाले हैं, मंडलधारी, स्वयं मृत्यु; पिंगल वर्ण, सबको तपाने वाले; क्रांतदर्शी कवि, स्वयं विश्व, महान तेजस्वी, रक्तिम, और समस्त सृष्टि के उद्गम।

## Word by word

- **ātapī** — blazing, full of heat
- **maṇḍalī** — bearer of the orb
- **mṛtyuḥ** — death
- **piṅgalaḥ** — tawny, golden-brown
- **sarva-tāpanaḥ** — scorcher of all
- **kaviḥ** — the seer, the poet
- **viśvaḥ** — the all
- **mahā-tejāḥ** — of great splendour
- **raktaḥ** — red, crimson (the colours of dawn and dusk)
- **sarva-bhava-udbhavaḥ** — the origin of all becoming

## Meaning

The hymn refuses to look away: among the bright names stands mṛtyuḥ — death, plainly. The sun that ripens also withers; sarva-tāpana, scorcher of all, is listed in the same breath as kavi, the seer-poet, and sarva-bhava-udbhava, the source of all becoming. Praise that only flattered would be smaller than its subject. The tawny and crimson names (piṅgala, rakta) are the sun's own dawn and dusk colours, the daily reminder that the source of beginnings presides over endings too. In the story, a man about to risk death is being taught to bow to a power large enough to include it.

## Meaning (hi)

स्तोत्र नज़र नहीं चुराता: उजले नामों के बीच सीधा-सा खड़ा है — मृत्युः। जो सूर्य पकाता है, वही सुखाता भी है; सर्वतापन उसी साँस में गिना जाता है जिसमें कवि — क्रांतदर्शी — और सर्वभवोद्भव, समस्त होने का उद्गम। जो स्तुति केवल चापलूसी करे, वह अपने विषय से छोटी पड़ जाती। पिंगल और रक्त — ये सूर्य के अपने ही भोर और साँझ के रंग हैं, रोज़ की याद कि आरंभों का स्रोत अंतों का भी अधिपति है। कथा में, मृत्यु का जोखिम लेने जा रहे व्यक्ति को उस शक्ति के आगे झुकना सिखाया जा रहा है जो इतनी बड़ी है कि मृत्यु भी उसमें समा जाए।

## Reflection

What would change if you honoured endings as belonging to the same light that begins things?
