## Shloka

**Devanagari:** धनुः पौष्पं मौर्वी मधुकरमयी पञ्च विशिखाः / वसन्तः सामन्तो मलयमरुदायोधनरथः । / तथाप्येकः सर्वं हिमगिरिसुते कामपि कृपाम् / अपाङ्गात्ते लब्ध्वा जगदिदमनङ्गो विजयते ॥6॥

**Meaning:** A bow made of flowers, a bowstring of honeybees, exactly five arrows, the spring season for an ally, the sandal-breeze from Malaya for a war-chariot — and yet, O daughter of the snow mountain, with such gear the bodiless Kama single-handedly conquers this whole world, having won one indescribable drop of grace from your side-glance.

## Word by word

- **dhanuḥ pauṣpam** — a bow made of flowers
- **maurvī** — bowstring
- **madhukara-mayī** — made of honeybees
- **pañca viśikhāḥ** — five arrows
- **vasantaḥ** — spring
- **sāmantaḥ** — the vassal-ally, second-in-command
- **malaya-marut** — the breeze from the Malaya hills
- **āyodhana-rathaḥ** — the war-chariot
- **tathā api** — even so
- **ekaḥ** — alone, single-handed
- **sarvam** — all (of it)
- **himagiri-sute** — O daughter of the snow mountain
- **kām api kṛpām** — some indescribable grace
- **apāṅgāt te** — from your side-glance
- **labdhvā** — having obtained
- **jagat idam** — this world
- **anaṅgaḥ** — the bodiless one (Kama)
- **vijayate** — conquers, is victorious

## Meaning

The poet takes an inventory of Kama's army and finds it absurd. His bow would wilt by evening; his bowstring would fly off to the nearest flower; he has five arrows total; his one ally is a season; his chariot is a breeze. He does not even have a body — Shiva burned it. Yet this is the general who defeats the entire world, gods and sages included. The verse's answer to the puzzle is its whole teaching: one flick of grace from the corner of the Devi's eye outweighs any armory. Equipment, the poem suggests, was never the deciding variable — backing is. For daily practice the verse cuts two ways: it explains why desire is so hard to fight, and why the under-equipped need not despair.

## Meaning (hi)

कवि कामदेव की सेना की गिनती करता है और उसे हास्यास्पद पाता है। धनुष शाम तक मुरझा जाएगा; प्रत्यंचा भौंरों की है, जो पहले फूल पर उड़ जाएगी; बाण कुल पाँच; इकलौता सहयोगी एक ऋतु; रथ के नाम पर एक हवा। शरीर भी नहीं है — शिव ने उसे भस्म कर दिया था। फिर भी यही सेनापति देवताओं और मुनियों समेत सारे संसार को जीतता है। इस पहेली का उत्तर ही श्लोक की पूरी शिक्षा है: देवी की आँख के कोने से मिली एक कृपा-भर किसी भी शस्त्रागार से भारी है। कविता का इशारा है कि निर्णायक चीज़ साधन कभी थे ही नहीं — पीठ पर किसका हाथ है, यह है। दैनिक जीवन में यह दो तरफ़ काटता है: यह बताता है कि वासना से लड़ना कठिन क्यों है, और यह भी कि कम साधन वाले को निराश क्यों नहीं होना चाहिए।

## Reflection

Where are you waiting for better equipment when what you actually need is to ask for backing?
