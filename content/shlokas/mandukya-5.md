## Shloka

**Devanagari:** यत्र सुप्तो न कञ्चन कामं कामयते न कञ्चन स्वप्नं पश्यति तत् सुषुप्तम् । / सुषुप्तस्थान एकीभूतः प्रज्ञानघन एवानन्दमयो ह्यानन्दभुक् चेतोमुखः प्राज्ञस्तृतीयः पादः ॥5॥

**IAST:** yatra supto na kañcana kāmaṃ kāmayate na kañcana svapnaṃ paśyati tat suṣuptam । / suṣuptasthāna ekībhūtaḥ prajñānaghana evānandamayo hyānandabhuk cetomukhaḥ prājñastṛtīyaḥ pādaḥ ॥5॥
**Say it:** YA-tra SOOP-to na KUN-cha-na KAA-mum KAA-ma-ya-tay na KUN-cha-na SVAP-num PASH-ya-ti tat soo-SHOOP-tum / soo-SHOOP-ta-STHAA-na ay-KEE-bhoo-tah prag-YAA-na-gha-na ay-VAA-nun-da-ma-yo HYAA-nun-da-bhook CHAY-to-moo-khah PRAAG-yas tri-TEE-yah PAA-dah
**Meaning:** Where the sleeper desires no desire and sees no dream — that is deep sleep. The third quarter is Prajna: his field is deep sleep; in him all is gathered into one; he is a solid mass of knowing, made of bliss, an enjoyer of bliss, with consciousness for his doorway.
**Meaning (hi):** जहाँ सोया हुआ न कोई कामना करता है, न कोई स्वप्न देखता है — वह सुषुप्ति है। तीसरा पाद प्राज्ञ है: उसका क्षेत्र गहरी नींद; उसमें सब कुछ एक हो जाता है; वह प्रज्ञान का ठोस पुंज है, आनन्दमय, आनन्द का भोक्ता, और चेतना ही उसका द्वार है।

## Word by word

- **yatra** — where
- **suptaḥ** — the sleeper
- **na kañcana kāmam kāmayate** — desires no desire at all
- **na kañcana svapnam paśyati** — sees no dream at all
- **tat suṣuptam** — that is deep sleep
- **suṣupta-sthānaḥ** — whose place is deep sleep
- **ekībhūtaḥ** — gathered into one
- **prajñāna-ghanaḥ** — a solid mass of knowing
- **eva** — verily
- **ānandamayaḥ** — made of bliss
- **hi** — indeed
- **ānanda-bhuk** — enjoyer of bliss
- **cetomukhaḥ** — with consciousness as his doorway
- **prājñaḥ** — Prajna, the knowing one
- **tṛtīyaḥ pādaḥ** — the third quarter

## Meaning

Deep sleep is defined by two absences — no desire, no dream — and yet the Upanishad refuses to call it nothing. Something remains: a self gathered into one, a dense mass of knowing with no objects left to know, and the state's signature is bliss — which is why waking from deep sleep feels like returning from rest that was genuinely enjoyed, though nothing happened in it. Prajna is called "the doorway of consciousness" because dream and waking both open out of this state, morning after morning. It is the quietest piece of evidence in the whole argument: even when mind and world switch off, you do not.

## Meaning (hi)

गहरी नींद दो अनुपस्थितियों से परिभाषित है — न कामना, न स्वप्न — फिर भी उपनिषद उसे शून्य कहने से इंकार करता है। कुछ बचा रहता है: एक में सिमटा आत्मा, बिना विषयों का ठोस प्रज्ञान-पुंज, और इस अवस्था की पहचान है आनन्द — इसीलिए गहरी नींद से जागना ऐसे आराम से लौटना लगता है जो सचमुच भोगा गया, हालाँकि वहाँ कुछ घटा नहीं। प्राज्ञ को 'चेतोमुख' — चेतना का द्वार — कहा गया है, क्योंकि हर सुबह स्वप्न और जागरण इसी अवस्था से खुलते हैं। यह पूरे तर्क का सबसे शान्त प्रमाण है: मन और संसार बुझ जाते हैं, पर आप नहीं बुझते।

## Reflection

What was present last night in your deepest sleep, when nothing else was?
