## Shloka

**Devanagari:** तस्य पालयतः सम्यक् प्रजाः पुत्रानिवौरसान् । / बभूवुः शत्रवो भूपाः कोलाविध्वंसिनस्तथा ॥1.4॥

<!-- review note: No transcription artifacts spotted. कोलाविध्वंसिनः is traditionally glossed as "the destroyers of the Kolas" or as the proper name of an enemy clan; commentators differ, and printed editions carry the same reading. -->

**IAST:** tasya pālayataḥ samyak prajāḥ putrānivaurasān । / babhūvuḥ śatravo bhūpāḥ kolāvidhvaṃsinastathā ॥1.4॥
**Say it:** TAS-ya paa-LA-ya-tah SUM-yak pra-JAAH poo-TRAAN i-VAO-ra-saan / ba-BHOO-vooh SHA-tra-vo BHOO-paah ko-laa-vidh-VUM-si-nas ta-THAA
**Meaning:** While he guarded his subjects well, as though they were his own sons, kings known as the Kola-destroyers became his enemies.

## Word by word

- **tasya pālayataḥ** — while he was protecting
- **samyak** — properly, well
- **prajāḥ** — his subjects
- **putrān iva aurasān** — as if they were sons of his own body
- **babhūvuḥ** — became, arose
- **śatravaḥ** — enemies
- **bhūpāḥ** — kings
- **kolāvidhvaṃsinaḥ** — the Kola-destroyers (an enemy clan of kings)
- **tathā** — then, likewise

## Meaning

The verse pairs two facts without softening the collision: Suratha ruled rightly, loving his people like his own children — and enemies rose against him anyway. Nothing in the text suggests he provoked the war or failed in dharma. This is a deliberate move by the storyteller: the crisis that will eventually bring the king to the Goddess is not a punishment for wrongdoing. Righteous conduct does not exempt anyone from loss; the Devi Mahatmya wants that understood before a single battle of its own is fought. Good kings, too, get invaded.

## Meaning (hi)

श्लोक दो तथ्यों को बिना किसी नरमी के आमने-सामने रख देता है: सुरथ धर्मपूर्वक शासन करते थे, प्रजा को अपनी सन्तान जैसा प्रेम देते थे — और फिर भी शत्रु उन पर चढ़ आए। पाठ में कहीं संकेत नहीं कि उन्होंने युद्ध को न्योता दिया या धर्म से चूके। कथाकार की यह चाल सोची-समझी है: जो संकट राजा को अन्ततः देवी तक ले जाएगा, वह किसी अपराध का दण्ड नहीं है। धर्माचरण हानि से छूट नहीं दिलाता — देवी माहात्म्य अपनी एक भी लड़ाई लड़ने से पहले यह बात समझा देना चाहता है। अच्छे राजाओं पर भी आक्रमण होते हैं।

## Reflection

When something went wrong despite your doing everything right, what did you conclude about yourself?
