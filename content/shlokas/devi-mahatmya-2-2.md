## Shloka

**Devanagari:** तत्रासुरैर्महावीर्यैर्देवसैन्यं पराजितम् । / जित्वा च सकलान् देवानिन्द्रोऽभून्महिषासुरः ॥2.2॥

**IAST:** tatrāsurairmahāvīryairdevasainyaṃ parājitam । / jitvā ca sakalān devānindro'bhūnmahiṣāsuraḥ ॥2.2॥

**Meaning:** There the army of the gods was broken by asuras of great might; and having conquered all the gods, Mahishasura made himself Indra.

## Word by word

- **tatra** — there
- **asuraiḥ mahā-vīryaiḥ** — by asuras of great might
- **deva-sainyam** — the army of the gods
- **parājitam** — was defeated
- **jitvā ca** — and having conquered
- **sakalān devān** — all the gods
- **indraḥ abhūt** — became Indra
- **mahiṣāsuraḥ** — Mahishasura

## Meaning

The verse names the disaster in one clean sentence: the gods lost, and the winner did not merely loot heaven — he sat down in its highest office. "Indro 'bhūt" — he _became_ Indra. The Devi Mahatmya's crises are usurpations of rightful order, not just raids: power seizes a seat that belongs to dharma, and everything downstream — rain, sun, sacrifice — now runs through an occupier. The story's whole engine is the gap between holding a position and deserving it, and that gap is what the Devi will come to close.

## Meaning (hi)

श्लोक विपत्ति को एक साफ़ वाक्य में कह देता है: देवता हारे, और जीतने वाले ने स्वर्ग को केवल लूटा नहीं — वह उसके सर्वोच्च आसन पर जा बैठा। "इन्द्रोऽभूत्" — वह इन्द्र _बन गया_। देवीमाहात्म्य के संकट केवल आक्रमण नहीं, धर्म-व्यवस्था का अपहरण हैं: बल उस आसन को हथिया लेता है जो धर्म का है, और फिर वर्षा, सूर्य, यज्ञ — सब कुछ एक क़ब्ज़ाधारी के हाथ से गुज़रता है। पद पर बैठने और उसके योग्य होने के बीच की खाई ही इस कथा का इंजन है — और वही खाई देवी पाटने आएँगी।

## Reflection

Where in your world is raw force currently sitting in a seat that rightly belongs to something better?
