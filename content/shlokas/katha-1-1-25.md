## Shloka

**Devanagari:** ये ये कामा दुर्लभा मर्त्यलोके / सर्वान् कामाँश्छन्दतः प्रार्थयस्व । / इमा रामाः सरथाः सतूर्या / न हीदृशा लम्भनीया मनुष्यैः । / आभिर्मत्प्रत्ताभिः परिचारयस्व / नचिकेतो मरणं माऽनुप्राक्षीः ॥1.1.25॥

**Meaning:** Whatever desires are hard to win in the mortal world — ask for them all at your pleasure. These lovely women with their chariots and their music — the like of them no mortal can win. I give them; let them attend you. But Nachiketa — do not ask me about dying.

## Word by word

- **ye ye kāmāḥ** — whatever desires
- **durlabhāḥ** — are hard to win
- **martya-loke** — in the world of mortals
- **sarvān kāmān** — all those desires
- **chandataḥ** — at your pleasure
- **prārthayasva** — ask for
- **imāḥ rāmāḥ** — these lovely women
- **sa-rathāḥ sa-tūryāḥ** — with chariots, with music
- **na hi īdṛśāḥ** — for such as these
- **lambhanīyāḥ manuṣyaiḥ** — cannot be won by mortals
- **ābhiḥ mat-prattābhiḥ** — by these, given by me
- **paricārayasva** — let yourself be attended
- **naciketo** — O Nachiketa
- **maraṇam** — about dying
- **mā anuprākṣīḥ** — do not ask

## Meaning

The last and most extravagant offer — every rare desire, celestial companions, chariots, music, all beyond what mortals can win — ends with the sentence that gives the whole game away: "do not ask me about dying." Everything on the table has been priced against a single question, which tells Nachiketa, and the reader, exactly what that question is worth. The story is not saying pleasure is evil; it is showing pleasure being used — as payment for silence. Whenever abundance arrives with one small condition attached, the condition is the real transaction.

## Meaning (hi)

आख़िरी और सबसे शानदार पेशकश — हर दुर्लभ कामना, दिव्य संगिनियाँ, रथ, संगीत, सब कुछ जो मनुष्यों की पहुँच से बाहर है — उस वाक्य पर ख़त्म होती है जो पूरा खेल खोल देता है: "मृत्यु के बारे में मत पूछो।" मेज़ पर रखी हर चीज़ की क़ीमत एक प्रश्न के बदले लगाई गई है, और इसी से नचिकेता को — और पाठक को — पता चल जाता है कि उस प्रश्न का मोल क्या है। कथा यह नहीं कह रही कि सुख बुरा है; वह दिखा रही है कि सुख का इस्तेमाल हो रहा है — चुप्पी की क़ीमत की तरह। जब भी प्रचुरता किसी एक छोटी शर्त के साथ आए, असली सौदा वही शर्त होती है।

## Reflection

What is being offered to you right now chiefly to keep you from asking something?
