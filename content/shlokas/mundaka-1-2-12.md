## Shloka

**Devanagari:** परीक्ष्य लोकान् कर्मचितान् ब्राह्मणो / निर्वेदमायान्नास्त्यकृतः कृतेन । / तद्विज्ञानार्थं स गुरुमेवाभिगच्छेत् / समित्पाणिः श्रोत्रियं ब्रह्मनिष्ठम् ॥1.2.12॥

**Say it:** pa-REEK-shya lo-KAAN kar-ma-chi-TAAN braah-ma-NO / nir-VAY-dum aa-YAAN NAAS-tya-kri-TAH kri-TAY-na / tad-vig-yaa-NAAR-thum sa gu-rum-ay-VAA-bhi-gach-chhayt / sa-mit-PAA-nih SHRO-tri-yum brah-ma-nish-THUM
**Meaning:** Having examined the worlds that works build up, let a seeker of Brahman grow dispassionate: the uncreated is not reached by the created. To know That, let him go, fuel in hand, to a teacher alone — one learned in the scriptures and established in Brahman.

## Word by word

- **parīkṣya** — having examined, tested
- **lokān** — the worlds
- **karmacitān** — built up by works
- **brāhmaṇaḥ** — a seeker of Brahman
- **nirvedam āyāt** — let him come to dispassion
- **na asti** — there is not (reaching)
- **akṛtaḥ** — the uncreated
- **kṛtena** — by the created, by what is done
- **tat-vijñānārtham** — in order to know That
- **saḥ** — he
- **gurum eva** — to a teacher, and to a teacher alone
- **abhigacchet** — let him go
- **samitpāṇiḥ** — fuel in hand
- **śrotriyam** — learned in the received scripture
- **brahmaniṣṭham** — established in Brahman

## Meaning

The hinge of the first Mundaka, and among the most quoted verses on finding a teacher. It moves in three steps. First an audit: examine — not despise — the worlds effort can build. Then the axiom that audit yields: nothing made reaches the unmade; no quantity of doing crosses over into being. Then the instruction, with its double qualification: the guru must be shrotriya, trained in the received word, and brahmanishtha, actually established in it — learning without realization fails one test, charisma without learning fails the other. Vedanta lineages all cite this verse for the necessity of a guide; their emphases differ between the unbroken word and the living attainment. And the seeker arrives samit-pani, fuel in hand: as one come to tend a fire, not as a customer.

## Meaning (hi)

प्रथम मुण्डक की धुरी, और गुरु खोजने पर सबसे अधिक उद्धृत श्लोकों में से एक। चाल तीन क़दमों की है। पहले जाँच: कर्म जो लोक बना सकता है, उन्हें परखो — उनसे घृणा नहीं। फिर वह सूत्र जो जाँच से निकलता है: बना हुआ अनबने तक नहीं पहुँचता; करने की कोई भी मात्रा होने में नहीं बदल जाती। फिर निर्देश, दोहरी शर्त के साथ: गुरु 'श्रोत्रिय' हो — परंपरा से उतरे शब्द में प्रशिक्षित — और 'ब्रह्मनिष्ठ' भी — उसमें सचमुच प्रतिष्ठित। अनुभव-रहित पांडित्य एक कसौटी पर गिरता है, शास्त्र-रहित करिश्मा दूसरी पर। वेदांत की सभी धाराएँ मार्गदर्शक की अनिवार्यता के लिए यही श्लोक उद्धृत करती हैं; ज़ोर कहीं अखंड शब्द-परंपरा पर है, कहीं जीवित उपलब्धि पर। और जिज्ञासु पहुँचता है 'समित्पाणिः' — हाथ में समिधा लिए: आग सँभालने आया सेवक बनकर, ग्राहक बनकर नहीं।
