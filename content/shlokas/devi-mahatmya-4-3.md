## Shloka

**Devanagari:** देवा ऊचुः / देव्या यया ततमिदं जगदात्मशक्त्या / निः शेषदेवगणशक्तिसमूहमूर्त्या । / तामम्बिकामखिलदेवमर्षिपूज्यां / भक्त्या नताः स्म विदधातु शुभानि सा नः ॥4.3॥

**Say it:** day-VAA OO-chooh / DAYV-yaa ya-YAA TA-tam i-DUM ja-ga-DAAT-ma-SHAKT-yaa / nih-SHAY-sha-DAY-va-ga-na-SHAK-ti-sa-MOO-ha-MOORT-yaa / TAAM am-bi-KAAM a-khi-la-DAY-va-ma-HAR-shi-POOJ-yaam / BHAK-tyaa na-TAAH sma vi-da-DHAA-too shoo-BHAA-ni saa nah
**Meaning:** The gods said: The Devi by whom, through her own power, this whole world is pervaded; whose body is the gathered power of all the hosts of gods with none left out — to that Ambika, worthy of worship by every god and every great seer, we bow in devotion. May she ordain good things for us.

## Word by word

- **devāḥ ūcuḥ** — the gods said
- **devyā yayā** — the Devi by whom
- **tatam idam jagat** — this world is pervaded, spread out
- **ātma-śaktyā** — by her own power
- **niḥśeṣa-deva-gaṇa-śakti-samūha-mūrtyā** — whose body is the gathered power of all the hosts of gods, none left out
- **tām ambikām** — to her, Ambika, the Mother
- **akhila-deva-maharṣi-pūjyām** — worthy of worship by all gods and great seers
- **bhaktyā natāḥ sma** — we bow in devotion
- **vidadhātu** — may she ordain, bring about
- **śubhāni** — good things, auspicious things
- **sā naḥ** — she, for us

## Meaning

The hymn says what the Devi is before it asks for anything. One claim is the story so far: in chapter 2 she took shape from the gods' combined radiance, and her body is their collected power. The other is larger: this world is spread out by her _own_ śakti. She is not merely the sum of the gods; she is what they are made of. The only request — "may she ordain good for us" — comes after the bow. The framings part here: Shakta theology takes ātma-śakti literally, the Devi's own power; Advaita hears the one Self's power to appear as a world; Vaishnava readers see the Lord's inseparable energy at work.

## Meaning (hi)

स्तुति देवी से कुछ माँगने से पहले यह कहती है कि वे हैं क्या। एक दावा अब तक की कथा है: दूसरे अध्याय में वे देवताओं के सम्मिलित तेज से प्रकट हुई थीं, और उनका विग्रह देवताओं की इकट्ठी शक्ति है। दूसरा दावा बड़ा है: यह जगत उनकी _अपनी_ शक्ति से फैला है। वे देवताओं का योग भर नहीं; वे वह हैं जिससे देवता बने हैं। एकमात्र माँग — "वे हमारा कल्याण करें" — प्रणाम के बाद आती है। यहाँ दृष्टियाँ अलग होती हैं: शाक्त दर्शन आत्मशक्ति को शब्दशः लेता है, देवी की अपनी शक्ति; अद्वैती इसमें एक आत्मा की जगत रूप में प्रकट होने की सामर्थ्य सुनते हैं; वैष्णव पाठ भगवान की अभिन्न शक्ति देखता है।
